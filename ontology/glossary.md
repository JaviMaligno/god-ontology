# Working Glossary

Definitions are local to this project unless explicitly attributed. They are meant to remove ambiguity before heavier formalization.

| Term | Working definition | Status/source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | A representation of what occurs or how a domain is structured, broad enough to include laws, mechanisms, dependencies, unification, and prediction. | accepted motivation; [U01](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u01), [U02](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u02) |
| **Scientific explanation** | A description that answers a scientific why-question through laws, mechanisms, causes, models, or unification. It need not answer why the fundamental explanatory structure obtains. | accepted distinction |
| **Metaphysical explanation** | An account of ontological dependence, ground, necessity, possibility, or ultimate stopping points that is not supplied merely by deriving consequences within a scientific model. | working definition |
| **Necessary** | True or existent in every metaphysically possible world/model admitted by the chosen semantics. This is stronger than causal inevitability under actual laws. | formalization pending |
| **Contingent** | Actual but not metaphysically necessary; it could have failed to obtain in at least one admissible world/model. | working definition |
| **Metaphysical possibility** | Genuine compatibility with the constitutive conditions of reality, not merely conceivability, logical syntax, or physical compatibility. Its ground remains open. | open; [U08](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u08), [U23](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u23) |
| **Ground** | A non-temporal relation of ontological dependence or explanation. Grounding must not be silently treated as efficient causation. | working definition |
| **Agent causation** | Origination of an action by an agent irreducible, in the intended libertarian version, to a sufficient chain of prior events or to randomness alone. | preferred; [U09](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u09) |
| **Generative structure $S$** | A fundamental specification—possibly laws, constants, boundaries, topology, and primitives—that constrains or generates histories without necessarily settling libertarian acts. | preferred; [U15](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u15) |
| **Possible continuation** | A causally/metaphysically admissible extension of a history prefix at a branching point. It need not be a separately existing universe. | provisional |
| **Actual history $H^*$** | The history that obtains; in preferred E+, it is a tenselessly complete history. The frame-indexed F model remains diagnostic. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Actualization** | The relation by which $G$ makes a structure or history actual. It must be distinguished from choosing every event inside it. | preferred distinction; [U12](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u12), [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21) |
| **Asymmetric co-production** | The actual history depends on divine actualization of its ontological framework and may also include genuine creaturely causal contributions; those contributions are not equal in scope or ontological priority. | preferred; [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21) |
| **ACO** | Actuality-complete omniscience: complete knowledge of every truth concerning complete actual reality, without temporal acquisition or updating. | accepted; [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21), [U24](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u24) |
| **Modal knowledge** | Knowledge of what is possible, necessary, or compossible. It accompanies knowledge of the actual in the preferred account, while its exact domain and ontological ground remain open. | preferred extension; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Pre-actual counterfactual knowledge** | Purported knowledge, prior in explanatory order to actuality, of what a libertarian agent would freely choose under non-actual conditions. It is stronger than knowledge that several outcomes are possible. | excluded from the current model; C13 |
| **Intervention** | A divine causal contribution distinguished by its target: structure, law, boundary, state, circumstances, or agency. | provisional taxonomy |
| **Miracle** | An event whose causally sufficient conditions are not exhausted by ordinary secondary causal structure. | preferred working definition |
| **Providence** | A global architectural relation among divine actualization/intervention and actual history, not a separate causal force. | accepted working definition |
| **Epistemic priority** | Dependence of knowledge on truth or its truthmaker. It is not a temporal learning sequence. | working distinction |
| **Ontological priority** | Dependence of one reality on another without implying earlier/later temporal order. | working distinction |
| **Temporal indexing** | Locating an event or proposition at a creaturely time $t$. | accepted distinction; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Perspectival indexing** | Relativizing what has occurred, is settled, or is epistemically available to a creature or temporal frame. | accepted distinction; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Actuality indexing** | Relating divine knowledge to the truths of the complete actual history $H^*$, without treating knowledge as temporally acquired or causally productive of those truths. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Agent-causal eternalism (E+)** | The working combination of a tenselessly complete actual history, libertarian originating acts within it, and actuality-indexed ACO. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Epistemic openness** | Lack of knowledge by a temporal agent about which future act will occur. It is neither necessary nor sufficient for metaphysical freedom. | working distinction; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Causal openness** | Absence of causally sufficient settlement of an act by the relevant prior event-history and laws. | preferred component of freedom; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Modal openness** | Availability of more than one metaphysically admissible continuation sharing the relevant creaturely causal past. | provisional; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Alethic openness** | Absence, at an earlier temporal frame, of a determinate truth value for a future contingent. | diagnostic F commitment, not required by E+ |
| **Pointed modal structure** | A structure $\langle\mathcal H(S),R,\alpha\rangle$ containing possible histories, their accessibility relation, and one history $\alpha$ marked as actual. | preferred formal device; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Actual-marker semantics** | Across alternative pointed evaluations, the modal structure and complete epistemic capacity remain fixed while the history occupying the actual index differs. Actual-indexed knowledge follows logically, without divine temporal change. | preferred response to fixity; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Exhaustive epistemic access** | The capacity expressed by ACO to know every truth that obtains. The phrase replaces observational imagery when that imagery would suggest sensory reception, temporal discovery, or causation of the known fact. | preferred clarification; [S03](../sessions/2026-08-31-actuality-and-coproduction.md) |
