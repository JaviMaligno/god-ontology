# Working Glossary

Definitions are local to this project unless explicitly attributed. They are meant to remove ambiguity before heavier formalization.

| Term | Working definition | Status/source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | A representation of what occurs or how a domain is structured, broad enough to include laws, mechanisms, dependencies, unification, and prediction. | accepted motivation; [U01](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u01), [U02](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u02) |
| **Scientific explanation** | A description that answers a scientific why-question through laws, mechanisms, causes, models, or unification. It need not answer why the fundamental explanatory structure obtains. | accepted distinction |
| **Metaphysical explanation** | An account of ontological dependence, ground, necessity, possibility, or ultimate stopping points that is not supplied merely by deriving consequences within a scientific model. | working definition |
| **Necessary** | True or existent in every metaphysically possible world/model admitted by the chosen semantics. This is stronger than causal inevitability under actual laws. | formalization pending |
| **Contingent** | Actual but not metaphysically necessary; it could have failed to obtain in at least one admissible world/model. | working definition |
| **Metaphysical possibility** | Genuine compatibility with the constitutive conditions of reality, not merely conceivability, logical syntax, or physical compatibility. Its ground remains open. | open; [U08](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u08), [U23](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u23) |
| **Ground** | A non-temporal relation of ontological dependence or explanation. Grounding must not be silently treated as efficient causation. | working definition |
| **Agent causation** | Origination of an action by an agent irreducible, in the intended libertarian version, to a sufficient chain of prior events or to randomness alone. | preferred; [U09](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u09) |
| **Minimal intellect** | The capacity to apprehend live possibilities and relevant considerations sufficiently to act for reasons; weaker than ACO or unrestricted omniscience. | preferred; C67 |
| **Relative transcendence** | $G$'s ontological priority to created spacetime and non-reducibility to a constituent wholly located within it; weaker than full immateriality or absolute non-spatiality. | preferred; C68 |
| **Existential sovereignty** | Dependence of every created entity, arena, and agentive power on $G$ for existence, without attributing the originating content of every event or free act to $G$. | preferred conditional thesis; C73 |
| **Non-continuous global dependence** | Atemporal existential dependence of the created order on $G$ without repeated maintenance actions at each created time. | preferred; C72 |
| **Qualified omnipotence $OMP_Q$** | Power to perform every action metaphysically coherent under independent necessary constraints and independently established essential features of $G$. | accepted definition; satisfaction provisional under C75 |
| **Agentic settling** | The exercise by which a conscious agent makes one live option actual without the token act being sufficiently settled by the action-relevant antecedent conditions. | preferred; C55–C57 |
| **Action-relevant antecedent $C_A^-$** | The agent's relevant nature, powers, reasons, circumstances, and causal background, excluding the token settling and its consequences. | provisional formal device; S06 |
| **Non-necessitating reasons** | Reasons that support, rank, attract, inhibit, or make an act intelligible without entailing the agent's token selection. | accepted role; C56 |
| **Reason-guided sourcehood** | Authorship in which the agent originates an act for reasons; neither the reasons alone nor a stochastic process is the complete source of the token act. | preferred; C56–C57 |
| **Conscious sourcehood** | The whole subject's efficacious exercise of originating power with some relevant options or considerations practically available to it. | preferred; C61 |
| **Practical availability $\Phi_A(t)$** | The action-guiding contents consciously available to a temporal subject at $t$; it need not include every influence or a complete introspective model. | provisional; C61–C62 |
| **Motivating reasons $M_A(a,t)$** | Considerations the agent takes as reasons in performing $a$, whether or not they are objectively correct or normatively sufficient. | preferred distinction; C61, C65 |
| **Reasons-responsiveness** | The capacity to apprehend, assess, and act for considerations across situations without those considerations constituting a deterministic choice function. | preferred; C63 |
| **Rational quality** | The degree to which an act fits the agent's evidence, motivating reasons, values, and standards; distinct from whether the act is minimally free. | provisional evaluative dimension; C65 |
| **Generative structure $S$** | A fundamental specification—possibly laws, constants, boundaries, topology, and primitives—that constrains or generates histories without necessarily settling libertarian acts. | preferred; [U15](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u15) |
| **Possible continuation** | A causally/metaphysically admissible extension of a history prefix representing compossible manifestations of powers and constraints. It is not a separately existing concrete universe. | preferred representation; C49 |
| **Actual history $H^*$** | The history that obtains; in preferred E+, it is a tenselessly complete history. The frame-indexed F model remains diagnostic. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Complete actual reality $\mathfrak R^*$** | The full domain of obtaining reality, including $G$, $S$, $H^*$, actual interventions, agents, and actual relations of grounding or dependence. | provisional ACO domain; [S05](../sessions/2026-08-31-aco-domain.md) |
| **Actualization** | The relation by which $G$ makes a structure or history actual. It must be distinguished from choosing every event inside it. | preferred distinction; [U12](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u12), [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21) |
| **Asymmetric co-production** | The actual history depends on divine actualization of its ontological framework and on heterogeneous contributions within it, including some agent-originated acts. Divine and creaturely roles differ in type, scope, and ontological priority. | preferred; [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21), [S03](../sessions/2026-08-31-actuality-and-coproduction.md) |
| **ACO** | Actuality-complete omniscience: atemporal knowledge of every truth grounded in complete actual reality $\mathfrak R^*$. It is the project's minimal, deliberately scoped omniscience thesis. | accepted minimum; [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21), [U24](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u24), [S05](../sessions/2026-08-31-aco-domain.md) |
| **Modal knowledge (MKO)** | Knowledge of necessary constraints, structures within $G$'s power, admissible continuations, agent-originable acts, and their compossibility relations. It accompanies ACO in one atemporal epistemic state. | preferred complement; [S05](../sessions/2026-08-31-aco-domain.md) |
| **Power-grounded modality** | The account on which possibilities are grounded by actualizing or originating powers together with structural constraints. The powers retain irreducible modal content. | preferred; C48 |
| **Conditional possibility** | A claim that act or outcome $a$ could occur under conditions $C$. It does not state which outcome a libertarian agent would select. | accepted distinction; C50 |
| **Compossibility** | The relation among possibilities that can be jointly realized within one structure or history. | provisional component of MKO |
| **Logical necessity $N_L$** | Constraints of logic and identity treated as constitutive of any coherent model. | preferred modal layer; C52 |
| **Mathematical structural necessity $N_M$** | Consequences that follow necessarily from a specified mathematical structure, without yet settling the ultimate ontology of that structure. | preferred modal layer; C52 |
| **Divine-essential necessity $N_G$** | Modal consequences of essential features of $G$ that have been independently established rather than imported from a completed God-concept. | provisional modal layer; C52 |
| **Possible agent-type** | A qualitative organization of agentive powers and relations that $G$ could actualize, without commitment to the individual identity of a non-actual person. | preferred initial domain; C53 |
| **Pre-actual counterfactual knowledge** | Purported knowledge, prior in explanatory order to actuality, of what a libertarian agent would freely choose under non-actual conditions. It is stronger than knowledge that several outcomes are possible. | excluded from the current model; C13 |
| **Intervention** | A divine-originating causal contribution that changes a temporal continuation and can thereby change which complete history is actual. It remains a constituent of the resulting history rather than a meta-temporal edit of an already actual block. | provisional taxonomy; [S04](../sessions/2026-08-31-intervention-ontology.md) |
| **Intervention zero $I_0$** | Divine actualization of existence and generative structure. It is a limiting case because it constitutes the causal arena rather than acting inside a prior arena. | retained from U20 |
| **Circumstantial intervention $I_C$** | A contribution altering an agent's information, incentives, opportunities, or environment without by definition settling the act. | provisional; S04 |
| **Power intervention $I_P$** | A contribution altering an agent's capacities or dispositions. It can enlarge, restrict, restore, or remove agency depending on its effect. | provisional; S04 |
| **Act intervention $I_A$** | A contribution targeting the originating act itself. If it sufficiently causes or settles the act, the creature lacks autonomy for that act. | preferred consequence; C16 |
| **Feasible options $Feas_I(A,t)$** | Actions physically executable by $A$ at $t$ after intervention $I$, independently of whether $A$ remains their libertarian source. | provisional formal device; C39 |
| **Originable alternatives $Orig_I(A,t)$** | Actions that remain available for $A$ to originate freely after intervention $I$. | provisional formal device; C36 |
| **Option closure** | Removal of executable alternatives until $|Feas_I(A,t)|=1$. | preferred distinction; C39 |
| **Source override** | Sufficient determination of the performed act by intervention while other actions may remain physically executable, replacing the agent as originating source. | preferred distinction; C39 |
| **Token-act autonomy** | Freedom attributable to one particular act. It can be removed without eliminating the agent's general causal powers. | preferred distinction; C40 |
| **Deliberative situation $D_A(t)$** | The structured information, reasons, feasible opportunities, and agentive capacities relevant to an agent at a time. | provisional model; C41 |
| **Non-determining influence** | An intervention that changes deliberative inputs or capacities without sufficiently causing the act or replacing the agent as its originating source. | preferred distinction; C42 |
| **Context control** | Divine determination of some information, reasons, opportunities, or capacities surrounding deliberation. It does not entail outcome control while the agent retains originable alternatives and sourcehood. | preferred distinction; C42 |
| **Non-agent target** | A law, boundary, state, or event targeted directly by intervention without the target itself being an agent-level condition or act. | preferred distinction; C46 |
| **Agent relevance** | The downstream property of changing some agent's deliberative situation, powers, or acts, regardless of the intervention's direct target. | preferred distinction; C46 |
| **Agent-neutral intervention** | An intervention that produces no downstream change to any agent's deliberative situation, powers, or acts. | permitted edge case; C46 |
| **Miracle** | An event whose sufficient production includes a direct, non-derivative intervention beyond creation itself and is not exhausted by ordinary secondary causes. It need not violate a law or be detectable. | preferred working definition; C17 |
| **Direct divine contribution** | A non-derivative causal constituent in the production of an event, distinguished from merely remote divine causal ancestry. | provisional anti-trivialization condition; C17 |
| **Providence** | The global relation among divine structural actualization, any interventions, and the coproduced actual history. It is not a separate causal force and does not assign divine authorship to every event. | accepted working definition; C18 |
| **Ordinary explanatory exhaustion** | The condition in which an event's sufficient causal explanation is supplied by the standing created structure and secondary causes, without an additional difference-making intervention. | provisional; C17 |
| **Epistemic priority** | Dependence of knowledge on truth or its truthmaker. It is not a temporal learning sequence. | working distinction |
| **Ontological priority** | Dependence of one reality on another without implying earlier/later temporal order. | working distinction |
| **Temporal indexing** | Locating an event or proposition at a creaturely time $t$. | accepted distinction; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Perspectival indexing** | Relativizing what has occurred, is settled, or is epistemically available to a creature or temporal frame. | accepted distinction; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Actuality indexing** | Relating divine knowledge to truths grounded in complete actual reality $\mathfrak R^*$, including the created history $H^*$, without treating knowledge as temporally acquired or causally productive. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md), [S05](../sessions/2026-08-31-aco-domain.md) |
| **Agent-causal eternalism (E+)** | The working combination of a tenselessly complete actual history, libertarian originating acts within it, and actuality-indexed ACO. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Epistemic openness** | Lack of knowledge by a temporal agent about which future act will occur. It is neither necessary nor sufficient for metaphysical freedom. | working distinction; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Causal openness** | Absence of causally sufficient settlement of an act by the relevant prior event-history and laws. | preferred component of freedom; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Modal openness** | Availability of more than one metaphysically admissible continuation sharing the relevant creaturely causal past. | provisional; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Alethic openness** | Absence, at an earlier temporal frame, of a determinate truth value for a future contingent. | diagnostic F commitment, not required by E+ |
| **Pointed modal structure** | A structure $\langle\mathcal H(S),R,\alpha\rangle$ containing possible histories, their accessibility relation, and one history $\alpha$ marked as actual. | preferred formal device; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Actual-marker semantics** | Across alternative pointed evaluations, the modal structure and complete epistemic capacity remain fixed while the history occupying the actual index differs. Actual-indexed knowledge follows logically, without divine temporal change. | preferred response to fixity; [S02](../sessions/2026-08-31-internal-time-and-atemporal-knowledge.md) |
| **Exhaustive epistemic access** | The capacity expressed by ACO to know every truth that obtains. The phrase replaces observational imagery when that imagery would suggest sensory reception, temporal discovery, or causation of the known fact. | preferred clarification; [S03](../sessions/2026-08-31-actuality-and-coproduction.md) |
| **Typed dependence relation** | A dependence relation with a specific role, such as existential grounding, constraint, event causation, agent origination, or intervention. Transitivity is not assumed across different types. | accepted distinction; C32 |
| **Contrastive authorship** | The explanatory role of an agent in why the actual history contains its act $a$ rather than an available alternative $b$. It does not compete with divine grounding of the agent's existence or arena. | preferred; C33 |
