# Working Glossary

Definitions are local to this project unless explicitly attributed. This file is the canonical register for working definitions; the treatise repeats a concise definition at first argumentative use, while a later formal appendix will collect symbols and satisfaction conditions. Definitions retain explicit statuses so that a provisional clarification is not silently promoted into a fixed thesis.

| Term | Working definition | Status/source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | A representation of what occurs or how a domain is structured, broad enough to include laws, mechanisms, dependencies, unification, and prediction. | accepted motivation; [U01](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u01), [U02](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u02) |
| **Scientific explanation** | A description that answers a scientific why-question through laws, mechanisms, causes, models, or unification. It need not answer why the fundamental explanatory structure obtains. | accepted distinction |
| **Metaphysical explanation** | An account of ontological dependence, ground, necessity, possibility, or ultimate stopping points that is not supplied merely by deriving consequences within a scientific model. | working definition |
| **Necessary** | True or existent in every metaphysically possible world/model admitted by the chosen semantics. This is stronger than causal inevitability under actual laws. | formalization pending |
| **Contingent** | Actual but not metaphysically necessary; it could have failed to obtain in at least one admissible world/model. | working definition |
| **Metaphysical possibility** | Genuine compatibility with the constitutive conditions of reality, not merely conceivability, logical syntax, or physical compatibility. S38 prefers a non-reductive constitutive-admissibility account ($Adm_C$) while leaving the ultimate meta-modal ground open; S57's five-clause $Poss_C^{ind}$ is reconciled with it in S60 as the same criterion with two explicit refinements. | preferred at F2; C343–C351, C528, [S38](../sessions/2026-09-02-created-possibility-and-intentional-content.md), [S57](../sessions/2026-09-02-modal-basis-independent-possibility.md) |
| **Ground** | A non-temporal relation of ontological dependence or explanation. Grounding must not be silently treated as efficient causation. | working definition |
| **Agent causation** | Origination of an action by an agent irreducible, in the intended libertarian version, to a sufficient chain of prior events or to randomness alone. | preferred; [U09](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u09) |
| **Minimal intellect** | The capacity to apprehend live possibilities and relevant considerations sufficiently to act for reasons; weaker than ACO or unrestricted omniscience. | preferred; C67 |
| **Relative transcendence** | $G$'s ontological priority to created spacetime and non-reducibility to a constituent wholly located within it; weaker than full immateriality or absolute non-spatiality. | preferred; C68 |
| **Circumscriptive or fundamental location** | A relation in which an entity's basic mode of existence is bounded, extended, or individuated by a spatial order; stronger than derivative presence to a region. | working distinction; C84 |
| **Created-space aspatiality** | $G$ is not circumscribed by or fundamentally constituted through a region of created spacetime. | preferred; C84 |
| **Physical immateriality** | $G$ is not a body, field, or physical system constituted under the spacetime and physical structure it actualizes. The cross-category grounding of material substances remains an explanatory burden. | preferred relative to the created order; C85, C88 |
| **Absolute fundamental non-spatiality $ASP_G$** | No spatial order circumscribes, extends, or spatially individuates $G$ in its basic mode of existence. | preferred abductively; C86 |
| **Material-continuity alternative** | A family of models in which $G$ is created matter, is uncreated matter of the same type as created matter, shares a prior necessary substrate with creation, or has creation as a modification of divine substance. | live but disfavored family; C89 |
| **Divine constitution question** | The decomposed challenge of whether $G$ has constituents, a substrate, internal structure, or a distinctive mode of substance, and how any answer bears on unity, ultimity, non-spatiality, and the production of matter. “Made of” may apply literally, analogically, or fail, but its status requires argument. | F2 response; C210–C211, C222–C253, CH12, S28–S31 |
| **Grounding reach** | Immediate existential dependence of $x$, or of the arena and powers enabling $x$, on $G$; it does not assign the contrastive content of a creaturely free act to $G$. | accepted omnipresence dimension; C90 |
| **Causal availability** | $G$'s capacity to act directly with respect to $x$ in every metaphysically coherent way without a necessary created intermediary; capacity does not entail exercise. Its universal scope inherits the provisional maximality claim and cannot independently prove it. | accepted conditional omnipresence dimension; C90, C260, S32 |
| **Ontological presence $Present_O$** | The conjunction of grounding reach, complete immediate epistemic access, and direct causal availability with respect to a created relatum. | accepted definition; C90 |
| **Actual ontological omnipresence $OP_A$** | Ontological presence to every actual entity, event, relation, and region admitted by the ontology of the actual created order. | preferred conditional thesis; C91–C92 |
| **Modal omnipresence capacity $OP_M$** | In every created order $G$ actualizes, $G$ is ontologically present to every item actual in that order; this is not present relation to non-actual possibilia. | preferred; C93 |
| **Personal availability $Avail_P$** | Direct subject-sensitive cognitive and causal availability by which $G$ can recognize, receive an address from, address, or respond to an actual conscious subject in a mode coherent with that subject's capacities and without a necessary created intermediary. | preferred conditional relation; C121–C122, S20 |
| **Universal personal availability $UPA$** | Personal availability of $G$ to every actual conscious subject. It attributes capacity and access, not universal exercised attention, response, love, or communion. | preferred; C122–C123, S20 |
| **Exercised personal presence** | An actual divine regard, address, response, or other intentional relation to a subject. It is a contingent exercise within providence rather than another dimension of minimal omnipresence. | provisional; C123, S20 |
| **Shared attention** | A reciprocal personal relation involving attention and mutual awareness between subjects. In the divine–creature case it is asymmetrically co-produced and cannot be inferred from divine awareness alone. | preferred distinction; C124, S20 |
| **Personal communion** | Stable significant personal presence involving mutual relation, value, and potentially love or union. Its divine form is deferred to goodness and fuller personhood. | deferred; C121, C123, S20 |
| **Existential sovereignty** | Dependence of every created entity, arena, and agentive power on $G$ for existence, without attributing the originating content of every event or free act to $G$. | preferred conditional thesis; C73 |
| **Non-continuous global dependence** | Atemporal existential dependence of the created order on $G$ without repeated maintenance actions at each created time. | preferred; C72 |
| **Qualified omnipotence $OMP_Q$** | Adequate strength over every well-formed action attributable to $G$ that is coherent under independent necessary constraints and independently established essential features. Ontology-relative $OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}$ is preferred at F2; unrestricted satisfaction remains defeasible by an independently grounded new action kind or non-created concrete limiter. | accepted definition; ontology-relative closure C585, S72; unrestricted form provisional |
| **Ground uniqueness $U_G$** | Exactly one being is the ultimate ground of the total contingent order. | preferred abductively; C80 |
| **Divine-agent uniqueness $U_D$** | Exactly one necessary agent is the ultimate ground of the total contingent order. It does not imply that every necessary truth, property, abstract object, or derivative necessary item is identical with that agent. | preferred conditional on C04–C05 and $U_G$; C81 |
| **Agentic settling** | The exercise by which a conscious agent makes one live option actual without the token act being sufficiently settled by the action-relevant antecedent conditions. | preferred; C55–C57 |
| **Action-relevant antecedent $C_A^-$** | The agent's relevant nature, powers, reasons, circumstances, and causal background, excluding the token settling and its consequences. | provisional formal device; S06 |
| **Non-necessitating reasons** | Reasons that support, rank, attract, inhibit, or make an act intelligible without entailing the agent's token selection. | accepted role; C56 |
| **Reason-guided sourcehood** | Authorship in which the agent originates an act for reasons; neither the reasons alone nor a stochastic process is the complete source of the token act. | preferred; C56–C57 |
| **Conscious sourcehood** | The whole subject's efficacious exercise of originating power with some relevant options or considerations practically available to it. | preferred; C61 |
| **Phenomenal consciousness $PCon$** | The presence of a unified first-person field or subjective character: there is something it is like for the subject to undergo the experience. It does not by itself entail freedom or causal indetermination. | accepted working concept; C134–C135, S22 |
| **Access consciousness $ACon$** | Availability of a content for reasoning, deliberation, report where applicable, or flexible guidance of action. It can influence action without originating it. | accepted working concept; C134, C136, S22 |
| **Self-consciousness $SCon$** | A subject's representation of itself, at least minimally, as bearer of states or author of actions. Explicit occurrent self-reflection is not required for every free act. | provisional; C134, S22 |
| **Agentive consciousness $AgCon$** | The role in which phenomenal and practically accessible contents participate non-epiphenomenally in the whole embodied subject's control or originating exercise before or during settlement of a token act. Consciousness need not be the episode's earliest causal contribution. $Originates$, not consciousness alone, carries the irreducible sourcehood claim. | preferred; C136–C138, C212–C216, S22, S29 |
| **Embodied agent** | A unified temporally persisting subject whose neural, physiological, affective, sensorimotor, and conscious processes may jointly realize or constitute action. It names the bearer and candidate source without treating mind and body as rival agents or settling whether the subject is reductive, non-reductive, dual-aspect, hylomorphic, or dualist. | preferred; C212, C219–C220, S29 |
| **Decision episode $\delta$** | The temporally extended process in which preparation, conscious access, control, agentive settlement, execution, and retrospective ownership may occur at different times. Not every stage or every episode contains all roles. | preferred; C213, S29 |
| **Preparation–settlement distinction** | Bodily or neural readiness, bias, or predictive information can change an action's probability without making its outcome causally unavoidable. Settlement is the stronger relation that fixes which live option becomes actual. | accepted; C213–C216, S29 |
| **Conscious causal control** | Causally relevant conscious apprehension, evaluation, endorsement, attention, veto, revision, or guidance within a decision episode before the outcome becomes irrevocably fixed. Retrospective awareness alone is insufficient. | preferred; C215–C216, S29 |
| **Conscious bearer** | The persisting subject $A$ whose token experience occurs and to whom it is first-personally presented. Bearer language does not by itself select organismic identity, non-reductive embodiment, hylomorphism, dual aspect, or substance dualism. | preferred F1 role; C310, C314–C315, S36 |
| **Conscious capacity $CapCon(A,t)$** | A power or organized disposition of $A$ to undergo conscious states. Its possession, first manifestation, and occurrent exercise must be distinguished. | preferred F1 distinction; C310, C316–C318, S36 |
| **Token conscious episode $c$** | An actual phenomenal state or process of a subject, temporally located for created embodied agents and distinct from the standing capacity and represented content. | preferred; C310, C312–C317, S36 |
| **Bearer–presentation–integration relation** | The relation through which episode $c$ is borne by $A$, presented first-personally to $A$, and connected to $A$'s continuing life or organization without requiring explicit report, memory, or self-concept. | preferred F1 model; C314, S36 |
| **Consciousness onset** | The first time at which a specified consciousness predicate is instantiated. Phenomenal, access, self, and agentive consciousness may have different onset conditions. | accepted typed question; C316, S36 |
| **Tenseless actuality versus atemporality** | A temporal episode may be an unchangingly actual part of complete history and be known atemporally without itself lacking temporal location. | accepted distinction; C312–C313, S36 |
| **Consciousness realization bridge** | The metaphysical relation connecting a bodily or other realization base, a subject, and a phenomenal episode. The F2 working bridge is embodied hylomorphic manifestation; its ultimate phenomenal ground remains open. | preferred locally/open in depth; C317–C318, C321–C341, S36–S37 |
| **Created atemporal subject** | A possible numerically distinct conscious subject that depends ontologically on $G$ without beginning at a created time. It is not the actual human model and remains an edge case for individuation and uniqueness. | open modal edge case; C319, S36 |
| **Question disposition** | The priority treatment of a newly raised question: banked without changing the queue, integrated into an active higher-priority module, or promoted after a dependency/centrality audit or explicit authorial ordering. Recency alone does not promote it. | accepted method; C324–C325, S37 |
| **Embodied power-bearing subject $EPBS$** | The preferred created-subject realization $\langle M_A,F_A,Cap_A,Life_A\rangle$: one organized living individual whose changing matter, unifying form, powers, and temporal life jointly ground embodiment and conscious manifestations. | preferred F2; C330–C334, S37 |
| **Thin hylomorphic form $F_A$** | The real unifying and power-conferring organization of an embodied subject, neither an extra spatial component nor a second personal substance. “Thin” excludes automatic importation of a complete Aristotelian or Thomistic system. | preferred F2; C331–C332, S37 |
| **Psychophysical power principle** | The still-open principle connecting an eligible organized subject with a phenomenal capacity. It cannot be replaced definitionally by report, intelligence, neural covariance, or future developmental potential. | open bridge; C333, C337–C338, S37 |
| **Conscious episode manifestation** | A temporal exercise of a conscious power by the whole bearer through a bodily, neural, affective, sensorimotor, environmental, and possibly further realization base. | preferred F2; C334, S37 |
| **Causal non-competition** | The compatibility proposal that constitution or realization and agent- or event-causation can explain one process under different typed relations without supplying two independent sufficient causes. It remains defeasible by evidence of antecedent lower-level sufficiency. | preferred compatibility principle; C335–C336, S37 |
| **Qualified phenomenal primitive** | The F2 stopping point that certain organized subjects possess fundamental phenomenal powers, with bearer, organization, manifestation, and causal roles specified even though phenomenality is not derived from nonphenomenal premises. | preferred provisional ground; C337, S37 |
| **Subject-level dual-aspect bridge** | The open proposal that a subject-level power has a physically describable structural or relational aspect and an intrinsic phenomenal aspect. It must still justify the macro-subject boundary and avoid combination problems. | open strong candidate; C338, S37 |
| **Practical availability $\Phi_A(t)$** | The action-guiding contents consciously available to a temporal subject at $t$; it need not include every influence or a complete introspective model. | provisional; C61–C62 |
| **Motivating reasons $M_A(a,t)$** | Considerations the agent takes as reasons in performing $a$, whether or not they are objectively correct or normatively sufficient. | preferred distinction; C61, C65 |
| **Reasons-responsiveness** | The capacity to apprehend, assess, and act for considerations across situations without those considerations constituting a deterministic choice function. | preferred; C63 |
| **Rational quality** | The degree to which an act fits the agent's evidence, motivating reasons, values, and standards; distinct from whether the act is minimally free. | provisional evaluative dimension; C65 |
| **Good-for $GF(x,A,C,\tau)$** | Value relative to how a subject's life goes for that subject under context $C$ and temporal scope $\tau$. The unindexed-by-dimension form is reserved for generic or all-things-considered use. It is distinct from the subject's current preference and from moral obligation. | open plural account; C127, C154, S21–S24 |
| **Welfare bearer $WBearer$** | A subject or other candidate entity whose life or condition can go better or worse for it. The present analysis begins with conscious subjects without declaring them the only possible bearers of every kind of good. | preferred initial scope; C143–C144, S23 |
| **Welfare profile $WProf(A,C)$** | The context-sensitive features through which states or relations may benefit or harm $A$, provisionally including phenomenal valence, functioning, agency, epistemic contact, relationship, and diachronic integrity or development. | provisional research profile; C146, S23 |
| **Temporal welfare scope $\tau$** | The moment, interval, project horizon, or whole life over which a welfare claim is evaluated. Local and diachronic claims can differ without contradiction. | accepted index; C149, C154, S24 |
| **Pro tanto good-for $PTGF_d(x,A,C,\tau)$** | Benefit to a subject in dimension $d$, context $C$, and temporal scope $\tau$ before conflicts with other benefits or harms are settled. | accepted distinction; C145, C154, S23–S24 |
| **Pro tanto bad-for $PTBF_d(x,A,C,\tau)$** | Harm to a subject in dimension $d$, context $C$, and temporal scope $\tau$ before conflicts with other dimensions are settled. | accepted distinction; C145, C154, S23–S24 |
| **All-things-considered good-for** | Overall prudential assessment across relevant dimensions, temporal distribution, uncertainty, and conflicts. No complete aggregation rule is presently adopted. | open; C145, C148–C149, S23 |
| **Multidimensional welfare dominance** | A partial comparison in which $x$ is no worse for a subject than $y$ in every admitted welfare dimension and better in at least one; it establishes some rankings without a total scalar. | accepted device; C148, S23 |
| **Alienation defeater** | Pressure against calling something good for a capable agent when the agent could not care about it from any sincere, reflective, non-manipulated standpoint responsive to accessible reasons. It is defeasible and does not reduce welfare to current desire or require complete or true information. | preferred constraint; C147, C200–C201, S23, S28 |
| **Creature-constructed normativity** | The family of views on which some or all evaluative concepts, reasons, norms, or truths are constituted by the attitudes, rational procedures, or social practices of created subjects. It is not one thesis. | open alternative; C155–C159, S24 |
| **No native divine evaluative standpoint $NoNativeNorm(G)$** | The candidate claim that $G$ has no primitive or constitutive standpoint of good prior in grounding order to created valuation. It is compatible with ACO knowledge of actual creaturely evaluative practices. | preferred formulation of an open alternative; C156, S24 |
| **Agency constitutivism** | The candidate that some normative standards follow from what is required to count as, sustain, or successfully exercise rational or autonomous agency. | open constructivist candidate; C159, S24 |
| **Hybrid welfare constructivism** | The candidate that some subject-affecting prudential facts are not constructed, while personal, moral, or institutional norms depend partly on autonomous or intersubjective construction. | open constructivist candidate; C159, S24 |
| **Sacrifice $Sacrifice(A,s,c,g,B,C,\tau)$** | An act $s$ in which $A$ deliberately accepts cost $c$ for an end, subject, relation, or project $g$, assessed for beneficiary set $B$ and horizon $\tau$. Cost, agentic or faithful worth, objective fittingness, causal success, personal welfare effect, and value for others remain separate. | accepted analysis; C160–C161, C175–C176, C202, S24–S25, S28 |
| **Defeasible diachronic priority $DP(g,p;A,B,C,\tau)$** | Priority of a stable commitment $g$ over a conflicting transient attraction $p$ when sincere orientation, non-culpable attention to accessible reasons, non-manipulation, temporal robustness, agency, proportionality, and effects on beneficiary set $B$ support it. $\tau$ may extend beyond $A$'s life; duration alone is insufficient. | preferred; C162, C175–C177, C200–C202, S24–S25, S28 |
| **Normative defeat $\triangleright$** | A relation in which one consideration justifiably takes priority over another without requiring a common numerical scale or erasing the defeated consideration's local valence. | provisional device; C163, S24 |
| **Norm-construction pressure $PressureConstruct(A,N,C)$** | A practical need or pressure for agent $A$ to formulate norm $N$ under context $C$, arising for finite subjects from such conditions as vulnerability, dependence, coordination, limited knowledge, or diachronic maintenance. It is distinct from construction capacity, exercise, and endorsement. | provisional; C165–C167, S25 |
| **Objective regulative good** | The preferred proposal that at least some evaluative truths set a correctness standard independent of actual endorsement and can be approached fallibly by temporal agents. It names a role, not a separate substance or completed metaphysical ground. | preferred direction; C168–C170, S25 |
| **Epistemic approximation $Closer_{Good}$** | A partial comparative relation between evaluative judgment systems, supported defeasibly by evidence, correction of error, resistance to distortion, coherence, affected-subject coverage, and adversarial survival. It neither supplies a numerical distance nor constitutes the good. | accepted device; C169, S25 |
| **Objective-anchor hybrid** | The preferred functional account on which phenomenal harms and some relational or agentic goods are objective anchors, while agents construct more specific personal, moral, and institutional norms under those constraints. Its boundary and ultimate ground remain open. | preferred; C170, S25 |
| **Conscious capacity profile $CProf(A,t)$** | A multidimensional profile of phenomenal, access, self, temporal, agentive-source, and normative capacities at a time. It replaces a single consciousness ladder and does not itself determine moral status. | accepted; C172–C174, S25 |
| **Beneficiary set $B$** | The subjects affected by or intended to benefit from an act or project. It may include the acting agent, contemporaries, later subjects, or several of these. | accepted index; C175–C177, S25 |
| **Transpersonal or posthumous value** | Value extending beyond the acting agent's own later welfare, including benefit to other or future subjects and fidelity to relations, institutions, knowledge, ecosystems, or projects that outlive the agent. | accepted distinction; C175–C177, S25 |
| **Minimal axiological interface** | The bounded evaluative module needed by the ontology to represent good, evil, sacrifice, defeasible long-term preference, transpersonal or species continuity, and their relation to $G$, without developing a comprehensive ethics. | F1 closed with local F2 repair; C178–C209, S26–S28 |
| **Transpersonal continuity** | Continuation beyond one individual of conscious lives, agency, relationships, knowledge, cultures, projects, forms of life, or conditions for future goods. Species survival is a central case but not an unconditional supreme value. | accepted adequacy role; C181–C182, S26 |
| **Defeasible long-term preference $LTP(x,y;B,C,\tau)$** | A positive adequacy condition under which a durable, generative, or transpersonal realization of $P$, $S$, or $R$ defeats an otherwise comparable transient benefit, unless grave present harm, weak causal connection, disproportionality, or a less costly alternative defeats that priority. It is stronger than future standing and weaker than a complete longtermist theory. | accepted correction; C186, C198–C199, S27–S28 |
| **Strong longtermism** | The substantially stronger family of views assigning effects on the far future dominant importance across a wide class of major decisions. It is neither presupposed nor excluded: particular stronger conclusions may follow from $LTP$, scale, probability, and the anchors, while a complete theory remains optional to the ontology. | open beyond the interface; C180, C186, C198–C199, R28, S27–S28 |
| **Minimum anchor domain $\mathcal A_{min}$** | Conscious valence $P$, agentive sourcehood $S$, and truthful recognition or relation $R$: the three existing ontological domains to which the minimal fit relation applies. | preferred at F1; C187, S27 |
| **Objective fit $Fit_*$** | The proposed necessary relation in which an eligible bearer has an anchor with a directed or due profile and an item is an objectively pertinent and proportionate response for realizing, protecting, restoring, or answering it in a stated context and horizon. Apt response can survive external failure and therefore differs from causal success. It supplies pro tanto fit before all-things-considered defeat; its irreducible normative burden lies in directedness or due realization and is provisionally grounded in $G$. | provisional/preferred; C188–C196, C203–C205, S27–S28 |
| **Objective misfit $Misfit_*$** | Frustration, corruption, destruction, or inappropriate response relative to the due profile of an anchor, bearer, context, and horizon. It supplies pro tanto badness and, when serious or grave, the minimal content of evil. | preferred; C189–C191, C203–C207, S27–S28 |
| **Epistemic orientation $EpStance$** | The agent's practical orientation toward an end through some profile of belief, trust, hope, faith, accessible evidence, and attention to counterevidence. Information can improve or defeat the orientation but complete, true, or predictive information is not a universal prerequisite for agentic or faithful worth. | preferred; C200–C202, S28 |
| **Due good $Due_a(B,C,\tau)$** | A realization of anchor $a$ called for by the relevant kind of bearer, capacity, or relation in context and horizon. It excludes mere absence of an optional enhancement from counting automatically as evil. | provisional/preferred; C203–C206, S28 |
| **Due-good privation $Priv_a(B,C,\tau)$** | Absence, frustration, or corruption of a due good. Evil qua evil is provisionally analyzed through this dependence on good, while pain, coercion, and deception can be positive occurrences that bear, express, or cause the privation. | provisional/preferred; C206–C207, S28 |
| **Hell-like absence** | A possible privation of communion, reciprocity, truthful recognition, or accepted personal relation with $G$, compatible with the subject's continued ontological grounding, knowability, and divine causal availability. It does not assert that hell exists or specify its duration or cause. | provisional interpretation; C208–C209, S28 |
| **Broad evil $Evil^{broad}$** | Objective badness across the anchor domain, including minor and severe as well as natural and moral cases. | preferred scope; C190–C191, S27 |
| **Grave evil $Evil^{grave}$** | Severe, non-trivial destruction, corruption, or violation of conscious life, agentive sourcehood, or truthful relation. | preferred scope; C190–C191, S27 |
| **Long-horizon standing** | Eligibility of actual later bearers, and of possible bearer types conditionally upon actualization, for the same anchor-relative value relations without discount merely for temporal distance. | preferred; C192, S27 |
| **Species continuity** | Preservation of conditions for future conscious lives, sourcehood, truthful relations, inherited projects, and further goods. Its value is derivative from those possibilities and constrained by their quality. | preferred; C193, S27 |
| **Empirical–ontological bridge $Bridge(O,E)$** | A premise or derivation connecting ontology $O$ to an empirical consequence represented by $E$. Scientific evidence constrains $O$ through such a bridge rather than by automatically defining metaphysical categories. | accepted methodological device; C183–C185, S26 |
| **Realization constraint** | Empirical evidence limiting how a metaphysical capacity, subject, or relation can be physically realized in the actual created order without necessarily defining the metaphysical kind itself. | accepted distinction; C183–C185, S26 |
| **Normative fit** | The relation in which an action, state, or object appropriately responds to or realizes genuine reasons, capacities, goods, or relations in context. Its ultimate ground remains open. | provisional evaluative bridge; C128–C131, S21 |
| **Unrestricted choice voluntarism** | The view that an item is good solely because $G$ chooses or endorses it as good. It is live but disfavored as a substantive account of divine goodness. | open/disfavored; C129, S21 |
| **Semantic voluntarism** | The claim that evaluative vocabulary such as “good” means or is defined through divine approval. It is a thesis about meaning. | distinguished, not adopted; C140 |
| **Axiological voluntarism** | The claim that divine will, choice, or approval metaphysically constitutes goodness or value. Its unrestricted version is the principal S21 target. | live/disfavored unrestricted form; C129–C140 |
| **Deontic voluntarism** | The claim that some or all obligations are constituted by divine commands. It can be accepted or rejected independently of unrestricted axiological voluntarism. | open; C140 |
| **Epistemic approval thesis** | The claim that divine approval reliably indicates or recognizes goodness without constituting it. | open; C140 |
| **Institutional voluntarism** | The claim that divine acts can create contingent roles, commitments, permissions, or obligations within an already evaluable relational domain. | provisional component of C130; C140 |
| **Relational divine-source account** | The candidate view that $G$ existentially grounds actual finite value bearers and may institute contingent norms, while their good-making features include objective fit among conscious life, capacities, agency, development, relationships, and context. | provisional candidate; C130, S21 |
| **Good agent** | A subject with stable dispositions to recognize, care about, and respond fittingly to genuine goods and reasons. This has not yet been established for $G$. | open target; C127, C133, S21 |
| **Omnibenevolence** | Perfect possession and exercise of the relevant good dispositions across their proper scope. Its meaning depends on prior accounts of good agency, applicable norms, and value. | deferred; C127, C133, S21 |
| **Generative structure $S$** | A fundamental specification—possibly laws, constants, boundaries, topology, and primitives—that constrains or generates histories without necessarily settling libertarian acts. | preferred; [U15](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u15) |
| **Possible continuation** | A causally/metaphysically admissible extension of a history prefix representing compossible manifestations of powers and constraints. It is not a separately existing concrete universe. | preferred representation; C49 |
| **Actual history $H^*$** | The history that obtains; in preferred E+, it is a tenselessly complete history. The frame-indexed F model remains diagnostic. | preferred; [S01](../sessions/2026-08-31-eternalism-and-indexing.md) |
| **Complete actual reality $\mathfrak R^*$** | The full domain of obtaining reality, including $G$, $S$, $H^*$, actual interventions, agents, and actual relations of grounding or dependence. | provisional ACO domain; [S05](../sessions/2026-08-31-aco-domain.md) |
| **Actualization** | The relation by which $G$ makes a structure or history actual. It must be distinguished from choosing every event inside it. | preferred distinction; [U12](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u12), [U21](../corpus/transcript.md#user--u21) |
| **Creation bridge** | The two-axis module connecting divine creative capacity to created reality: ontological production asks how created substance and structure depend on or arise from $G$; creative reason asks why $G$ contingently creates this kind of order. | production at F2, reason at F1; C95–C114, C222–C238, S16–S18, S30 |
