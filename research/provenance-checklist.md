## Opening attribution — John C. Lennox

The mathematician named by the author is John C. Lennox. The official publisher record confirms [*Can Science Explain Everything?* (2019)](https://www.thegoodbook.com/can-science-explain-everything), and a published review locates Lennox's description/explanation contrast at pp. 34–36. The repository therefore attributes the motivating formulation to Lennox but treats “science describes rather than explains” as the author's paraphrase, not as a verified verbatim quotation.

The author's second Lennoxian motivation is also verified as an identifiable argument, not as the exact wording of a quotation. In [“The Big Bang: Is There Room for God?”](https://johnlennox.org/article/big-bang-is-there-room-for-god/), Lennox uses the analogy of distrusting a computer produced by a mindless unguided process to challenge naturalistic confidence in the mind doing science. Plantinga develops the nearby evolutionary argument against naturalism in [*Where the Conflict Really Lies*, ch. 10](https://academic.oup.com/book/5857/chapter-abstract/149115442); his [preface](https://academic.oup.com/book/5857/chapter-abstract/149105183) also makes explicit that naturalism is stronger than atheism. [Fales (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1086/289920) supplies a direct peer-reviewed objection: selection can support general cognitive reliability, while theism alone does not fix its probability. **Verdict: V/Q.** The Lennox attribution and argument family are verified; the project's appeal to an analogue of divine agency transmitted asymmetrically to finite cognizers is the author's extension and remains motivation rather than a new commitment.

## Findings that change the record

1. Murphy is a direct close precedent for a non-requiring divine reason to promote creaturely welfare. S48 remains a project-specific derivation, but not an apparently unlabeled literature gap.
2. Shoemaker is the materialist or psychological-continuity interlocutor in the 1984 debate with Swinburne, not a simple-view author.
3. Aquinas, ST I.75-76 and I.89, supports subsistence and the incomplete or non-natural condition of the separated soul; bodily completion by resurrection requires Supplement q.75 (especially a.1), not those Prima Pars questions alone.
4. Quantum field theory and Noether's theorem support only the physical bridge in S51. They do not establish the metaphysical claim that matter has no transferable substrate.
5. Spinoza supports the contrast of created bodies as modes of one extended substance. Describing this as determinate properties being inherited by substance was too strong.
6. Williamson's necessitism and Wallace's Everettian account are comparison cases, not direct support for modal uniqueness or for the settlement-branching constraint.
7. Dummett permits a carefully constrained retrospective-prayer case; Geach rejects prayer for past events. They do not jointly derive C551. Grounding well-foundedness is a live family of positions, not an uncontested theorem supplied by Fine and Rosen.

## Classification disposition

The overall classification remains “established components; project-specific synthesis” for S46-S58 and S64, with three sharpenings:

- S47's agentic stopping point has a close established precedent in O'Connor.
- S48's non-requiring orientation toward creaturely welfare has a direct close precedent in Murphy; the project's distinctiveness lies in its derivation and typing.
- S53's non-criterial and intermittent persistence has a direct close precedent in Merricks; the project's distinctiveness lies in deriving and integrating it with sourcehood, eternalism, and re-realization.

No verified source warrants a standalone novelty claim. The residual limits are interpretive page-level questions in broad monographs, not missing or fabricated bibliographic items.
