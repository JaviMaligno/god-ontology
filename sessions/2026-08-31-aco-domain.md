# Session: ACO Domain and Modal Complement

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Session ID:** S05
**Primary question:** What does actuality-complete omniscience include, and what lies in its modal complement?
**Dependencies:** E+, complete actuality, truthmaking, modal structure
**Starting ledger items:** C10–C14, C23, C28, C47

## Reminder and role

ACO means **actuality-complete omniscience**, a project-specific label for the minimal thesis that $G$ knows every truth grounded in complete actual reality without temporal acquisition or updating.

It was introduced to avoid building disputed claims about possibility and counterfactuals into the word omniscience. ACO entails neither that knowledge causes its objects nor that nonexistent pre-actual counterfactuals of libertarian choice must be known.

## Domain refinement

$H^*$ is the complete created temporal history, but complete actuality also includes $G$, the generative structure, interventions, agents, and actual relations of dependence. Define:

\[
\mathfrak R^*=\langle G,S,H^*,\mathbf I^*,Rel^*\rangle.
\]

Then:

\[
ACO(G)\leftrightarrow
\forall p[True_{\mathfrak R^*}(p)\rightarrow K_G(p)].
\]

The domain includes divine self-knowledge, every physical and agentive fact in $H^*$, all actual interventions, and obtaining structural or grounding relations. A false proposition presents no gap because its true negation falls within the quantified truths.

## Four features

1. **Exhaustive:** no truth in $\mathfrak R^*$ is omitted.
2. **Factive:** $K_G(p)\rightarrow p$.
3. **Atemporal:** knowledge is not acquired or updated at a creaturely time.
4. **Non-productive:** knowing $p$ does not efficiently cause $p$; the obtaining truth grounds its inclusion in ACO.

## Modal complement

ACO is intentionally a minimum about actuality. The preferred fuller account adds knowledge of possibilities and modal structure:

\[
MKO(G)\leftrightarrow
\forall q[ModalTrue_{\mathfrak M}(q)\rightarrow K_G(q)].
\]

The current working account of divine knowledge is therefore:

\[
DK(G)=ACO(G)\land MKO(G).
\]

MKO includes truths such as which structures, continuations, and agent acts are genuinely possible. It excludes purported pre-actual truths stating which act a libertarian agent would choose, because C13 rejects that truth-domain.
