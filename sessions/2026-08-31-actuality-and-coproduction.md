# Session: Actuality and Asymmetric Co-production

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Session ID:** S03
**Primary question:** What grounds the actual history and divine knowledge of its free acts?
**Dependencies:** E+, ACO, structural actualization, agent causation
**Starting ledger items:** C07–C13, C22–C29

## Authorial correction

The project had begun repeating the foreknowledge and counterfactual discussion after the author had already rejected pre-actual counterfactual knowledge of libertarian choices. Formalization must preserve settled conclusions rather than continually reopen them. Molinism remains provenance for the rejected option, not an active comparison frame.

## Grounding proposal

God does not know a free choice as actual with explanatory priority over the existence of the universe containing that choice. Before the actual history obtains, there is no truth about which autonomous choice is actual. This is not a limitation of omniscience: no actual truth exists at that explanatory level for God to fail to know.

Once the choice is described as produced, the priority intended is metaphysical rather than a temporal sequence in God. The agent's act belongs to the tenselessly complete history, grounds the corresponding truth, and that truth falls within ACO.

The actual marker $\alpha$ merely designates $H^*$. It is not an extra cause, a second act of selection, or a property imposed by God after the history is complete.

The word observation is usable as a metaphor for exhaustive epistemic access. It must not imply sensory reception, waiting, or divine acquisition of information.

## Counterfactual closure

The model retains knowledge of modal structure and of genuinely possible alternatives. It excludes pre-actual truths about what a libertarian agent would choose and therefore excludes exhaustive knowledge of such truths. This point is closed unless a later inconsistency in an accepted commitment directly reopens it.

## Next advance

Decompose asymmetric co-production without treating its contributors as equals:

- $G$ grounds the existence of the arena, structure, and created powers;
- $S$ constrains admissible events without determining every free act;
- ordinary causes produce events within the structure;
- created agents originate some acts that become parts of $H^*$;
- divine interventions, if any, require separately typed causal targets.

The next formal task is to state these relations and test whether structural actualization accidentally collapses into complete-history selection.
