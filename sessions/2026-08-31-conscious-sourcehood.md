# Session: Conscious sourcehood and reasons-responsiveness

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Session ID:** S07
**Primary question:** What minimal role must consciousness and psychology play in agentic settling?
**Dependencies:** unified agentic choice, deliberative situation, power-grounded modality
**Starting ledger items:** C41–C44, C55–C60

## Starting position

The unified model requires more than deterministic processing and more than probabilistic openness. A conscious agent must be the originating source of the token act. Consciousness cannot remain an unanalyzed label, so this session specifies its minimal functional and ontological roles without yet selecting a complete philosophy of mind.

## Definitions

| Term/symbol | Definition used in this session |
| --- | --- |
| $D_A(t)$ | The temporal agent's information, reasons, opportunities, and capacities |

| $M_A(a,t)$ | Considerations $A$ takes as motivating reasons in acting |
| Conscious sourcehood | Efficacious settling by the whole conscious subject |
| Rational quality | Fit among the act, evidence, motivating reasons, values, and relevant standards |

## Minimal conscious-source condition

Retain:

\[
D_A(t)=\langle E_t,R_t,F_t,\Pi_t\rangle.
\]

For a reason-guided act:

\[
Subject(A)
\land a\in\mathcal O_A(C_A^-)
\land M_A(a,t)\cap\Phi_A(t)\neq\varnothing
\land Originates(A,a,t).
\]

The condition assigns four roles to consciousness:

1. subject unity for experiences, capacities, and action;
2. practical availability of some options or considerations;
3. appropriation of considerations as motivating reasons;
4. causal efficacy of the agent's settling.

It does not require exhaustive introspection. Unconscious processes, emotion, habit, character, and bodily conditions can influence action without every contribution appearing in $\Phi_A(t)$.

## Reasons without deterministic policy

A motivating reason explains the act through the agent:

\[
ForReasons(A,a,M_A)
\land Originates(A,a).
\]

It is not converted into an independently sufficient event-cause:

\[
Supports(M_A(a,t),a)
\not\models
Settles(A,a).
\]

Reasons-responsiveness is therefore a modal capacity to apprehend, assess, and act for considerations across situations. It does not assert a pre-actual fact about what the agent would select for every reason profile.

Taking a consideration as a reason need not be a separate earlier choice. It can be an aspect of the same act by which the whole agent settles $a$. This blocks a regress in which an inner will must first choose what the outer agent will choose.

## Freedom, rationality, and psychology

Minimal freedom and rational quality vary independently. A free act can be:

- well supported by evidence and stable values;
- based on a mistaken motivating reason;
- akratic, when the agent acts against its considered judgment;
- impulsive or weakly deliberated while still consciously sourced.

Automatic or habitual behavior requires token-level analysis. If the conscious agent genuinely settles the act, habit can influence without replacing sourcehood. If a mechanism runs to completion without practical availability or agentic settling, the token behavior is not free under this minimal condition. Earlier free choices may still matter to later responsibility, which is a separate question.

## Realization without causal duplication

A neural or physical process may realize an agentive capacity:

\[
Realizes(n,\Pi_A)\not\models SufficientCause(n,a).
\]

Realization need not introduce a second cause competing with the agent. If the event-level base independently and sufficiently fixes $a$, however, agentic settling becomes causally redundant and the token act fails the model.

This leaves open reductive, non-reductive, dual-aspect, and substance-based theories of mind. Any eventual choice must preserve the causal efficacy assigned to the whole subject.

## Application to $G$

$G$ does not require temporal deliberation, memory accumulation, emotion, habit, or bodily realization. Replace $D_A(t)$ with an atemporal field of comprehended possibilities and reasons. The shared agential core remains:

- a unified subject;
- apprehension of live possibilities and reasons;
- action for reasons without necessitation;
- efficacious contingent settling.

The created and cosmic cases therefore have different implementations of one choice structure.

## Adversarial tests

### Is consciousness merely decorative?

Not in this model. Awareness that only accompanies an outcome produced elsewhere supplies no sourcehood. Conscious settling must be causally relevant to the token act.

### Does conscious appropriation create a homunculus?

Only if consciousness or will is treated as a second agent. Here the whole subject acts, and taking a consideration as a reason is one aspect of its action.

### Does poor reasoning eliminate freedom?

Not automatically. It lowers rational quality and may affect responsibility without erasing alternatives and sourcehood. Compulsion, incapacitation, or sufficient prior settlement can remove freedom; error alone does not.
