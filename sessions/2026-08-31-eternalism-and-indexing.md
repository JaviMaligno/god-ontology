## Where the E/F distinction came from

The original conversation did not consistently maintain two explicit parallel models. It moved among three claims without fully separating them:

1. God knows a complete actual history atemporally.
2. Before Alice's choice, the choice is “not true yet.”
3. Divine knowledge is indexed to actuality and grounded by the actual choice rather than causally determining it.

The E/F distinction was introduced during repository analysis to expose the tension between claims 1 and 2. It is an analytic reconstruction with genuine precedents in the literature, not a dichotomy explicitly adopted in the original conversation.

## Three meanings of “indexed”

| Kind | Indexed to | Example | Metaphysical commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Temporal indexing | a time $t$ | Alice chooses at $t_1$, not at $t_0$ | Events occupy temporal locations. |
| Perspectival indexing | a creature/frame at $t$ | At $t_0$, the choice has not occurred and is not epistemically available to Alice | Does not by itself deny tenseless future truth. |
| Actuality indexing | the complete actual history $H^*$ | $G$ knows Alice's act because it is part of $H^*$ | Knowledge tracks the actual history rather than selecting the act. |

The intended ACO model primarily requires **actuality indexing**. It may use temporal and perspectival indexing to explain creaturely language such as “not yet,” without requiring the stronger thesis that future contingent propositions lack any truth value.

## Preferred working model: E+

Provisional name: **agent-causal eternalism with actuality-indexed omniscience**.

1. One complete actual history $H^*$ exists tenselessly.
2. $G$ knows $H^*$ as a whole and does not update its knowledge temporally.
3. Alice's act is located at $t_1$; relative to Alice at $t_0$, it has not yet occurred.
4. The tenseless proposition “Alice chooses $X$ at $t_1$” is nevertheless true in $H^*$.
5. Alternative choices remain metaphysically available in virtue of Alice's agent-causal power, even though only one is actual.
6. Alice's act is the truthmaker for the proposition and therefore the epistemic ground of divine knowledge; divine knowledge is not its efficient cause.
7. Interventions are constituents of $H^*$, not later revisions of an already completed history.

This selects eternalism as the primary architecture. The `+` marks the additional commitments to libertarian agent causation, asymmetric co-production, and ACO; it does not claim a new school of philosophy.

## Secondary comparison model: F

The open-future, frame-indexed model instead says that before the act no future branch is privileged and the future contingent lacks a determinate truth value. An atemporal God may know all indexed frames without learning sequentially.

This model remains useful for one controlled comparison because it preserves the literal, alethic reading of “not true yet.” It is no longer co-primary unless E+ fails or the author later decides that future contingents literally lack truth values.

## Consequences of preferring E+

- The freedom problem cannot be solved merely by saying that there was no future truth to know.
- The main burden becomes the **modal/fixity problem**: how can an infallibly known actual act still be one the agent could have done otherwise?
- “Possible histories” should normally represent modal alternatives, not simultaneously actual branches.
- ACO becomes simpler: it ranges over truths of $H^*$.
- Modal knowledge remains a separable extension concerning non-actual alternatives.
- An intervention at $t$ belongs to the complete history; talk of God “changing” the history is normally creature-relative shorthand.
- Providence concerns the architecture and contents of $H^*$, including genuine creaturely contributions, without requiring Molinist counterfactuals.

## Literature location

- [Law, Miller, and Rasmussen](https://doi.org/10.1017/can.2024.36) provide the closest checked precedent for eternalism combined with strong causal libertarianism and divine foreknowledge.
- [De Florio and Frigerio](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11153-014-9471-4) are closer to the alternative frame-indexed model in which future free propositions remain indeterminate until the act.
- [Rogers](https://doi.org/10.14428/thl.v9i2.85953) is relevant to eternal perspective, creaturely input, and qualified providence.

These comparisons belong here and in the literature chapter. They should be recalled elsewhere only when a specific dependency requires them.

## Decisions

| Decision | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use E+ as the primary model | `preferred` | Best fits the author's intended “complete universe with all times” picture. |
| Retain F as a diagnostic alternative | `open alternative` | Tests the literal “not true yet” intuition and clarifies the cost of E+. |
| Interpret ACO as actuality-indexed, not necessarily frame-indexed | `preferred` | Separates divine knowledge from temporal acquisition without importing open-future semantics. |
