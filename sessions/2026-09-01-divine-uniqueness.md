# Session: Divine uniqueness

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Session ID:** S13
**Primary question:** Does the preferred necessary ground have to be one?
**Dependencies:** C04–C05, C68–C75, C77–C79, S10–S12
**Target depth:** F1 reconstruction, plurality stress test, and targeted precedent check

## Result

The project prefers one ultimate ground of the total contingent order and, conditional on its existing necessary-agent commitments, one divine ultimate-ground agent. The conclusion is abductive rather than demonstrative.

Let $\mathcal C$ be the total contingent order:

\[
U_G\quad\text{iff}\quad
\exists!g\,UltimateGround(g,\mathcal C),
\]

\[
U_D\quad\text{iff}\quad
\exists!g\,[Necessary(g)\land Agent(g)\land UltimateGround(g,\mathcal C)].
\]

$U_G$ is preferred directly by the comparative argument below. Given C04–C05:

\[
U_G\land C04\land C05\;\Rightarrow\;U_D.
\]

Neither thesis says that only one necessary truth, property, abstract object, or derivative necessary being exists. S12's unrestricted $U_N$ is retained only as a scope contrast and is not a project target.

## Audit of standard routes

[Aquinas's three arguments](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1011.htm#article3) have unequal force inside the present architecture:

| Route | Additional premise | Assessment here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simplicity and individuation | God is identical with the divine nature or existence | Valid as a conditional classical route; unavailable from minimal non-composition alone |
| Exhaustive perfection | God possesses the whole perfection of being | Conditional only because the project has not derived perfection and rejects importing it by definition |
| Unity of order | One integrated cosmic order points to one ordering source | Useful abductively; cooperation or unity of counsel prevents a deductive inference |

Contemporary surveys add total causality or sovereignty and omnipotence arguments. The project's existential sovereignty can support one total source without making $G$ the sufficient cause of the originating content of every creaturely free act. Qualified omnipotence supplies further pressure only if the proposed divine agents possess independently maximal action domains.

## Plural-ground stress test

The strongest plural alternatives take three forms:

| Model | How it grounds $\mathcal C$ | Comparative cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partitioned | Each $G_i$ grounds a distinct suborder $\mathcal C_i$ | Cross-domain laws, relations, and coordination require an additional ground; otherwise the model does not explain one integrated order |
| Joint | $\{G_1,\ldots,G_n\}$ grounds $\mathcal C$ only as a collective | The coordinating structure becomes an additional fundamental fact and performs much of the unifying work |
| Redundantly total | Every $G_i$ grounds all of $\mathcal C$ | Adds idle duplication and requires possible conflict, reciprocal restriction, or primitive necessary concordance |

None is formally contradictory without further premises. Their cost is additional partition, organization, redundancy, or modal harmony. The one-ground model is therefore preferred for explanatory unity and economy, not presented as the only logically describable model.

## Power argument

Suppose $G_1$ and $G_2$ are distinct ultimate agents with independently maximal power. For some coherent contingent effect, either:

1. their wills can diverge, creating a case in which both cannot exercise the claimed power;
2. each agent's domain is restricted by the other;
3. their wills necessarily agree.

The first two qualify independent maximality. The third can preserve coexistence, but necessary concordance becomes an additional modal fact requiring an account. This reinforces $U_D$ without converting the cumulative case into a premise-free proof.
