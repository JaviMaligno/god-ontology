# Session: Qualified omnipotence and non-continuous dependence

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Session ID:** S11
**Primary question:** How can omnipotence be developed consistently with ACO/MKO, E+, intervention, and creaturely agency?
**Dependencies:** C04–C20, C31–C46, C51, S10
**Target depth:** F1 with only the formal distinctions required to prevent circularity

## Authorial corrections

The attribute map understated the intended cognitive profile: minimal intellect is only a bridge, while the project already aims at ACO plus modal knowledge. Relative transcendence and uniqueness are development tasks rather than endpoints; standard unity arguments should later be reconstructed. Full immutability was introduced without a present role and is deferred.

The author prefers a parsimonious God who possesses sustaining and intervening power without needing to exercise it continuously. Capacity, exercise, and existential dependence must therefore be separated.

## Power types

| Power or relation | Meaning | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actualizing power | Capacity to make a generative structure actual | earned/preferred |
| Existential grounding | Created entities, arena, and powers depend on $G$ for existence | preferred conditional on C04–C05 |
| Enabling power | Capacity to establish structures in which secondary causes and agents operate | earned/preferred |
| Intervening power | Capacity for additional token contributions within history | provisional but already modeled |
| Repeated temporal maintenance | A new sustaining act at every created time | not required |

Capacity does not entail exercise:

\[
CanPerform(G,a)\not\Rightarrow Performs(G,a).
\]

In E+, global dependence may be atemporal:

\[
Created(x)\rightarrow DependsExistentially(x,G),
\]

\[
DependsExistentially(x,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists t\,RepeatedMaintenance(G,x,t).
\]

This preserves existential sovereignty without turning every moment into a fresh intervention or making $G$ the originating author of creaturely acts.

## Qualified omnipotence

Define the independent coherent-action domain:

\[
\mathcal A_M(G)=\{a:Coherent(a,N,E_G)\}.
\]

$N$ supplies necessary constraints and $E_G$ only essential features established independently. Then:

\[
OMP_Q(G)
\quad\text{iff}\quad
\forall a\in\mathcal A_M(G)\;CanPerform(G,a).
\]

The definition excludes contradictions, not difficult actions. It cannot be trivialized by adding every discovered incapacity to $E_G$.

The freedom case is consistent. Sufficiently determining a token choice while preserving that same token as libertarian-originated is one incoherent task description. $G$ can preserve the creature's sourcehood or override it; omnipotence does not require the impossible conjunction.

## Consistency with knowledge

MKO includes the coherent action domain, powers, and compossibility constraints. ACO includes every actual exercise. Neither knowledge relation causes an exercise:

| Epistemic component | Power component |
| --- | --- |
| MKO knows what can coherently be done | $G$ has the relevant capacities if $OMP_Q$ holds |
| ACO knows which powers are exercised | $G$ contingently exercises or refrains from them |

This mirrors the existing actual/modal distinction without rebuilding its semantics.

## Current assessment

Qualified omnipotence is the preferred definition. The established architecture supports actualizing, grounding, enabling, and intervening powers, and no created reality can be the ultimate limiter of its own ground. This makes maximality plausible and coherent.

The remaining gap is not definitional but justificatory: ultimate grounding does not deductively exclude a non-created external limiter or an essential limitation internal to $G$. A bounded-power model would, however, add an ultimate boundary $L_G$. That boundary requires independent motivation; if external, it also adds another item to the fundamental ontology. Without such motivation, maximality gives greater explanatory unity with less brute boundary-setting.

C75 is therefore provisionally favored on abductive and preferential grounds. It is not obtained through an imported perfection premise and is not presented as a demonstration. No additional maximality axiom is adopted at this stage; a substantive internal-limit defeater could reopen the result.

## Later F2 refinement in S32

[S32](2026-09-02-qualified-omnipotence-blockers.md) distinguishes established powers, created-order maximality $OMP_C$, and unrestricted $OMP_Q$. It types eligible divine actions independently, separates range from strength, and refuses to count enabling a creaturely libertarian act as strong divine production of its content. No created relatum is an ultimate blocker under sole divine grounding; $OMP_C$ is preferred at F2, while $OMP_Q$ remains preferred but more provisional because the universal-range bridge and exclusion of non-created concrete limiters are not deductively available.
