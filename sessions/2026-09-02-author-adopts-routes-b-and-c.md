## The decision and its rationale

The author's reasoning is recorded as given: C is nearly free and fits what the truthful-relation anchor already says; B adds the valence-directed reason without paying A's price, which is continuation as an admissibility condition on the created order; D is worth developing because love is a question in its own right, and any further gain may come from there. The branch's assessment agrees. B together with C is, as it happens, the classical position on divine love as *willing the good of the other* (Aquinas, ST I q.20): a volitional orientation toward each creature's good, without passion. A adds a filter the tradition did not add, and D adds passibility, which the tradition mostly refused. So B and C leave the project where the mainstream of theism stands, with the difference that here it is earned in stages and its remainder is named.

## What is adopted

**Route 2′ (C665).** Adequate recognition of a subject as a subject counts the subject's anchors among the considerations that can defeat and be defeated. This is adopted as part of the content of $R$, at C105's evidential level. Consequence: a permission whose reason has no fit-standing subordinates a bearer's misfit to something that is not a good for anyone, which is a failure of recognition and so a misfit toward $R$; since $GoodOrient_R(G)$ is earned (C463), every reason for which $G$ permits a suffering is a cover. $G_1$ is thereby earned:

\[
GoodOrient_R(G)\land \text{Route }2'\ \rightarrow\ G_1.
\]

**Route 1 in favoring mode (C666).** $\langle P,\text{favoring},\text{creation}\rangle\in\mathcal R_G$ and $\langle P,\text{favoring},\text{specification}\rangle\in\mathcal R_G$: subjects are made actual for their own sake, and among eligible structures and interventions those that realize bearers' own horizons more fully are more attractive. Consequence: a defeasible presumption toward bearer-level closure, at both the creation and the intervention level, on the same footing as the agency-preservation presumptions (C115, C501): other things equal, $G$ prefers histories and interventions under which each bearer's suffering is covered within the bearer's horizon. $G_3$ is not thereby closed, since a favoring reason can be outweighed.

$GoodOrient_P(G)$ is therefore partial in a precise sense (C667): the profile is valence-directed, since it now contains a $P$-reason, and it satisfies two of the three conditions; the third it favors without guaranteeing. The P-gap narrows from “no disposition toward valence” (C464 as written in S48) to “no guarantee that each bearer's horizon closes.” The problem of evil enters the architecture at $G_3$ and nowhere else.

Two consequences follow for S49. First, the threshold residue changes shape. Under $G_1$, the gravest tokens that could be prevented without collapsing the policy are permitted for a reason that is a cover; the candidate is the diachronic component of the source cover (C504), under which lowering the prevention threshold funnels the field of consequences. The residue is no longer whether such permissions have a cover but whether that cover's weight defeats the misfit in the gravest cases; the arbitrary-threshold reply stays contested (C474). Second, the bearer-level remainder (C477–C478) is now the object of a presumption rather than a blank: the profile favors its closure, and continuation is the way closure is available to bearers whose lives end uncovered (C478).

## What the register's belief becomes

C479 and C651 fixed the register's faith content as $G_3$ with continuation as its consequence, which is Route 1 in requiring mode. After S84 the belief has a different relation to the profile (C668): it is no longer an addition foreign to the established reasons but the completion of a presumption the profile contains. To believe that $G$ is good in what we feel is to believe that the presumption toward closure is never in fact defeated, that is, that $G_3$ holds; and to believe that is to believe in continuation for the uncovered (C485). The status of continuation is unchanged: it is faith content, not an admissibility condition, because A was not adopted. What changes is that the belief now goes with the grain of the profile rather than beyond it.

## The research line on love (CH27)

The author's questions are banked as accessible questions P55–P58 and opened as CH27: whether love means anything beyond biology; whether love between persons and love of $G$ are the same thing; whether $G$ loves each one in particular; whether $G$ loves itself. Route 3 (C650) is the ontological core of the line: what it would be for $G$ to have affective regard, what it would cost, and whether the volitional love that B and C already earn is what “God is love” means. S85 gives the line a first typing.
