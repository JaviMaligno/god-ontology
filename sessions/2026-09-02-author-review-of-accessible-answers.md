## No gap in the ontology was exposed

Each correction was answerable from existing rows. The two that came closest to a gap were the chance objection and the branching justification; in both cases the ledger held the answer (C406, C459; C484) and the accessible text had not used it. This is the expected failure mode of the accessible register, and the reason rule 2 exists.
