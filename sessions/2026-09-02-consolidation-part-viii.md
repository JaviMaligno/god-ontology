## Reconciliation with the author's S44

S44 promotes identity to the next F2 trunk module (C434, CH18) and asks for a general framework $IdGround_K(x,t_1,t_2,c)$ that distinguishes numerical identity from qualitative similarity, individuation from persistence, constitution from reidentification evidence, continuous from intermittent existence, essential preservation from preservation of every property, and creaturely persistence from the identity of $G$ across modal witnesses. The question-driven track's S53 and S55 already contain each of these distinctions: the four-notion partition (C514), individuation of subjects as sources and of $G$ by role (C515–C516), simple persistence scoped to sources with continuity-based persistence for non-subjects (C506, C517), identification as epistemic and never constitutive (C518), gappy existence under E+ (C508), de re recreation and duplicates (C509–C510), and modal identity across histories as the same primitive as individuation (C519). The branch therefore offers its results as a candidate framework for the promoted module, not as its completion: the author's list also asks for a comparison of candidate families (organismic, hylomorphic, psychological, causal, simple, haecceitist, constitution, pattern) applied symmetrically to ordinary persistence, duplication, gaps, and divine cases, which S53 did only in part. The offer is recorded as C555 and is not promoted.
