Lennox's *Can Science Explain Everything?* (2019), especially pp. 34–36, is the source family; “science describes but does not explain” is treated as the author's paraphrase rather than a verified verbatim quotation. A later authorial clarification identifies Lennox as the project's principal philosophical inspiration and adds his challenge from unguided naturalistic origins to confidence in human reasoning. The treatise now presents that challenge as background abductive motivation, targets evolutionary naturalism rather than evolution or atheism simpliciter, states the adaptive-reliability and institutional-error-correction replies, and marks the link to asymmetrically transmitted finite agency as the author's own extension. No ledger commitment is inferred from influence alone.
