# Session: Created possibility and intentional content

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S38
**Primary questions:** What grounds created metaphysical possibility independently of divine power, knowledge, actual choice, and creaturely imagination; and what kind of reality belongs to an imaginative act, its content, and its represented object?
**Dependencies:** C12, C47–C54, C255–C281, C292, C324–C342, CH05, CH14, S05, S32–S34, S37
**Target depth:** F2 modal ground and F1–F2 intentionality test; no complete meta-modal theory

## Result in one sentence

The preferred F2 account is **constitutive-admissibility actualism**: a created specification is possible when a complete extension satisfies logical and identity constraints, real definitions or constitutive profiles, power-profile coherence, global compossibility, and created-dependence conditions; these modal facts are not additional concrete worlds and are neither made true nor certified by being known, chosen, or imagined. This removes the power-relative circularity from B1 of S33, while leaving the ultimate ground of the necessary constitutive profiles and modal sole-source invariance as distinct deeper questions.

The author's “Ok adelante” after S37 accepts its F2 realization and stopping point under the default-acceptance rule and authorizes this next priority.

## The independence required

Four relations had been compressed together:

| Question | Relation |
| --- | --- |
| Is specification $S$ metaphysically eligible? | $Poss_C^{ind}(S)$ |
| Does a subject represent that eligibility? | $Rep(x,S)$ or $Knows(x,Poss_C^{ind}(S))$ |
| Can an agent actualize it? | $CanCreate(x,S)$ |
| Is it selected and actual? | $Selects(x,S)$ and $Actual(S)$ |

The bridge in S33 needs the first relation to constrain the third. Therefore it cannot use either of these biconditionals:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)\leftrightarrow CanCreate(G,S),
\]

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)\leftrightarrow Knows_G(Poss_C^{ind}(S)).
\]

The required independence is **criterion independence**: the truth conditions for $Poss_C^{ind}$ do not contain the divine capacity whose range is being tested, the actual divine choice, or a subject's representation. It does not yet assert that every necessary truth or constitutive profile is ontologically independent of $G$. That stronger ultimacy question remains open and must not be smuggled into B1.

## Candidate families

| Family | What grounds possibility | Main advantage | Main burden in this project |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| concrete modal realism | concrete non-actual worlds | sharp truth conditions independent of actual choice and imagination | multiplies concrete reality and makes divine creation and knowledge answer to worlds not produced by the selected actual creative act |
| abstract-world actualism | maximal propositions, states, models, or world-representations | avoids concrete duplicate universes and gives useful semantics | representation still needs a criterion distinguishing a genuinely possible model from a merely consistent or impossible description |
| pure logical consistency | absence of formal contradiction | clear and economical | misses substantive identity, essence, dependence, and power constraints; formal vocabulary can conceal the impossibility |
| divine power reduction | $S$ is possible iff $G$ can actualize it | integrates modality with theism | circular for the present derivation of creative range |
| divine-idea or knowledge reduction | a possible is a divine representation | gives every possibility an actual cognitive locus | knowing or representing $S$ does not by itself explain why the represented content is possible rather than impossible |
| token-power actualism | manifestations supported by powers of actual things | fits the project's powers ontology and avoids possible worlds | cannot by itself cover wholly uninstantiated created kinds or pre-creative specifications without using $G$'s disputed range |
| essence-first account | possibilities follow from real definitions and constitutive natures | explains why possibility is narrower than syntax and why identity matters | needs global compossibility, dynamic power profiles, and an ontology for uninstantiated types |
| constitutive-admissibility actualism | essence, power, identity, dependence, and compossibility constraints jointly determine admissible specifications | fits C48 after repair, preserves actualism, and remains independent of the target divine power | retains irreducible modal or constitutive structure and owes a deeper account of its ultimate ground |

The last family is preferred at F2. It is a non-reductive grounding account: it orders modal explanation without pretending to eliminate every modal primitive.

## Constitutive-admissibility actualism

Let $S$ remain the generative specification $\langle L,C,B,O\rangle$. A complete candidate specification may add enough type, power, identity, and dependence information to settle its metaphysical eligibility. Define:

\[
Adm_C(S):=
WF(S)
\land Cons_{LM}(S)
\land EssSat(S)
\land PowSat(S)
\land Comp(S)
\land DepSat_C(S).
\]

The conditions are:

| Condition | Work performed |
| --- | --- |
| $WF(S)$ | $S$ has determinate enough terms and typing to be assessed |
| $Cons_{LM}(S)$ | $S$ satisfies logical, identity, and the mathematical consequences of its stated structure |
| $EssSat(S)$ | its kinds and relata satisfy their real definitions or constitutive profiles |
| $PowSat(S)$ | laws and proposed changes fit the manifestation profiles attributed to their bearers |
| $Comp(S)$ | all locally admissible components can obtain jointly in one order |
| $DepSat_C(S)$ | the proposed basis has a coherent created or dependent profile and does not combine dependence with underived ultimity in the same respect |

Then:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)\leftrightarrow Adm_C(S).
\]

This biconditional is a working truth-condition, not a claim that a formal test available to humans decides every case. `EssSat`, `PowSat`, and `Comp` express objective constitutive relations rather than mere derivability from our current descriptions.

### Partial specifications

Most human proposals are incomplete. Their possibility is existential over complete admissible extensions:

\[
Poss_C^{partial}(s)
\leftrightarrow
\exists S\,[Extends(S,s)\land Adm_C(S)].
\]

Failure to detect a contradiction in $s$ is weak evidence because no admissible completion may exist. This explains why a verbal scenario can look coherent while hiding an identity or essence conflict.

### Structures and histories

The criterion must preserve the distinction between a possible generative order and its possible histories:

\[
PossStruct(S)\leftrightarrow Adm_C(S),
\]

\[
PossHist(h\mid S)
\leftrightarrow
PossStruct(S)\land HistSat(h,S).
\]

Where $S$ includes libertarian agents, $HistSat$ requires that each free token outcome fall within a real power of its agent and the shared causal past. It does not require a pre-actual truth that the agent *would* choose that outcome:

\[
CanOriginate(A,a\mid P_{<t},S)
\not\Rightarrow
WouldOriginate(A,a\mid P_{<t},S).
\]

Possible histories remain representations of compossible manifestations, not additional concrete eternalist blocks. Only one complete history bears the actual marker.

## What exists on this account

The account distinguishes ontological categories rather than treating “a possibility” as one ambiguous object:

1. the actual concrete order and its actual history, if created;
2. actual subjects and their actual acts of representing;
3. actual representational vehicles or structures instantiated in those acts;
4. objective constitutive, power, identity, and compossibility facts;
5. specifications or world-models used to represent ways reality could be.

It does not add a concrete universe for each admissible specification. A possible created individual is not thereby an existing concrete subject. A type-level constitutive profile can constrain an admissible specification without a token bearer already existing.

The weakest sufficient ontological commitment at F2 is realism about the relevant constitutive relations or truths, not about concrete possibilia. Their ultimate basis remains open between:

- necessary structural or essential facts that do not function as rival concrete agents or causes;
- grounding in independently established necessary features of $G$, without making divine knowing or willing constitutive of their truth;
- a mixed account in which logic and identity are fundamental while created-type profiles are grounded in the rational and power-structured nature of $G$.

This residual question is meta-modal. It does not restore the S33 circularity unless $Adm_C(S)$ is defined through $CanCreate(G,S)$.

## Imagination and thought as CH14's test

Let:

\[
Img(A,i,p,t)
\]

mean that subject $A$ performs imaginative act $i$ at $t$ with content $p$. At minimum:

\[
Img(A,i,p,t)
\rightarrow
Actual(i,t)\land Rep(i,p).
\]

It does not follow that:

\[
Actual(p),
\qquad
Exists(Obj(p)),
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
Poss_C^{ind}(p).
\]

The actual reality of imagining lies first in the temporal act of the embodied subject and its realized representational organization. The fact that the act has content $p$ is actual. The target represented through $p$ may be actual, non-actual but possible, impossible, fictional, or too incomplete to classify.

This does not require deciding now whether contents are abstract propositions, immanent structures of acts, or another kind of representational entity. The minimum commitment is relational:

\[
\exists i\,Actual(i)\land Rep(i,p)
\]

does not require a concrete intentional object corresponding to $p$.

Thought is broader than sensory imagination, but the same typing applies to supposition, planning, memory errors, fiction, and propositional reasoning. An actual cognitive episode can be causally efficacious because the episode is actual even when its target is absent or impossible.

### Epistemic role without modal constitution

Imagination may provide defeasible evidence for possibility when the content is positively developed, stable under scrutiny, supported by adequate concepts, and not carried merely by an assigned label. It is never the truthmaker:

\[
Img(A,i,p,t)\land QImg(A,i,p)
\Rightarrow_{def}
Evidence_A(Poss(p)),
\]

\[
Img(A,i,p,t)\not\Rightarrow Poss(p).
\]

Peter Kung's distinction between qualitative and assigned content gives a useful diagnostic. A subject can picture two objects and assign them identities that are necessarily identical, or leave a decisive constitutive detail unspecified. The assignment does not transmit modal evidence. Conversely, cognitive limits make imaginability unnecessary for possibility.

The evidential hierarchy is therefore:

\[
mere\ verbal\ description
< negative\ conceivability
< detailed\ imagination
< scrutinized\ constitutive\ model,
\]

with no stage replacing $Adm_C$ as the truth condition.

## Consequences for divine knowledge

MKO tracks the admissibility facts and their compossible extensions:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)
\rightarrow
Knows_G(Poss_C^{ind}(S)),
\]

conditional on the established MKO profile. The direction is epistemic tracking, not truthmaking by cognition. Atemporal knowing removes temporal acquisition; it does not reverse the grounding direction.

Possible conscious types and agentive power profiles may therefore be represented exhaustively without there being a concrete merely possible subject, an $ASA$ relation to that subject, or a fact about what that subject would freely choose. Actual subject acquaintance begins only with an actual bearer and episode.

## Effect on the S33 power bridge

S38 replaces the power-relative reading of C48 with:

\[
Adm_C(S)
\rightarrow
Poss_C^{ind}(S),
\]

where none of the conjuncts is $CanCreate(G,S)$. B1 of the modal-source bridge is therefore locally available at F2. The full inference still requires:

1. modal sole-source invariance: the same necessary $G$ is the ultimate source in every admissible world containing a fundamental created order;
2. essential possession of the relevant originative power;
3. agent-relative accessibility and specification transparency;
4. later, separate bridges for constitutive control, history-sensitive intervention, and task-domain exhaustion.

The present result strengthens creative plenitude without proving it. It also reveals a useful asymmetry: possibility constrains power range; complete divine knowledge tracks possibility; actual choice selects one admissible specification; exercised power makes the selected order actual.

## Adversarial tests

### Hidden modal vocabulary

`Power profile` and `compossibility` are modal notions. The account therefore does not reduce modality to non-modal vocabulary. This is not circular in the problematic sense because it does not define the range of $G$'s power through itself. It is a non-reductive order of ground: local possibilities are constrained by constitutive profiles and their joint fit.

### Alien or wholly uninstantiated kinds

Actual token powers alone cannot ground every created possibility. Type-level profiles must be admissible without a token instance. The account treats these as structural or real-definitional contents rather than dormant powers of nonexistent objects. Why the space contains those profiles rather than others remains an F3 meta-modal burden.

### “Anything can be made a type”

A label does not supply a constitutive profile. An alleged type enters the modal domain only if a complete specification satisfies $EssSat$, $PowSat$, $Comp$, and $DepSat_C$. This blocks fabricated kinds whose definitions conceal incompatible roles.

### A Platonic realm above $G$

Necessary constraints are not automatically substances, agents, efficient causes, or co-ultimate concrete grounds. C264 therefore prevents an immediate plurality objection. Whether their truth is ultimately grounded in $G$ or remains necessary and primitive is a later ultimacy question, not a reason to identify possibility with divine choice.

### The actual world might fail the test

The actual created order must satisfy every genuine constitutive constraint. If the project's description of it produces inconsistency, that description is defeated; actuality cannot be used to protect an incoherent model. Human inability to exhibit a complete specification limits our proof, not the world's eligibility.

## Literature and novelty notes

- [Kit Fine, “Essence and Modality”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/366643157_ESSENCE_AND_MODALITY_Kit_Fine_Philosophy_NYU) argues that essence should not be explained through necessity and suggests the explanatory direction may run from essence to modality. The project uses this direction without adopting a complete Finean logic of essence.
- [Barbara Vetter, “Essence, Potentiality, and Modality”](https://philpapers.org/archive/VETEPA.pdf) compares essence-first and potentiality-first grounds and argues that they are competitors connected by a weaker semi-duality. This cautions against pretending that the present essence-and-power hybrid follows automatically from either family.
- [Peter Kung, “Imagining as a Guide to Possibility”](https://pages.pomona.edu/~pfk04747/research/papers.html) distinguishes qualitative from assigned imaginative content and argues that assignment alone provides no modal evidence, while mixed imagination can still be evidential.
- [David Chalmers, “Does Conceivability Entail Possibility?”](https://digitalcollections.anu.edu.au/items/28e938ae-e396-4d15-94b2-8a76fe24aa38/full) distinguishes several notions of conceivability and defends carefully qualified links to possibility. The project adopts no general entailment and uses conceivability only as defeasible evidence.
- [Paul Sheehy, “Theism and modal realism”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/abs/theism-and-modal-realism/90C9D3D421CF821B4556E5E0112B7305) identifies tensions between concrete modal realism and classical theistic claims about divine necessity, knowledge, and creation. Those tensions support caution here without constituting a general refutation of modal realism.

Essence grounding, powers theories of modality, actualism, possible-world representation, and the fallibility of imagination are established families. The potentially distinctive element is their local integration with the project's actual-marker semantics, libertarian history branches, ACO/MKO/ASA typing, and the four-premise modal-source bridge. This is a project synthesis, not yet a novelty claim.
