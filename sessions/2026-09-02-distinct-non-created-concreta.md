## Outcome

The question splits into two, and both halves close conditionally.

First, a non-created concrete relatum that *limits* $G$ is possible exactly when one of two premises the trunk already prefers fails: modal grounding coverage extended to every contingent fact ($MGC^+$) or modal uniqueness of the underived source ($MUU$). Under both, every possible distinct non-created concrete is either a necessary dependent concretum (an emanation) or an inert necessary concretum, and neither is a limiter in the sense of S32's blocker taxonomy: its necessary features are constraints, its contingent features are dependent conditions, and any settlings of its own are role mismatches. This is the **no-limiter lemma** ($NLL$). It converts the “non-created concrete limiter” row of S32's table, which had stood as an unanalyzed live defeater (C265), into a named modal failure of $MGC^+\land MUU$.

Second, the surviving cells are coherent but outside the preferred ontology, for reasons independent of power: an emanation requires a necessary exercise by $G$, which the preferred model of divine production excludes (every $OEC^+$ exercise is contingent, C363), and an inert necessary concretum either fails a causal criterion of concreteness or, if it grounds its own contingent states, falls under $MUU$. The only eligible tasks concerning a surviving relatum are contingent, essence-compatible state changes, whose range would be a small extension of the intervention ranges to dependent non-created relata. No range is assigned within $\mathcal O_*$, because $\mathcal O_*$ contains no such relatum; a rival ontology that adopts one carries the burden.

The consequence for omnipotence is a reduction, not an upgrade. Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ had two cross-ontology conditions: no limiting non-created concretum and no independently motivated novel action kind (C585, R224). The first now reduces to $MGC^+\land MUU$, whose status is abductive preference (C361). Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ over concreta is therefore exactly as strong as conditional modal ultimity, and no stronger. What remains cross-ontology is the second condition, the trunk's open question 2.

## Step 1: What “distinct non-created concrete” can mean

Write

\[
NCC(L):=Concrete(L)\land\neg Created(L)\land L\neq G,
\]

where $Created(L)$ means that $L$'s existence is originated, immediately or through derivative production terminating in $G$, by an exercise of $P_{OEC}$ (the $OEC^+$ relation with C359 and C364's allowance for proximate producers). Two independent partitions apply to any candidate $L$: its modal status (necessary or contingent) and its dependence (underived, or existentially grounded in $G$ by some typed relation other than origination, C32). This gives four cells.

| Cell | Description | Examples in the literature and traditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (N,U) underived necessary | a second necessary concrete not grounded in $G$: impersonal (co-fundamental matter $M_0$, a necessary field or structure), agentic (a second necessary agent), or inert (grounds nothing contingent) | demiurge with pre-existent matter; cosmic dualism; plural uncreated souls |
| (N,D) derived necessary | a concrete that exists in every world and is existentially grounded in $G$ without being created, since creation is contingent | Neoplatonic emanation of *Nous* from the One |
| (C,U) underived contingent | a brute contingent concrete | brute-basis countermodels to $CMU_G$ (C358) |
| (C,D) derived contingent | a contingent concrete existentially grounded in $G$ | this is creation; the cell is $Created(L)$ and does not satisfy $NCC$ |

Two candidates that are often raised do not satisfy $NCC$ and are set aside. Necessary abstracta (truths, properties, structures, Platonic forms) are not concrete; S32 already classifies them as modal constraints that are not causal patients. Plural divine persons within one being (CH01's fourth variant) are not distinct concrete relata on their own account, since the account denies that they are distinct substances; they require a separate person/being ontology and are neither adopted nor excluded here.

The fourth cell is creation. The question is therefore about (N,U), (N,D), and (C,U).

## Step 2: What it is for $L$ to limit $G$

S32's taxonomy sorts every candidate blocker into eight rows, of which only the last, “non-created concrete limiter,” is a genuine external defeater. The other rows are: no eligible task (contradiction or identity failure), role mismatch (a state whose contrastive source is another agent, C256), dependent condition (created resistance or law), preservation condition internal to the task, independently established essential feature of $G$, circular pseudo-exclusion, and necessary abstract constraint. The last row was left as a live defeater because no analysis said what makes a non-created concrete *external*. The definition needed is:

\[
Limiter_G(L):=NCC(L)\land\exists p\,[Concerns(p,L)\land Contingent(p)\land\neg UltGround(G,p)],
\]

where $UltGround(G,p)$ holds when the ultimate existential or originative ground of the fact $p$ is $G$, directly or through relata whose existence and powers are themselves grounded in $G$. The definition says that $L$ limits $G$ only if some contingent fact concerning $L$ escapes $G$'s grounding: it is brute, or its ultimate ground is an underived concrete other than $G$.

The definition is justified by elimination over the fact-types concerning $L$. A *necessary* fact about $L$ (its existence, if $L$ is necessary; its essential features) is a constraint on task coherence, not a power contest; a task whose result contradicts it is not an eligible task, exactly as for abstract necessities (S32 row 7). This is not the circular pseudo-exclusion of row 6, because the necessity is $L$'s, established independently of any claim about $G$'s abilities. A *contingent* fact about $L$ whose ultimate ground is $G$ is a dependent condition (row 3), whatever proximate role $L$ plays (C364). A *contingent* fact about $L$ that is $L$'s own settling, where $L$ is a dependent agent, is a role mismatch (row 2), as for creaturely free acts. Only a contingent fact about $L$ with no ground in $G$ remains, and that is what the definition names. So $Limiter_G$ captures S32's eighth row and nothing else.

## Step 3: The no-limiter lemma

S39 decomposed $CMU_G$ into three components (C362). Two are needed here.

*Modal grounding coverage* $MGC$ says that every possible instantiated fundamental created basis has an underived concrete ultimate existential ground. Its scope is fundamental created bases, which was all S39 needed. The present question needs its scope to be every contingent fact concerning a concrete, and the strengthening should be explicit:

\[
MGC^+:\quad\Box\forall p\,[(Contingent(p)\land ConcernsConcrete(p))\rightarrow\exists x\,(Underived(x)\land Concrete(x)\land UltGround(x,p))].
\]

$MGC^+$ is the modal form of the actual thesis the project already holds: $U_G$ says that exactly one being is the ultimate ground of *the total contingent order* (C80), which includes every contingent fact, not only fundamental bases; and S47's partition of stopping points (C459) ranges over the contingent order as a whole. $MGC^+$ carries the same status as $MGC$: preferred, not demonstrated, since brute contingent facts remain a coherent countermodel (C358, C361). It is recorded as its own row so that nothing here rests on an unstated generalization.

*Modal uniqueness of the underived source* $MUU$ says that, necessarily, any underived concrete existential ground is identical with $G$ (C362; glossary).

**Lemma ($NLL$).** $MGC^+\land MUU\rightarrow\neg\Diamond\exists L\,Limiter_G(L)$.

*Proof.* Suppose, in some world, $Limiter_G(L)$. Then some contingent fact $p$ concerning $L$ has no ultimate ground in $G$. By $MGC^+$, $p$ has an underived concrete ultimate ground $x$. By $MUU$, $x=G$. So $UltGround(G,p)$, contradicting the supposition. $\square$

The proof uses nothing about $G$'s power. It uses only the explanatory-coverage premise and the uniqueness premise, which is why it does not commit the circularity S44 warned against: $OMP_Q$ is not used to prove the coherence or incoherence of the relatum.

## Step 4: What survives, and why it is not in $\mathcal O_*$

$NLL$ does not say that no non-created concrete is possible. It says that none is a limiter. The three cells of Step 1 fare as follows under $MGC^+\land MUU$.

**(C,U), brute contingent concreta.** Excluded directly by $MGC^+$, since a brute contingent concrete's existence is a contingent fact with no ground. This is the brute-basis countermodel of C358, and it fails exactly when $MGC^+$ holds.

**(N,U), underived necessary concreta.** If $L$ grounds any contingent fact, whether a created order (the second-source countermodel of C358), its own exercises (a second necessary agent), or its own states (necessary matter with contingent configurations), then $L$ is an underived concrete existential ground, and $MUU$ identifies it with $G$; so the case is excluded. Co-fundamental matter (C238) and a second necessary agent (C81, C83) are the impersonal and agentic instances, and C491's dilemma and the necessitation lemma (C458) apply to the impersonal one. What is *not* excluded by $MUU$ is an **inert** necessary concretum: one that grounds no contingent fact at all, has no contingent states, and exercises nothing. Such an $L$ is not a limiter, since no contingent fact concerns it (Step 2), and it does not compete for ultimity, since $U_G$ concerns the ground of the contingent order. It offends only against economy and against the unrestricted uniqueness of every necessary entity, which the project does not claim (C78). Whether it is concrete at all is doubtful: on a causal criterion of concreteness, an entity with no causal power and no contingent state is an abstractum, and an entity with causal power that is ever exercised grounds a contingent fact and falls under $MUU$. So the inert cell is either empty or power-irrelevant.

**(N,D), necessary dependent concreta.** An emanation, a concrete that exists in every world because $G$ necessarily grounds it, is consistent with $MUU$ (it is derived) and with $MGC^+$ (its contingent states, if any, are grounded in $G$ through it, since a dependent bearer's powers are aspects that exist only with the bearer, C492). It is not a limiter: its necessary existence is a constraint, its contingent states are dependent conditions, and if it is an agent its settlings are role mismatches. It is nevertheless outside $\mathcal O_*$ for a reason that does not mention power. The preferred model of divine production is a contingent, specification-bearing, reason-selected exercise of $P_{OEC}$ ($OEC^+$; C371), and $CMU_G$ was expressly built not to imply necessary exercise of any divine power (C363); creation and non-creation remain live alternatives (C111, C132). An emanation would be a necessary exercise, and so an ontology extension of the same kind as a qualitative intrinsic mode (C582): coherent, not generated by any established kind, and carrying its own burden. The weak explanatory preference (C457) also counts against it, since it adds a necessary concrete that explains nothing the contingent order needed explained.

Hence, under the two premises, every possible $NCC$ relatum is an emanation or an inert necessary concretum, and neither limits $G$.

## Step 5: Which tasks concerning a surviving relatum pass $Task_G$

The second half of the question is answered by the same elimination. For an emanation $E$ or an inert necessary concretum $I$:

- *Create $E$ or $I$*: not an eligible task; both exist necessarily, and “originate what exists in every world” contradicts the description (S32 row 1).
- *Annihilate $E$ or $I$*: not an eligible task, for the same reason; this is not an incapacity but a necessary constraint (row 7), and treating it as an incapacity would be the move R237 rejects.
- *Change a contingent, essence-compatible state of $E$*: eligible in form, and its range is a dependent-relatum analogue of the created-directed ranges. $CC_G^{elig}$ and $HI_G^{elig}$ are defined over created relata, so covering $E$ would need their preservation and prefix conditions restated for a dependent but non-created bearer. This is a small extension, and it is not undertaken here because $\mathcal O_*$ contains no $E$; an ontology that adopts emanation owes the extension. Nothing about $E$ suggests the extension would fail, since the reasons the created ranges succeed (specification fidelity, preservation profiles, source roles) do not depend on the bearer being created.
- *Settle a state that is $E$'s own free settling*, if $E$ is an agent: a role mismatch, not a divine task (C256), exactly as for creatures.
- *Any task concerning $I$*: none is eligible, since $I$ has no contingent states.

So the answer to “what actions concerning it pass $Task_G$” is: contingent state changes only, with a range that is an extension rather than a new power, and nothing that could count against unrestricted $OMP_Q$.

## Step 6: What this does to unrestricted $OMP_Q$

C585 left unrestricted $OMP_Q$ preferred but provisional because of two cross-ontology tests: a distinct non-created concrete relatum and an independently motivated novel action kind (R224). The first test is now located: a limiting non-created concretum is possible if and only if $MGC^+\land MUU$ fails, and surviving non-limiting ones do not touch the range claim. So the evidential condition on unrestricted $OMP_Q$ contributed by non-created concreta is *identical* to the evidential condition already carried by conditional modal ultimity. This is a reduction of two independent gaps to one, not a closure: $MGC^+$ and $MUU$ remain abductive preferences (C361), and the project records them as such. The remaining cross-ontology test is open question 2, novel action kinds.

C265's “live defeater” status should be read accordingly: the defeater is live exactly as far as the countermodels to $CMU_G$ are live, and no further. C430's statement that the modal impossibility of such a relatum has not been demonstrated remains true, since $NLL$ is conditional on premises that are not demonstrated; but the impossibility is now conditional rather than unexamined.

## Adversarial tests

### Is $MGC^+$ a hidden strengthening that does all the work?

It is a strengthening and it is not hidden: C605 records it with its own status. It does the work jointly with $MUU$, and it is exactly the modal form of $U_G$'s quantification over the total contingent order. The alternative, keeping $MGC$ restricted to fundamental created bases, would leave contingent facts about a non-created concrete without an explanatory demand while imposing one on created bases, which no argument in S39 or S47 motivates. A theorist who rejects $MGC^+$ accepts brute contingent facts somewhere, and then the project's stopping-point comparison (S47) applies to them as it applies to a brute created order.

### Does $NLL$ define the limiter away?

No. $Limiter_G$ is derived in Step 2 from S32's own taxonomy by elimination over fact-types; it does not mention $G$'s abilities. A counterexample would be a fact concerning $L$ that is neither necessary, nor grounded in $G$, nor $L$'s own settling as a dependent agent, and is not brute. There is no such fourth kind.

### Does the emanation cell smuggle in a limit on refraining?

If $E$ exists necessarily, $G$ cannot refrain from grounding $E$. This is a necessary fact, not a task $G$ fails at, on the same footing as $G$'s inability to make itself not exist (S32's internal constraints). The project's answer is that emanation is not in $\mathcal O_*$, and that if it were, the refraining description would be ineligible rather than a defeat. What emanation would cost is not omnipotence but the contingency of every divine exercise, which is why it is excluded on that ground.

### Does this reopen $ANDC$?

No. An $ANDC$ creature is created (cell (C,D)) and is covered by $CP_G^{elig}$ (C570). The session concerns what is *not* created.

### Could a non-created concrete be dependent on $G$ by a relation other than existential grounding, so that $MGC^+$'s existential ground is $G$ while some other dependence relation points elsewhere?

C32's typed relations are existential grounding, structural constraint, ordinary causation, agent origination, and intervention. $Limiter_G$ quantifies over the ultimate *existential or originative* ground of contingent facts, which is what independence from $G$ would require; structural constraint by a necessary structure is row 7, ordinary causation by created causes is row 3, and origination by a dependent agent is row 2. A relation that made $L$ independent would be an underived existential or originative ground, and $MUU$ applies.

## Literature and precedent

Recorded from memory; to be verified in the author's next literature pass under the provenance checklist. The blocker analysis follows the shape of the omnipotence literature's distinction between range and external constraint (Geach's “power over all things” versus “ability to do everything,” already cited in S42 and S44). The emanation cell is the classical Plotinian scheme in which *Nous* proceeds necessarily from the One; its contrast with contingent creation is the standard Aquinas–Avicenna dividing line on whether God produces by necessity of nature or by will, which S47's necessitation lemma and C363 place on the side of will. Demiurgic and dualist architectures are the *Timaeus* and the Manichaean or Zoroastrian two-principle models that C238 and C265 already name. The claim that ultimity concerns the ground of the contingent order rather than the uniqueness of every necessary entity follows C78. No novelty is claimed for the taxonomy; the local contribution is the reduction of the non-created-relatum condition on unrestricted $OMP_Q$ to the components of $CMU_G$.
