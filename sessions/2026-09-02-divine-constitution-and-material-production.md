# Session: Divine constitution and material production

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S30
**Primary question:** What is $G$ made of, if anything, and how can an underived non-material subject produce a numerically distinct material substance?
**Dependencies:** C04–C07, C67–C103, C210–C211, S13–S18, S28
**Target depth:** F2 at the divine-constitution/OEC junction

## Result in one sentence

The preferred model treats $G$ as an **underived power-structured subject**: one necessary non-abstract agent that is not assembled from ontologically prior ingredients or borne by an external substrate, while possessing integrated essential powers that need not be numerically identical. Material creation is the exercise of a specific originative existential power whose distinct effect instantiates the material profile specified by $S$ without receiving a material ingredient from $G$.

This is stronger than saying only that “made of” is inapplicable and weaker than full divine simplicity.

## Decomposing “made of”

The ordinary question combines at least five relations:

| Question | Relation sought | Example in created reality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What stuff is it made from? | material input or patient | bronze in a statue |
| What parts compose it? | mereological composition | organs in an organism |
| What bears its features? | substrate or property bearer | a subject bearing capacities |
| What structure makes it the kind it is? | essence, organization, or constitutive profile | organization of a living body |
| What sort of individual is it? | ontological category | subject, event, field, abstract object, or substance |

Failure of the first relation does not answer the other four. Conversely, distinguishable powers do not automatically function as prior ingredients.

Define an ingredient as:

\[
Ingredient(x,y)
\leftrightarrow
x\neq y
\land Constitutes(x,y)
\land ConstitutivelyPrior(x,y).
\]

This priority condition matters. A feature can be intrinsic, essential, and genuinely distinguishable without being an independently available component from which its bearer is assembled.

## A thin use of substance

“Substance” will not mean material stuff or a bare propertyless substratum. At this depth it means a non-abstract individual that is the unitary bearer and source of its powers and acts:

\[
Substance_T(x)
\leftrightarrow
NonAbstractIndividual(x)
\land IdentityUnit(x)
\land BearsPowers(x).
\]

Created substances may depend existentially on another source and can be material composites. $G$ is a substance in this thin subject sense, not necessarily in every Aristotelian, Cartesian, or contemporary constituent-ontological sense. The definition prevents equivocation while leaving the general metaphysics of substance open.

There is no need to place a bare $g$-substratum behind divine consciousness, knowledge, will, and power. The subject-with-its-essential-powers is the proposed terminus. Replacing it with “substrate plus properties” would add structure without yet adding explanation.

## Minimal constitutive simplicity

Define:

\[
MCS(G)
\leftrightarrow
\neg\exists x\,Ingredient(x,G)
\land
\neg\exists s\,[s\neq G\land ExternalSubstrate(s,G)].
\]

$MCS$ excludes an ontologically prior material, bearer, or separable ingredient from which $G$ is constituted. It does not entail:

\[
G=Existence=Knowledge=Will=Power=Goodness,
\]

nor pure actuality, absence of every intrinsic distinction, absence of contingent acts, or the identity of every true divine predicate. Those stronger claims belong to absolute divine simplicity and require premises not established here.

The model also needs positive articulation:

\[
PowerStructured(G)
\leftrightarrow
\exists p_1,\ldots,p_n
\,[EssentialTo(p_i,G)\land IntegratedIn(p_i,G)]
\land RoleDistinct(p_i,p_j)
\text{ for some }i\neq j.
\]

The known roles include subjectivity, modal apprehension, reason-guided settling, actuality-complete knowledge, existential grounding, and direct causal capacity. “Role-distinct” is deliberately weaker than “separable metaphysical part” and stronger than “merely different human words.” The exact ontology of powers—property, trope, formal aspect, or way of acting—remains open.

The preferred constitution thesis is therefore:

\[
UPSS(G)
\leftrightarrow
Substance_T(G)
\land Necessary(G)
\land Underived(G)
\land MCS(G)
\land PowerStructured(G).
\]

## Why this fits the existing architecture

1. **Ultimity:** no external substrate or constitutively prior ingredient explains $G$ more fundamentally than $G$.
2. **Unity:** one subject owns and integrates the powers; no partitioned center chooses, knows, or acts independently.
3. **Agency:** apprehension, reasons, will, and settlement remain functionally articulated rather than collapsed by identity stipulation.
4. **Non-spatiality:** powers need no spatial arrangement or extension merely because their roles differ.
5. **Contingency:** $G$ can have an essential capacity while contingently exercising it. A contingent atemporal creator–creation relation adds no temporal acquisition or loss.
6. **Epistemic modesty:** the model states the minimum constitution required by prior commitments without pretending to reveal an inaccessible internal mechanism.

## Comparison of constitution models

| Model | Main advantage | Main cost | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| absolute divine simplicity | maximal independence and unity; major classical precedent | requires strong identities and supporting act–potency and essence–existence premises not yet accepted | live, not derived |
| underived power-structured subject | preserves ultimity and unity while retaining intelligible agency roles | owes an ontology of integrated powers and an answer to causal-duplication worries | preferred |
| fundamental complex with real constituents | permits richer internal metaphysics | must explain why constituents are not prior and why exactly one subject results | live variant if constituents are inseparable and non-prior |
| divine material or quasi-material substance | offers apparent continuity with creation | must define non-spatial matter and still explain production of new material tokens | disfavored |
| external or co-fundamental substrate | directly supplies material input | replaces $G$ with a joint ultimate basis or reduces $G$ to an organizer | incompatible with current sole-ground preference unless the architecture is revised |

## From divine constitution to material effect

The causal question should be expressed as a requirement of **causal adequacy**, not material resemblance:

\[
CausallyAdequate(c,e)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists m\,[MaterialPart(m,c)\land MaterialPart(m,e)].
\]

The weaker and more general requirement is that the cause possess a power able to originate the relevant effect and determinate content for that exercise. For creation:

\[
AdequateFor(G,\mathcal C_0,S)
\leftrightarrow
Knows_M(G,S)
\land HasPower(G,P_{OEC})
\land CanSpecify(P_{OEC},S).
\]

$P_{OEC}$ is not generic agent causation. It is a specific existential power whose exercise terminates in a new bearer of powers rather than in a modification of a patient already present. The original explanatory move from contingent reality to a necessary actualizer already needs something of this kind. Making it explicit reveals the cost of the creator hypothesis rather than inventing an extra solution after the fact.

The strengthened production condition is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
OEC^+(G,\mathcal C_0,S)\quad\text{iff}\quad &
UPSS(G)\\
&\land Originates(G,a_C)\\
&\land ExerciseOf(a_C,P_{OEC})\\
&\land Specifies(a_C,S)\\
&\land a_C\Rightarrow_{causal}
[Exists(\mathcal C_0)\land Instantiates(\mathcal C_0,S)]\\
&\land Distinct(G,\mathcal C_0)\\
&\land \neg\exists m\,MaterialInput(m,a_C,\mathcal C_0)\\
&\land GroundsExistence(G,\mathcal C_0).
\end{aligned}
\]

The created material profile—spatial extension, fields, quantities, dispositions, or whatever physics ultimately requires—belongs intrinsically to $\mathcal C_0$ by its instantiation of $S$. It is not transferred from $G$. Divine knowledge supplies specification but is not itself efficient production; $P_{OEC}$ and its exercise supply the productive relation.

## What kind of explanation this is

$OEC^+$ gives source, power, act, specification, distinct effect, lack of patient, and asymmetric dependence. It does not give a mechanism composed of earlier material states. Requiring such a mechanism would assume that originative causation must be one more process inside the order whose existence it explains.

This is a legitimate stopping point only if fundamental powers are admissible elsewhere in the ontology. They already are: the project treats agentive sourcehood and the modal powers encoded by $S$ as irreducible rather than as abbreviations for exceptionless event regularities. $P_{OEC}$ is a stronger and more unusual power, but not a new explanatory grammar.

The remaining ignorance should be explicit:

> We can state what would make originative production coherent and how it differs from transformation. We do not possess an independent model showing how an existential power operates beneath the level of its exercise.

That limitation prevents a coherence model from being advertised as a demonstration of metaphysical possibility.

## Two material-causality principles

S17 used one label, PMC, for claims of different strength. Split them:

\[
PMC_T:
MaterialTransformation(e)
\Rightarrow
\exists m\,MaterialPatient(m,e),
\]

\[
PMC_U:
ConcreteCausedExistence(e)
\Rightarrow
\exists m\,MaterialCause(m,e).
\]

$PMC_T$ is accepted: transformation analytically or metaphysically presupposes something transformed. $PMC_U$ is not accepted. Its evidential support comes predominantly from transformations and generations within an existing material order, so it cannot be extended to all concrete origination without an additional modal argument.

The maxim *ex nihilo nihil fit* can mean:

\[
\neg\exists c\,Cause(c,e)
\Rightarrow
\neg Originates(e).
\]

$OEC^+$ respects that principle because $G$ is a cause. Adding “and every cause requires material input” yields $PMC_U$ and therefore adds the disputed premise. “Nothing” is neither cause nor material in the preferred account; *ex nihilo* denies a prior patient.

$PMC_U$ remains a live defeater if independently established:

\[
\Box PMC_U
\Rightarrow
\neg\Diamond OEC^+(G,\mathcal C_0,S).
\]

Its present status moves from a symmetrical open option to **unestablished and disfavored as a universal principle**. The project has no proof that it is false.

## Why not creation *ex deo*?

Creation *ex deo* offers genuine token continuity only if some divine reality constitutes, persists in, or becomes the created effect. Mere “virtual containment”—$G$'s power and specification of the effect—is not material continuity.

An *ex deo* model can preserve divine freedom by treating the world as a contingent manifestation or mode, so it is not contradictory. It nevertheless incurs several connected costs:

1. created matter becomes a part, mode, or ontological overlap of $G$;
2. the strong substantial distinction between creator and creation is weakened;
3. contingent privations, suffering, and agency occur within a divine constituent or mode;
4. divine non-spatiality must be reconciled with a spatial divine manifestation;
5. creaturely sourcehood must remain non-trivial inside the larger divine reality;
6. “production” may collapse into contingent self-modification or manifestation.

These costs may be accepted by panentheist, emanationist, or qualified non-dualist models. Nothing currently gained over $OEC^+$ requires them, so *ex deo* remains live but disfavored.

## Why not co-fundamental matter?

An uncreated substrate supplies material continuity cleanly:

\[
G+M_0\rightarrow\mathcal C_0.
\]

But $M_0$ then exists independently of $G$, and the ultimate basis is at least $\langle G,M_0\rangle$. $G$ becomes a demiurgic organizer or co-ground rather than the sole existential ground preferred in C04–C05 and C80–C81. This is a coherent fallback if OEC fails, but it is a revision of the project rather than an interpretation of the same architecture.

## Adversarial checks

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Distinct powers are parts, so $MCS$ is false | Only if every real distinction is constitutive priority. The project leaves the ontology of powers open and requires integration and non-priority, not verbal identity. |
| $UPSS$ is just a bundle with a name | A bundle is insufficient; $Substance_T$ makes the subject the identity and agency unit. A further bare substrate is not added. |
| A cause cannot give what it does not possess | The cause must possess adequate power and determinate specification, not the effect's material token. Same-token possession is precisely the disputed PMC claim. |
| $P_{OEC}$ says only that $G$ can create | Correct as a fundamental modal posit; $OEC^+$ adds its exercise and truth conditions but does not reduce the power to something more familiar. Its warrant is the explanatory role of the creator hypothesis. |
| No mechanism means no explanation | It limits the depth of explanation. Fundamental powers terminate rather than eliminate every regress; whether this terminus is satisfactory is part of the global abductive comparison. |
| A contingent creative act adds an accidental part to $G$ | An act-relation can vary across possible actualities without temporal change or becoming a constitutive ingredient. Stronger simplicity accounts owe a separate treatment. |
| A formal model proves creation possible | Rejected. It shows consistency relative to the stated predicates unless $PMC_U$ or another principle is added; metaphysical possibility remains substantive. |

| Use “substance” in the thin individual-subject sense | preferred | It identifies a bearer and source without importing material stuff or a bare substratum |
| Prefer minimal constitutive simplicity $MCS(G)$ | preferred | It excludes prior ingredients and external substrate while preserving intelligible internal articulation |
| Adopt absolute divine simplicity | not derived | Its stronger identity claims require premises absent from the project |
| Treat $G$ as an underived power-structured subject | preferred at F2 | It best integrates ultimity, unity, agency, non-spatiality, and contingent exercise |
| Require a material cause to share material tokens with every material effect | rejected as a default | Causal adequacy does not entail material inheritance without $PMC_U$ |
| Strengthen OEC to $OEC^+$ with explicit constitution and power | preferred at F2 | It exposes rather than conceals the fundamental existential-power commitment |
| Claim a deeper creation mechanism | rejected as currently unavailable | The account terminates in a fundamental exercise rather than an intraworld process |
| Accept $PMC_T$ | accepted | Material transformation requires something transformed |
| Accept universal $PMC_U$ | unestablished/disfavored | Intraworld transformation does not establish universal modal scope; it remains a possible defeater |
| Prefer creation *ex deo* | no; live but disfavored | It gains continuity through divine–world overlap at substantial architectural cost |
| Adopt co-fundamental matter | rejected relative to the current architecture | It replaces sole divine ultimity with a joint basis; retain only as fallback if OEC fails |
| Treat formal consistency as proof of metaphysical possibility | rejected | The model is a coherence and comparative result |

## Literature and novelty notes

- [Aristotle, *Categories*, chapter 5](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Works_of_Aristotle/Categories) is precedent for treating primary substance as an individual subject of predication. $Substance_T$ uses only a thin analogue and does not inherit Aristotle's full category theory.
- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 3](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1003.htm) develops far stronger simplicity through matter–form, essence–existence, subject–accident, and act–potency exclusions. Those arguments show what $MCS$ does not yet establish.
- [Jeffrey Brower, “Making Sense of Divine Simplicity”](https://place.asburyseminary.edu/faithandphilosophy/vol25/iss1/1/) reconstructs divine simplicity through contemporary truthmaker and constituent ontology, confirming that the relation between subject, nature, properties, and truthmakers requires a substantive model.
- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 45](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1045.htm) distinguishes creation of being from transformation and treats “from nothing” as denial of a material cause rather than use of nothing as material.
- [Daniel Soars, “Creation in Aquinas: *ex nihilo* or *ex deo*?”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4A5826AE8894EC8AB283E83F1D2DA747/S002842890000144Xa.pdf/creation-in-aquinas-ex-nihilo-or-ex-deo.pdf) argues that virtual pre-containment and emanation language blur the usual opposition. The project responds by reserving *ex deo* for token constitution or ontological overlap, not mere causal power.
- Recent [grounding-panentheist work](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/grounding-panentheism/21D7EB3F1DD9DAFF19106417558BF796) explicitly treats cosmic grounding as divine–world ontological overlap. This supports classifying overlap as an additional model rather than as an automatic implication of existential dependence.

Minimal constitutive simplicity, dispositional or power-based models, creation without material input, emanation, and panentheism all have extensive precedent. The local work is the typed combination of $UPSS$, $OEC^+$, $S$-specification, E+, qualified power, and asymmetric co-production. No novelty claim is made.
