# Identity, Individuation, and Persistence

**Session ID:** S45

**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Primary question:** What makes an embodied power-bearing subject numerically the same through change, and can the same individual exist again after a genuine interval of non-existence?

**Secondary applications:** consciousness, personhood, duplication, fission, divine transworld identity, self-copy, task eligibility, and strict *ex deo*

**Target depth:** F2 working identity theory; enough to resolve the current trunk dependencies without pretending to complete the general metaphysics of identity

## Outcome

The project adopts a two-level account:

1. **numerical identity is absolute and fundamental** rather than reducible to qualitative, material, psychological, or causal similarity;
2. **kind-sensitive persistence profiles** explain and constrain ordinary diachronic identity without replacing the numerical relation.

For actual human and animal subjects, the preferred profile is **anchored hylomorphic-organismic persistence**. The subject is one living embodied power-bearing continuant, individuated synchronically by its integrated token organization and persisting ordinarily through a non-branching immanent causal-organizational career. Matter, occurrent consciousness, memory, personality, and the exercise of agency may change while the bearer persists. Psychological continuity remains important evidence and partly constitutes narrative or agentive continuity, but it is neither necessary nor sufficient for numerical identity.

This makes the pre-conscious organism and the later conscious agent one numerical individual. “Person” may mark a capacity or phase of that subject rather than a second colocated substance. The exact threshold for personhood remains a semantic, developmental, and normative question; it no longer creates a second identity bearer by default.

Ordinary hylomorphic-organismic continuity does not by itself cross a genuine existence gap. The project therefore rejects the claim that reassembly, information-pattern recovery, memory duplication, or qualitative identity automatically restores the individual. It also rejects the stronger claim that intermittent existence is impossible. Within E+, a complete admissible history may contain one numerically identical individual's disconnected life-set. The identity assignment is a primitive non-qualitative fact constrained by kind, origin, non-branching, and essence; it is not manufactured by divine recognition or by calling a later duplicate the original.

Consequently, numerical recreation is now a **preferred metaphysical possibility at F2**, not merely an uninterpreted placeholder. It remains conditional at the task level: $G$ can recreate the same $x$ when the admissible history assigns one non-branching gappy career to $x$; $G$ can instead originate a matching $y\neq x$. Neither result follows from qualitative matching alone, and no actual resurrection is inferred.

## Five questions that must remain distinct

### Synchronic individuation

What makes there be one subject rather than none, several colocated subjects, or an arbitrary sum of parts at a time?

### Numerical identity

Is the entity at one location or time literally the same entity as another, expressed by $x=y$?

### Diachronic persistence

Which changes can one entity undergo while continuing to exist?

### Reidentification

What evidence or procedure justifies a finite knower in judging that a later entity is the earlier one?

### Survival or continuity of concern

Which later relations preserve memory, projects, agency, relationships, responsibility, or rational self-concern?

These questions interact but do not collapse. A perfect duplicate may be evidentially indistinguishable while numerically distinct. Severe amnesia may destroy much psychological continuity while the organism remains. Fission may preserve relations that matter while numerical identity fails because identity is one-to-one.

In ordinary language, **persistence** is the continuing temporal career of the same individual through changes such as growth, sleep, matter turnover, injury, and psychological development. It is not a second identity relation that joins temporal stages. Numerical identity states that the individual at the different times is one and the same; the persistence profile explains which changes belong to that one career; reidentification concerns the evidence by which another subject recognizes the career. These normally travel together during a life but can separate in duplication, fission, severe discontinuity, or recreation after non-existence.

## Absolute primitive numerical identity

Let $=_N$ denote numerical identity. It obeys the ordinary equivalence and substitutivity constraints:

\[
x=_N x,
\]

\[
x=_N y\rightarrow y=_N x,
\]

\[
x=_N y\land y=_N z\rightarrow x=_N z,
\]

and, where the predication is extensional and evaluated in the same respect:

\[
x=_N y\rightarrow [F(x)\leftrightarrow F(y)].
\]

The project does not adopt relative identity of the form “the same $F$ but not the same $G$.” Kind terms constrain persistence and predication; they do not create several incompatible numerical-identity relations.

Numerical identity is treated as fundamental because every proposed reduction presupposes the individuals whose qualitative, causal, material, or psychological relations are being compared. This does not make every identity claim brute in the same explanatory sense. Organization, origin, causal continuity, and kind explain why ordinary transformations are careers of one bearer rather than productions of another. They do not analyze $=_N$ into a weaker similarity relation.

Write:

\[
IdFact_H(x,t_1,t_2)
\]

for the fundamental fact that the subject existing as $x$ at $t_1$ is numerically the subject existing as $x$ at $t_2$ in complete history $H$. The notation records a fact in the history; it does not make identity depend on a knower's description.

## Kind-sensitive persistence without relative identity

Kinds determine which powers and forms of change belong to the career of a bearer. Define a persistence profile:

\[
PP_K(x,[t_1,t_2])=
\langle U_K,O_x,C_K,B_K,E_K\rangle,
\]

where:

- $U_K$ is synchronic unity appropriate to kind $K$;
- $O_x$ is the original token origin and individuation anchor;
- $C_K$ is the ordinary causal-organizational continuity profile;
- $B_K$ is the non-branching condition;
- $E_K$ is the independently established kind-essential profile.

$PP_K$ is neither a checklist for an observer nor a reductive definition of $x=_N y$. It states the constitutive burden for a proposed history of one $K$-bearer.

For ordinary continuous persistence:

\[
\begin{aligned}
ContPers_K(x,t_1,t_2):={}&
\forall t\in[t_1,t_2],ExistsAt(x,t)\\
&\land MaintainsUnity_K(x,[t_1,t_2])\\
&\land ImmanentCausalCareer_K(x,[t_1,t_2])\\
&\land NonBranching_K(x,[t_1,t_2])\\
&\land EssenceCompatible_K(x,[t_1,t_2]).
\end{aligned}
\]

In the ordinary domain, $ContPers_K$ grounds and warrants diachronic identity judgments. The formula is not asserted as necessary for every extraordinary case because its first line excludes intermittent existence by construction.

## Synchronic identity of embodied subjects

For an actual embodied subject $A$:

\[
EmbodiedUnit(A,t):=
LivingBearer(A,t)
\land IntegratedOrg(A,t)
\land UnifiedPowerProfile(A,t)
\land OperationalBoundary(A,t).
\]

`IntegratedOrg` is the token organization through which the material constituents form one self-maintaining and world-engaging whole. `UnifiedPowerProfile` gathers metabolic, sensorimotor, phenomenal, cognitive, and agentive capacities in the bearer that exercises them. `OperationalBoundary` is not merely a skin boundary; it concerns which processes are integrated into the continuing life and control of the whole.

The thin hylomorphic claim is:

\[
EmbodiedUnit(A,t)\rightarrow OneSubstantialBearer(A,t).
\]

The form is not another occupant inside the body. It is the concrete organizing principle of this token living subject. Its parts, organs, neural processes, and conscious episodes act as powers, realizers, or states of the one bearer rather than as an automatic plurality of colocated agents.

This remains compatible with difficult biological cases. Conjoined organisms, distributed systems, artificial support, split-brain organization, and colonies require case-specific boundary analysis. “Maximal integration” is not treated as a numerical metric that settles every edge case without interpretation.

## The preferred human persistence profile

The working view is a moderate hylomorphic animalism:

\[
HumanSubject(A)\rightarrow
LivingOrganismicContinuant(A)
\land EmbodiedPowerBearer(A).
\]

It agrees with animalism that the thinking being here is the living human animal rather than a distinct psychological object sharing its place. It differs from a purely biological description by treating real organization and the integrated power profile as ontologically explanatory, including phenomenal and agentive powers not reduced to an inventory of matter.

### Matter turnover

Exact material sameness is neither necessary nor sufficient:

\[
SameMatter(x,t_1,t_2)\not\Leftarrow ContPers_K(x,t_1,t_2),
\]

and:

\[
SameMatterProfile(x,y)\not\Rightarrow x=_N y.
\]

An organism can exchange matter while its immanent organizational career continues. Reassembling earlier matter can produce a duplicate because the earlier career is not thereby resumed.

### Consciousness and memory

Occurrent consciousness is not required at every moment:

\[
ContPers_{Human}(A,t_1,t_2)
\not\Rightarrow
\forall t\in[t_1,t_2],EpisodeCon(A,t).
\]

Sleep, anesthesia, coma, infancy, and other non-conscious intervals do not by themselves terminate the bearer. Memories and character can be lost, distorted, or duplicated. They are major components of practical, narrative, and relational survival, but not the numerical identity-maker.

### Personhood as status or phase

The same individual may acquire or lose the capacities picked out by “person”:

\[
SameSubject(A,t_1,t_2)
\land \neg PersonCap(A,t_1)
\land PersonCap(A,t_2).
\]

This avoids postulating a new colocated entity when consciousness first appears. Whether “person” should be predicated before the capacity is realized—because of an intrinsic developmental potential, present capacity, or another criterion—remains open. Numerical identity of the subject does not wait for that semantic or normative decision.

### Brain and organism

The brain is a central realizer and controller in actual human agentive organization, not automatically a second subject. On the preferred organismic reading, moving a cerebrum normally moves an organ between organisms rather than moving the whole original animal. A case in which the complete integrated life and control career genuinely migrated rather than merely transferring information would require further analysis; the word “transplant” cannot decide the ontology.

This is a conscious divergence from psychological-continuity intuitions. The project prefers one embodied bearer and pays the cost that a psychologically continuous recipient need not be numerically the original organism.

## Why psychological and information accounts are insufficient

Let:

\[
PsychCont(x,y)
\]

cover memory, intention, character, belief, and first-personal continuity, and let:

\[
InfoMatch(x,y)
\]

cover structural or computational equivalence. Then:

\[
PsychCont(x,y)\not\Rightarrow x=_N y,
\]

\[
InfoMatch(x,y)\not\Rightarrow x=_N y.
\]

Both relations can branch. Two successors can inherit the same memories, dispositions, and information pattern, while numerical identity cannot relate the original to both without violating transitivity and the distinctness of the successors.

The reverse implications also fail as universal claims. A numerically persisting subject may undergo severe psychological discontinuity or informational change. What survives in such cases may be thin from the standpoint of projects and responsibility, but it remains the same bearer.

## Fission and closest continuers

Suppose:

\[
PsychCont(x,y)\land PsychCont(x,z)\land y\neq z.
\]

Then:

\[
\neg(x=_N y\land x=_N z).
\]

The preferred rule is:

- where one branch uniquely preserves the immanent organismic career, it may be the continuation of $x$;
- where two branches are perfectly symmetric and no independent identity fact distinguishes them, the original career terminates and the successors are distinct, strongly related continuers;
- the mere existence of a better competitor cannot retroactively change an otherwise intrinsic identity fact.

Closest-continuity can be evidence for identity but is not the constitutive relation. This protects symmetry and transitivity and prevents a remote duplicate from changing whether the local bearer survived.

## Intermittent existence

Let $J_1$ and $J_2$ be disjoint intervals:

\[
Life_H(x)=J_1\cup J_2,
\qquad \sup J_1<\inf J_2.
\]

The absence interval is genuine:

\[
\forall t\in(\sup J_1,\inf J_2)\;\neg ExistsAt(x,t).
\]

E+ already represents complete histories and does not require every life-set to be connected. A gappy history therefore introduces no meta-temporal disappearance from and re-entry into the set of actual truths. It represents one complete fact pattern in which $x$ exists at the earlier and later intervals and not between them.

### Gap-admissibility profile

Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
GapAdm_K(x,J_1,J_2):={}&
IdFact_H(x,J_1,J_2)\\
&\land SameOriginAnchor(x,J_1,J_2)\\
&\land EssenceCompatible_K(x,J_1,J_2)\\
&\land NonBranching_K(x,J_1,J_2)\\
&\land OneGroundedCareer_H(x,J_1\cup J_2).
\end{aligned}
\]

`IdFact_H` is explicit because no qualitative surrogate entails it. `SameOriginAnchor` blocks treating an independently originated duplicate as the original merely because it matches. `NonBranching_K` ensures that no two distinct simultaneous successors are each $x$. `OneGroundedCareer_H` integrates both temporal segments into one complete created relatum of the atemporal grounding relation.

The revised identity condition is:

\[
GapIdentity_C(x,J_1,J_2):=
GapAdm_K(x,J_1,J_2)
\land \neg ExistsBetween(x,J_1,J_2).
\]

This definition does not reduce identity; it states the primitive identity fact and its admissibility constraints. That is a deliberate stopping point, not a concealed qualitative criterion.

### Why this is not divine fiat

The order of explanation is:

\[
GapAdm_K(x,J_1,J_2)
\rightarrow Poss_C^{ind}(GappyCareer(x,J_1,J_2)),
\]

then:

\[
CanNumRecreate(G,x,J_1,J_2)
\]

through the already supported $CC_G^{ann}$, $HI_G^{elig}$, and $P_{OEC}$ architecture.

$G$ does not first create a qualitative duplicate and then declare it to be $x$. The eligible action is intentionally to restore the existence of the already numerically specified $x$ in a history whose identity profile is independently admissible. A different task can originate $y\neq x$ with the same qualitative properties.

Divine knowledge also tracks rather than creates the identity fact. ACO knows which career is actual; MKO knows admissible de re continuations of an actual $x$. Merely possible subjects that have never been actual remain represented initially by qualitative types rather than by pre-actual creaturely identities.

### Why no persisting carrier is required

The theory does not posit a soul, atom, information token, or hidden substance that exists during the gap. If such a carrier persists, the carrier's life has no gap and the original problem has been transformed into the persistence of that carrier.

The preferred primitive-identity account instead permits $x$ to have a disconnected life-set. This is metaphysically more austere but admits a fundamental identity fact. A subsistent-form or soul model remains a coherent alternative if later work independently supports such a bearer; it is not added solely to solve recreation.

### Modal status

The project now prefers:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(\exists x,J_1,J_2\;GapIdentity_C(x,J_1,J_2))
\]

at F2. The support is abductive rather than demonstrative:

1. numerical identity is not reducible to a continuity checklist;
2. no established human essence includes continuous temporal existence;
3. E+ already admits disconnected life-sets as complete-history profiles;
4. the origin, essence, and non-branching constraints eliminate the obvious duplicate and fission failures;
5. token-specific existential origination is already required when $G$ creates any actual individual for the first time.

The fifth point is an analogy, not a proof. Creating $x$ originally required no earlier material or psychological continuity with $x$. If the same de re identity is admissible after a gap, restoring $x$ need not require such continuity either. The remaining primitiveness is located in numerical identity rather than hidden in divine power.

## Recreation, reconstruction, and multiple successors

Numerical recreation is:

\[
NumRecreate(G,x,J_1,J_2):=
GapIdentity_C(x,J_1,J_2)
\land DirectlyRestoresExistence(G,x,J_2).
\]

Duplicate reconstruction is:

\[
DupRecon(G,x,y,t_2):=
QualMatch(y,x)\land y\neq x\land Originates(G,y,t_2).
\]

Both are possible action types. Recreating $x$ is not necessary whenever $G$ produces a matching later bearer. The task may specify $x$, a new $y$, or both. If $x$ and $y$ coexist later, only $x$ continues the original career; qualitative symmetry does not collapse them.

A task requiring two numerically distinct later bearers each to be identical with $x$ is incoherent. A task requiring one recreated $x$ and one matching $y$ is coherent if the identity profile independently distinguishes their careers.

## Divine identity

$G$ is not a temporal organism and does not use $PP_{Human}$. Its identity profile is:

\[
PP_G=\langle
UnderivedSubject,
NecessaryExistence,
UnifiedAgency,
UPSS,
EssentialPowerProfile
\rangle.
\]

These features constrain de re modal predication but do not reduce $G$'s numerical identity to a qualitative description. Two qualitatively indiscernible candidates would not thereby be one individual; the project's uniqueness arguments must still exclude a second ultimate subject.

The possible-history semantics treats alternatives as ways the same $G$ could exercise or refrain from exercising its powers:

\[
SameEssentialAgent(H_i,G).
\]

This is de re numerical identity anchored in the actual necessary subject, not resemblance between world-bound divine duplicates. It therefore introduces no pre-actual truth about creaturely choices.

### Self-copy

If $G$ creates $G'$:

\[
CreatedBy(G,G')\rightarrow G'\neq G
\land Depends(G',G).
\]

Even exact qualitative matching would not make $G'=G$. Dependence and created origin also prevent $G'$ from sharing the independently essential underived profile. A dependent analogue may still be immensely powerful; it is not a second instance of the same ultimate identity.

### Self-reconstitution

An action can change contingent relations or exercise powers while preserving $G$. A description in which the same $G$ loses necessary existence, underivedness, or unified subjecthood fails because its result removes the identity profile of the agent to whom completion is attributed. This application now rests on $PP_G$, not on an omnipotence-saving stipulation.

### Strict *ex deo*

Identity theory narrows but does not close strict non-destructive *ex deo*. Constituting a created effect does not automatically divide or destroy $G$. A viable overlap model must preserve one $PP_G$ subject, avoid turning an essential divine constituent into a detachable or depleted part, and individuate the created relatum as dependent and numerically distinct. Whether such a relation is coherent remains a constitution problem rather than a numerical-identity problem alone.

## Adversarial tests

### Is primitive identity an evasion?

It would be if invoked instead of explaining ordinary persistence. Here organismic organization, origin, immanent causation, and non-branching explain the ordinary career and constrain extraordinary specifications. The irreducible remainder is the same numerical relation presupposed by every reductive proposal.

### Does the same origin guarantee identity?

No. Origin is a constraint and anchor, not a sufficient qualitative recipe. Multiple later structures may be causally related to one origin; identity remains one-to-one.

### Can God make a duplicate be the original by deciding so?

No. The eligible histories already distinguish numerical recreation from duplicate production. Divine action actualizes or grounds the bearer described; it does not make $x=y$ true after independently producing $x\neq y$.

### Does E+ make a gap disappear?

No. Tenseless actuality of the complete career does not imply existence at every created time. $x$ is absent throughout the open interval even though the complete history contains both life segments.

### Does a primitive gappy career contradict the hylomorphic account?

No, provided hylomorphic organization explains each embodied interval and is not asserted to be the reductive identity-maker across every possible case. The token form does not float through the gap; the one individual has two intervals of embodied realization.

### Could a perfect copy and the recreated original be observationally indistinguishable?

Yes. Reidentification evidence could be insufficient even while ACO tracks a determinate identity fact. Epistemic indistinguishability does not entail ontological identity or indeterminacy.

### Does the view settle every brain-transplant case?

No. It sets the burden: identify the continuing integrated organismic career rather than assuming that memory, matter, or a named organ decides the result. Highly artificial cases may remain underdescribed.

### Is personal identity vague?

The present model treats numerical identity in a complete history as determinate. Application of kind terms, personhood thresholds, and finite reidentification judgments may be vague or epistemically indeterminate without making $x=_N y$ itself degree-valued.

## Literature and precedent

- [Eric Olson, “An Argument for Animalism”](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/734/4/Argument_animalism_fixed.pdf) argues that the thinking being in one's location is the human animal and that psychological continuity does not suffice for organismic identity. This supports the one embodied bearer and exposes the cost of following a transplanted cerebrum.
- [William Jaworski, “Hylomorphism and the Metaphysics of Structure”](https://media.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/assets/pdf_file/0014/40190/Res_Philosophica.pdf) treats structure as a real power-conferring principle relevant to the persistence and capacities of organized individuals. The project retains a thinner hylomorphic organization without importing a subsistent rational soul.
- [Derek Parfit, “Personal Identity”](https://www.uvm.edu/~lderosse/courses/metaph/Parfit%281971%29.pdf) uses division and branching to distinguish numerical identity from psychological relations that may preserve what matters. The project keeps the distinction but does not reduce identity to psychological continuity.
- [Trenton Merricks, “How to Live Forever Without Saving Your Soul”](https://www.trentonmerricks.com/public/publications/how-to-live-forever-without-saving-your-soul.pdf) argues that matter and psychology are neither adequate criteria for identity across a gap and defends primitive numerical identity and the epistemic possibility of intermittent existence. This is the closest checked precedent for the project's gap conclusion.
- [David Hershenov, “The Metaphysical Problem of Intermittent Existence and the Possibility of Resurrection”](https://doi.org/10.5840/faithphil200320127) directly argues that resurrection after non-existence need not violate mereological or biological principles. The present account is less committed to a reductive biological criterion.
- [Lynne Rudder Baker, “Persons and the Metaphysics of Resurrection”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/abs/persons-and-the-metaphysics-of-resurrection/9519CB04F4659AE384A0B42B9AF880F1) makes same-person embodiment an adequacy condition and develops a constitution/first-person-perspective alternative. It remains a live competitor but is not selected because the project prefers one embodied bearer.
- [Dean Zimmerman, “The Compatibility of Materialism and Survival: The ‘Falling Elevator’ Model”](https://doi.org/10.5840/faithphil199916220) shows the pressure to preserve immanent-causal continuity in material resurrection models. The project uses that pressure for ordinary persistence but does not make uninterrupted causation necessary for every admissible history.

Primitive numerical identity, animalism, hylomorphism, gappy existence, and resurrection models all have clear precedent. The potentially useful synthesis is their integration with E+, disconnected life-sets, thin hylomorphic powers, token-specific $OEC$, ACO/MKO, non-branching source roles, and the intervention taxonomy. No novelty claim is warranted before deeper comparison with endurance, perdurance, origin essentialism, haecceitism, and non-criterialist identity.
