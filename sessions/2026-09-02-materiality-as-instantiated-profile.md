## Starting position

S30 prefers $G$ as an underived power-structured subject ($UPSS$) and strengthens creation to $OEC^+$; S31 adds constitutive disjointness ($CCD$) and types *ex deo* into four senses. Both sessions stop at coherence: $OEC^+$ is consistent relative to its predicates, $PMC_U$ is unestablished and disfavored but remains a live defeater (C235), and non-destructive strict *ex deo* is neither shown coherent nor incoherent (C269). The ledger records the residue as open questions 5 and 6.

The session claims that the residue is smaller than S30–S31 left it, for a reason those sessions did not use: the ontology already contains an account of what it is for something to be material, and that account, together with the stopping-point analysis of S44, does most of the remaining work.

## Step 1: what matter is in this architecture

C07 and C34 make $S$ the specification of laws, constants, boundaries, and primitives; C114 gives physical features derivative explanation through $S$; C241 says $G$ is not a member of the physical order under $S_{phys}$. Nowhere does the ontology treat matter as a stuff. What it has is a **profile**: to be material is to instantiate what $S_{phys}$ specifies for material bearers.

\[
Mat(x)\;\leftrightarrow\;Instantiates(x,\Pi_{phys}).
\]

Under C183–C184 the profile account is at least as faithful to physics as the stuff conception: contemporary physics describes particles as excitations of fields, treats mass and energy as interconvertible, derives conservation laws from symmetries of the dynamics, and nowhere posits a transferable substrate that persists through every transaction. This is not a proof that there is no stuff; it is a bridge showing that the stuff conception is not what the empirical description supports, so the ontology may adopt the profile account without an empirical defeater.

The consequence for CH12 is immediate. The question “how can a non-material cause produce matter?” presupposes that matter is something a cause must have in order to hand it on. Under the profile account the question becomes “how can a cause make something instantiate $\Pi_{phys}$?”, and C229–C231 already answer it: adequacy is power plus specification, and the profile belongs to the effect through its instantiation of $S$. The cross-category worry does not disappear by fiat; it is re-described as the worry that instantiation of a profile requires a prior instance of the same profile, which is exactly $PMC_U$.

## Step 2: the level argument against $PMC_U$

$PMC_U$ says that every concrete caused existence requires a material cause. S30 observed that its evidential support comes from intraworld transformations. The present step says why that support cannot be extended.

Conservation laws are consequences of $S_{phys}$:

\[
S_{phys}\models Conserv(S).
\]

They therefore hold for $H(S)$-internal processes. The origination of $\mathcal C_0$ is not $H(S)$-internal: it is the instantiation of $S$ itself, creation as $I_0$, prior in grounding order to any history $S$ admits. A principle whose warrant is $Conserv(S)$ cannot govern the event that makes $S$ obtain without $S$ governing its own origination:

\[
\big(S_{phys}\models Conserv(S)\big)\land\neg H(S)\text{-internal}(I_0)
\;\Rightarrow\;
Conserv(S)\ \text{does not constrain}\ I_0 .
\]

This is a level distinction, not a claim that conservation fails. Within the created order it holds without exception the ontology needs to deny. The point is that $PMC_U$, applied to origination, quantifies over a process outside the domain in which its only evidence lives.

What remains for $PMC_U$ is an a priori route: the intuition that nothing concrete can begin without material input. C236 has already separated that intuition from *ex nihilo nihil fit* read as denial of uncaused origination. The residual intuition is precisely the stuff conception restated modally. Under the project's method it is a comparative cost to be weighed (WEP, C403), not a premise, and R146-style reasoning applies: inability to imagine profile-instantiation without a prior instance does not establish its impossibility.

Result: $PMC_U$ moves from “unestablished and disfavored” to **“disfavored with no evidential route identified.”** It remains a defeater if someone supplies an independent argument; the session records that none of the routes considered by S30–S31 survives the level distinction.

## Step 3: $OEC^+$ as a consequence, not only a coherent model

Open question 6 asks whether $OEC^+$ can receive support beyond formal coherence and abductive integration. It can, from commitments already preferred:

1. **Sole ultimity.** There is exactly one necessary ultimate ground, $G$ (C04–C05, C80–C81).
2. **Contingency of the material order.** $S$ is one of several eligible structures (C34, C108); its constants, laws, and primitives could have differed, and creation could have failed to occur (C111). Hence the material order, as specified by $S_{phys}$, is contingent: $Cont(\mathcal C_0)$.
3. **Necessitation lemma and stopping points.** Every contingent item requires a ground, and the ground of the contingent order is $G$ (C404–C406).
4. **Physical immateriality.** $G$ is not a member of the physical order and does not instantiate $\Pi_{phys}$ (C84–C87, C241): $\neg Mat(G)$.

From 1–4: the origination of the material order has a non-material ground. Under $CCD$ (C248) that grounding involves no divine constituent in the effect; the effect's materiality is its own instantiation of $\Pi_{phys}$; there is no material cause of $\mathcal C_0$. Therefore:

\[
\text{(1)–(4)}\land CCD(G,\mathcal C_0)\;\Rightarrow\;\neg PMC_U .
\]

Without $CCD$, a strict *ex deo* model could try to satisfy $PMC_U$ only if the divine token that constitutes the effect is itself material, which contradicts 4. So the conclusion is robust across the $CCD$ / $ED_S$ fork: given 1–4, $PMC_U$ is false unless $G$ is material. The only escape is to deny 2 by making matter necessary, and a necessary material basis is co-fundamental matter, already rejected relative to the architecture (C238) and, by S44, a second impersonal stopping point subject to the necessitation dilemma.

The dilemma is therefore:

> Matter is either contingent or necessary. If contingent, its origination has a non-material ground and $PMC_U$ fails. If necessary, it is co-fundamental and sole ultimity fails.

$OEC^+$ is thus not merely coherent: it is entailed by the conjunction of sole ultimity, the contingency of matter, physical immateriality, and $CCD$. Its evidential status rises to that of the weakest of these, which is the preferred rather than accepted level. One who holds $PMC_U$ must reject one of the four, and the ledger now says which rejection each objector is making.

## Step 4: integrated powers are aspects, not parts

R41 rejects the inference from distinguishable powers to prior parts, but C225 leaves the ontology of powers open, and the parts objection returns in a sharper form: if the powers are real and distinct, what stops them being constituents? The session supplies the criterion S30 lacked:

\[
Aspect(p,G)\;\leftrightarrow\;
Essential(p,G)
\land\neg\Diamond\big(Exists(p)\land\neg Exists(G)\big)
\land\neg Grounds\big(p,Identity(G)\big)
\land\exists m\,[Manif(p)=m].
\]

An ingredient (S30) is a constituent that is constitutively prior. Aspects fail priority on two independent counts: they cannot exist without their subject, and they do not ground its identity. They are individuated by manifestation-type — what exercising them does — not by being separable items. So $RoleDistinct(p_i,p_j)$ is satisfied by aspects and $MCS(G)$ is preserved. Whichever ontology of powers the project later adopts (property, trope, formal aspect, way of acting) must satisfy the four conditions; that is a constraint, not a stipulation of the answer.

The causal-duplication worry has the same shape as the one S29 resolved for created agents, and the same answer applies. C220 says that agent-level and subsystem-level causal descriptions are not rival sufficient causes; extended to $G$, the subject acts, and “the power produces” describes the same exercise at aspect level. A power is not a second cause beside the subject whose way of acting it is.

## Step 5: the non-inheritance condition for strict *ex deo*

Open question 5 asks whether a non-destructive strict *ex deo* manifestation is coherent with $UPSS$. The profile account makes the test exact. Let $d$ be a numerically divine token that constitutes, persists in, or becomes a material bearer $y$ (C247). Either:

- **Case A:** $d$ instantiates $\Pi_{phys}$. Then $Mat(d)$, and $d$ is a physical member under $S_{phys}$. If $d$ is a mode or constituent of $G$, then unless the profile fails to transfer upward, $G$ has a material constituent and C241 and $ASP_G$ fail.
- **Case B:** $d$ does not instantiate $\Pi_{phys}$ while $y$ does. Then $d$'s contribution to $y$ is not material constitution; if $d$ merely causes or specifies $y$, this is $OEC^+$ under another name (C246), and if $d$ persists in $y$ without bearing the profile, it is the whole-presence variant (C251).

Both non-trivial cases require the same principle:

\[
NI:\quad \big(ModeOf(y,G)\lor ConstituentOf(d,G)\big)\land Mat(y\text{ or }d)\;\not\Rightarrow\;Mat(G).
\]

$NI$ is coherent as a formula, but it is unmotivated. The reason to describe something as a mode of a subject is that it is a way the subject is; the classic strict *ex deo* system (Spinoza) accepts inheritance and therefore attributes extension to the substance, which the project has excluded in S14. A model that keeps $ASP_G$ and $CT$ while positing material modes must explain why the profile stops at the mode, and no current commitment supplies that explanation.

Result for open question 5: a non-destructive strict *ex deo* manifestation is coherent with $UPSS$, $CT$, and $ASP_G$ **only under $NI$**, and $NI$ is unmotivated within the project. C254 keeps $ED_S$ open by the author's decision; the session does not close it, it states its price. C269's conditional inclusion under $OMP_Q$ inherits the same condition.

## Step 7: created subjects and the identity criterion

$Substance_T$ (C223) applies to created subjects: an identity unit that bears powers. A created subject's material profile is instantiated by its realization base $RB_A$ (S29), not by the subject's identity unit as such. This does not select a realization model, but it gives CH21 a sharper statement: re-realization (S47) would be re-instantiation of $\Pi_{phys}$ by the same identity unit, and the open question is what individuates that unit across a gap. The session records the pointer and does not decide it.

## Literature and novelty notes

Cited from memory; not verified online in this session.

- Field-theoretic descriptions of matter (particles as field excitations; Noether's theorem linking conservation laws to symmetries) supply the empirical bridge for the profile account; a physics reference should be added in the literature pass.
- Spinoza, *Ethics* I, on extension as an attribute of the one substance: the classic strict *ex deo* system that accepts mode-to-substance inheritance and therefore denies what $ASP_G$ asserts.
- Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 45, on creation as denial of a material cause; q. 3 on simplicity, which the aspect criterion approaches without adopting.
- Contemporary dispositional and power ontologies (Molnar, *Powers*, 2003; Mumford and Anjum, *Getting Causes from Powers*, 2011) for the individuation of powers by manifestation-type.
- Jeffrey Brower's truthmaker treatment of divine simplicity as the nearest contemporary comparison for aspects without constituent ontology.

Classification: the profile account, the level distinction for conservation, and the aspect criterion are established or adaptable materials; the project-specific results are the derivation of $\neg PMC_U$ from sole ultimity, contingency of matter, immateriality, and $CCD$, and the identification of $NI$ as the exact price of non-destructive strict *ex deo*. No novelty claim.
