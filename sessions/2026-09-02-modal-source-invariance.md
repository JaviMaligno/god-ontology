# Session: Modal source invariance

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S39
**Primary question:** Can the actual uniqueness and ultimity of $G$ justify the claim that the same $G$ is the ultimate source of every possible created order, without defining creatorhood into the essence of $G$ or making creation necessary?
**Dependencies:** C04–C05, C73, C80–C83, C97–C111, C223–C280, C343–C357, CH05, S13, S30, S32–S33, S38
**Target depth:** F2 modal-source bridge; distinguish deduction, abductive extension, and remaining direct-power premises

## Result in one sentence

Actual one-ground ultimity does not deductively modalize itself, but the project provisionally prefers **conditional modal ultimity**—whenever a fundamental created basis exists, its ultimate origin terminates in the same necessary $G$—as the best F2 abductive extension of its one-ground architecture; this preserves worlds with no creation, corrects B2 by separating ultimate sourcehood from direct $OEC^+$, and leaves direct creative plenitude dependent on the essential-power, specification-transparency, and agent-accessibility premises.

The author's “DALE” accepts S38 at its proposed F2 status under the default-acceptance rule and authorizes this queued test.

## Why the actual claim does not modalize itself

The current uniqueness claim concerns the actual total contingent order $C^*$:

\[
U_G(C^*)\leftrightarrow \exists!g\,UltimateGround(g,C^*).
\]

Together with the necessary existence and underived constitution of $G$, this establishes at most:

\[
\Box E(G)\land Underived(G)\land UltimateGround(G,C^*).
\]

It does not entail:

\[
\Box\forall C\,[FundCreatedBasis(C)\rightarrow UltimateGround(G,C)].
\]

A countermodel can preserve necessary $G$ while assigning the ultimate-source role in another possible created order to a second source $L$, or treating that order as brute. The current premises make those models explanatorily costly; they do not yet make them contradictory.

This exposes three claims that must remain separate:

| Claim | Modal force | Current standing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $G$ grounds the actual created order | actual | part of C04–C05 and the preferred architecture |
| $G$ necessarily exists and possesses $P_{OEC}$ essentially | de re modal | preferred constitution of the agent, but says no created relatum must exist |
| every possible fundamental created order ultimately originates in $G$ | relational and transworld | substantive modal extension under examination |

Necessary existence alone does not make every relation of a necessary being essential. Creatorhood involves $G$ and a created relatum; in a no-creation world, there is no such relation to instantiate.

## Repairing the target

S33 formulated B2 through direct originative exercise:

\[
\Box\forall C\,[FundCreatedBasis(C)\rightarrow OEC^+(G,C,Spec(C))].
\]

That formula combines two questions:

1. **ultimate-source identity:** where the existential dependence of $C$ terminates;
2. **direct production:** whether $G$ immediately exercises $P_{OEC}$ with the full specification of $C$.

The first can hold even if $G$ creates or empowers a derivative source that immediately produces some later bearer. Ultimate grounding therefore does not by itself entail direct token production. B2 is repaired as **modal ultimate-source invariance**:

\[
MUS_G:=
\Box\forall C\,[FundCreatedBasis(C)\rightarrow UltimateOrigin(G,C)].
\]

For clarity, the present framework treats `FundCreatedBasis` as existence-entailing. An explicit conditional version is:

\[
CMU_G:=
\Box\forall C\,[(Exists(C)\land FundCreatedBasis(C))
\rightarrow UltimateOrigin(G,C)].
\]

$MUS_G$ and $CMU_G$ express the same intended restriction at this level. Neither says that a created order exists in every world.

Direct production needs an additional bridge:

\[
DirectBasis(C)
\land UltimateOrigin(G,C)
\land NoDelegatedOrigin(C)
\rightarrow OEC^+(G,C,Spec(C)).
\]

Whether every fundamental created basis satisfies `NoDelegatedOrigin` must be argued from the meaning of *fundamental basis* or from the production model; it is not supplied by uniqueness alone.

## The support structure

The modal thesis can be decomposed into three principles:

### MGC — Modal grounding coverage

\[
\Box\forall C\,[FundCreatedBasis(C)\rightarrow
\exists x\,(UnderivedConcreteGround(x)\land UltimateGround(x,C))].
\]

Every instantiated fundamental created basis has an underived concrete ultimate existential ground. This is a modalized explanatory-demand principle. Without it, a possible brute created order remains open.

### MUU — Modal uniqueness of the underived concrete source

\[
\Box\forall x\,[UnderivedConcreteGround(x)\rightarrow x=G].
\]

No possible second underived concrete existential ground exists alongside $G$. This is stronger than actual $U_G$ and stronger than the claim that $G$ necessarily exists.

### GOL — Grounding–origin link

\[
UltimateGround(x,C)\land FundCreatedBasis(C)
\rightarrow UltimateOrigin(x,C).
\]

For a fundamental created basis, existential grounding must be connected to origination rather than treated as a causally idle explanatory label.

MGC, MUU, and GOL support $CMU_G$. The decomposition is valuable because each premise can fail independently. It also shows why simply calling $G$ “God” or “creator” would hide rather than solve the issue.

## Comparative modal configurations

| Possible configuration | Coherent relative to present logic? | Cost in the project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| no created order; necessary $G$ exists | yes | none; preserves contingent creation |
| a created order ultimately originates in $G$ | yes and preferred | continues actual explanatory architecture |
| a contingent source $L$ originates an order but itself depends on $G$ | yes | $L$ is a derivative source; not a rival to $CMU_G$ |
| a brute fundamental created order | not formally excluded | abandons modal grounding coverage in precisely the domain the ontology was built to explain |
| a second necessary underived source $L$ originates an order | not formally contradictory | multiplies concrete ultimates and reopens the partitioned, joint, and redundant-ground costs from S13 |
| $G$ is ultimate source through a derivative producer | yes | satisfies source invariance but does not witness direct creative plenitude |

The fourth and fifth rows are the live countermodels. The project prefers excluding them because they fragment its explanatory stopping point or permit the very brute contingency for which $G$ was introduced. That is an abductive judgment. A strict demonstration would require independently necessary MGC and MUU, which the project does not currently possess.

## Preferred F2 position

The project adopts $CMU_G$ provisionally as an **abductive modal extension**:

\[
\boxed{
\Box\forall C\,[(Exists(C)\land FundCreatedBasis(C))
\rightarrow UltimateOrigin(G,C)]
}
\]

The support is cumulative:

1. $G$ is already the preferred necessary, underived, unique ultimate ground of the actual total contingent order.
2. A brute fundamental created order would suspend the explanatory demand exactly where the current ontology applies it.
3. A second underived concrete origin would multiply ultimate stopping points without an independently established need.
4. A contingent or derivative producer does not threaten the thesis because its own existential contribution still terminates in $G$.
5. The same-source thesis unifies the modal family of created orders without identifying modal eligibility with divine power.

This is enough for a working F2 preference because the project does not require a demonstration of ultimate metaphysical choices. It is not enough to label the alternatives incoherent or to upgrade $CMU_G$ to an axiom beyond revision.

## Modal-collapse test

Conditional modal ultimity has the form:

\[
\Box(CreatedBasisExists\rightarrow UltimateOriginByG).
\]

It does not entail:

\[
\Box CreatedBasisExists,
\]

\[
\Box Creates(G,C^*),
\]

or:

\[
\exists C\,\Box Creates(G,C).
\]

Worlds with no creation remain admissible. Worlds containing different admissible specifications also remain admissible. What is invariant is the identity of the ultimate source when creation occurs, not the exercise, object, or result of divine choice.

This fits the project's distinction between essential power and contingent exercise:

\[
\Box P_{OEC}(G)
\not\Rightarrow
\Box\exists C\,Exercise(G,P_{OEC},C).
\]

## Effect on the S33 bridge

The repaired bridge now reads:

1. **B1:** $Poss_C^{ind}(S)$ through S38's constitutive admissibility;
2. **B2r:** $CMU_G$, the same ultimate source for any instantiated fundamental created basis;
3. **B2d:** a directness link from that basis to $G$'s $OEC^+$, where direct creation is required;
4. **B3:** $P_{OEC}$ belongs essentially to the same $G$;
5. **B4:** an admissible $S$ is agent-accessible through $G$'s own selection and exercise, with specification transparency.

B2r is now preferred at F2, but B2d cannot be obtained from source uniqueness alone. B3 identifies a stable power-bearer; B4 must show that the relevant admissible difference can arise through that agent's settlement. A weakened B2 stating merely that there is a world where $G$ creates each $S$ would be almost identical to $CanCreate(G,S)$ and would make the bridge circular.

The present result therefore advances the argument without completing $CP_G$. Its next exact task is to recombine B1, B2r, B3, and B4 and determine whether $P_{OEC}$ plus patient-independence and specification transparency supplies B2d rather than presupposing universal range.

## Adversarial tests

### “Another source is logically impossible”

No current axiom establishes that. S13 supplies an abductive case for actual uniqueness. Modal MUU is a stronger thesis and remains revisable if a coherent independently motivated plural-source architecture gains explanatory value.

### “A contingent demiurge disproves invariance”

Only if it is ultimate. A powerful created producer can originate an order proximately while depending existentially on $G$. The thesis concerns the terminus of origin, not monopoly over every intermediate causal role.

### “If $G$ is always the source, $G$ always creates”

The inference drops the conditional antecedent. Sourcehood is instantiated only in worlds containing creation. The essential item is $P_{OEC}$; its exercise remains contingent.

### “Ultimate source means direct manufacturer of every token”

That would collapse existential grounding, immediate production, conservation, secondary causation, and creaturely authorship. The typed causal architecture rejects the collapse.

### “Replace B2 with a direct ability premise”

That would shorten the formula by assuming its intended conclusion. The source principle is useful only if kept distinct from agent-relative ability and direct manifestation range.

## Literature and precedent

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 44](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1044.htm) argues from participated being to one first cause of all beings. Its route depends on doctrines of subsistent being, participation, and simplicity that the project has not adopted in full, but it is direct precedent for universal existential sourcehood.
- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 19, a. 3](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1019.htm) distinguishes God's necessary being and will from the non-absolute necessity of willing creatures. This supports the coherence of invariant source identity conditional on creation without necessary creation.
- [Ansari, Dunaway, and McGinnis, “Necessary existent theology”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/necessary-existent-theology/4A69981323DF739CEFC5D7901B86E991) explain that, on their reading of Avicenna, being first cause is a relational and derived fact rather than part of the divine essence. This independently motivates the project's refusal to infer transworld creator relations from necessary existence alone.
- [Partington, Vesga, and Nichols, “Is the cosmological argument intuitive?”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/is-the-cosmological-argument-intuitive/47AE3B7F622257DE4C2210463841EF0B) notes that even the possibility of brute facts weakens the explanatory demand behind a regress stopper. The project therefore records MGC as a substantive modal principle rather than treating it as free logic.

Universal divine sourcehood, contingent creation, modal PSR disputes, and the distinction between essential and relational divine claims are established. The project-specific contribution is the typed repair of B2 into conditional ultimate-source invariance plus a separate directness bridge, integrated with constitutive-admissibility actualism and agent-relative power semantics. This is a useful synthesis, not yet a general novelty claim.
