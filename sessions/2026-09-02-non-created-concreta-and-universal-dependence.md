# Session: Non-created concreta and universal dependence

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S73
**Primary question:** Is a concrete relatum distinct from and not created by $G$ metaphysically possible under the strongest justified uniqueness and ultimity theses, and which actions concerning it could threaten unrestricted $OMP_Q$?
**Dependencies:** C04–C05, C29–C34, C73–C83, C98–C100, C223–C231, C255–C265, C319, C343–C376, C420–C430, C573–C585; S13, S30, S32, S36, S39–S40, S44, S72
**Origin:** First trunk priority after S72; the author's parallel publication-protocol work is explicitly outside this session

**Later refinement (S77):** this session records the original S73 proposal and is retained as historical reasoning. S77 corrects its modal scope: the one-terminal argument principally supports actual concrete dependence $ACD_G$; full modal $UCD_G$ additionally requires modal total-ground and dependence/origin-essentiality bridges. Any S73 language of F2 closure should therefore be read through C634–C644 rather than as an independently completed derivation.
**Target depth:** F2 modal and task-domain audit; no claim of a premise-free demonstration

## Outcome

The apparent blocker becomes much narrower once *created* is kept in the project's established sense. Creation is ontological dependence on $G$, not temporal beginning and not only the actual contingent exercise $OEC^+$. A concrete that exists necessarily *through another* can therefore be created. Relative to C04's claim that $G$ grounds the total contingent reality, any concrete that is genuinely not created by $G$ must be necessary and existentially independent of $G$.

Actual ground uniqueness $U_G$, divine-ground uniqueness $U_D$, and conditional modal ultimity $CMU_G$ exclude a second source when it grounds a contingent order. They do not deductively exclude an independent necessary concrete that grounds no created order. The project therefore cannot derive the modal impossibility of every non-created concrete from those theses alone.

The strongest justified continuation is **universal concrete dependence**:

\[
UCD_G:=
\Box\forall x[(Concrete(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow DependsExistentially(x,G)].
\]

$UCD_G$ is proposed as an F2 abductive extension of the one-terminal-ground architecture. It is supported independently of omnipotence by explanatory unity, well-founded dependence, and the absence of any independently motivated second concrete stopping point. It is stronger than $CMU_G$, is not demonstrated, and is not unrestricted uniqueness of everything necessary. Necessary abstracta and necessary dependent concreta remain compatible with it.

If $UCD_G$ is accepted, no distinct non-created concrete is metaphysically possible within the preferred architecture. Rival models containing one remain intelligible descriptions of a different ultimate ontology rather than contradictions of logic. If $UCD_G$ is withheld, mere coexistence with such an $L$ still does not defeat $OMP_Q$. Creating an essentially uncreated $L$, annihilating a necessary $L$, or making an autonomous $L$ freely supply a settlement chosen by $G$ fails task eligibility. The genuine threat is a causally relevant $L$ that blocks an otherwise independently eligible divine contribution concerning it.

The non-created-relatum branch is therefore conditionally closed at F2 under $UCD_G$, while the exact consequence of rejecting that thesis is now known. Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ remains provisional until the separate possibility of independently motivated new action kinds is audited.

## 1. Fixing the scope of creation

For concrete relata, make explicit the convention already implicit in C73 and C319:

\[
Created_G(x):=
x\neq G\land DependsExistentially(x,G).
\]

`DependsExistentially` may be direct or terminate in $G$ through a well-founded derivative chain. It does not require that $x$ begin at a time:

\[
Created_G(x)\not\Rightarrow
\exists t\,BeginsToExist(x,t).
\]

It also does not require that every created item be the immediate terminus of the actual contingent root act $OEC^+$. That act is the project's selected account of the actual root created order. A hypothetical concrete that exists necessarily through $G$ would be a necessary creature in the broad dependence sense, although its production relation would not be the contingent exercise used for $\mathcal C_0$.

Define the target:

\[
NCC_G(L):=
Concrete(L)\land L\neq G\land \neg Created_G(L).
\]

This is a negative classification. It does not yet say whether $L$ is necessary, agentive, causally active, a ground, or capable of interacting with $G$.

## 2. The contingency lemma

Let $\mathcal C$ contain the total contingent concrete reality, not merely the physical universe selected in the actual creation model. Under C04 and C73:

\[
Concrete(L)\land Contingent(L)
\rightarrow DependsExistentially(L,G).
\]

Together with the definition above:

\[
NCC_G(L)\rightarrow Necessary(L).
\]

The conclusion is conditional on the working foundation thesis. A brute contingent $L$ is a coherent rival to C04; it is not a counterexample internal to C04. This prevents the audit from oscillating between accepting total contingent grounding and quietly placing a contingent exception outside its domain.

Necessary dependence also needs care. From $Necessary(L)$ it does not follow that $L$ is underived:

\[
\Box Exists(L)\land DependsExistentially(L,G)
\]

is coherent as a modal profile. Because creation is dependence, such an $L$ is created even if it has no temporal beginning and exists in every admissible world. Necessary existence *ab alio* and underived necessary existence are different statuses.

## 3. What present uniqueness does and does not exclude

The current theses have role-limited scopes:

| Thesis | Quantified role | What it excludes | What it leaves open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $U_G$ | ultimate ground of the actual total contingent order | two actual ultimate grounds of $\mathcal C$ | a necessary concrete with no grounding role in $\mathcal C$ |
| $U_D$ | necessary agent occupying that actual ground role | a second necessary agent that is also ultimate ground of $\mathcal C$ | a necessary agent outside that role |
| $CMU_G$ | ultimate origin of every possible fundamental created basis | a second source of a possible root-created order within the preferred modal architecture | an independent necessary concrete that originates no created basis |
| $MUU$ | underived concrete existential grounds | a second underived concrete source in that grounding role | an underived but groundingly idle concrete, unless the domain is strengthened |

Thus:

\[
U_G\land U_D\land CMU_G
\not\Rightarrow \neg\exists L\,NCC_G(L).
\]

If $L$ originates, constitutes, or ultimately grounds any contingent domain, it conflicts with $U_G$ or $CMU_G$ and changes the ultimate basis from $G$ to a plurality or partition. If $L$ does none of these things, present uniqueness has no deductive handle on it. Calling $L$ “another God” would add properties not supplied by `NCC`.

## 4. Profiles hidden by “non-created concrete”

Use the diagnostic profile:

\[
NCCProf_G(L)=
\langle n,d,a,g,c,r\rangle,
\]

where $n$ is modal status, $d$ dependence status, $a$ agency, $g$ grounding role, $c$ causal accessibility, and $r$ admissible action roles. After the contingency lemma, the principal rival configurations are:

| Configuration | Relation to present architecture | Power relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| necessary dependent concrete | created under the project definition; not an $NCC$ | any range question concerns a created relatum, although a necessary-creature production model would need its own bridge |
| necessary independent and causally isolated $L$ | not excluded by $U_G/U_D/CMU_G$ alone; explanatorily idle | coexistence supplies no action or blocker by itself |
| necessary independent active $L$ with no created grounding role | compatible with the narrow letter of $U_G$ but makes concrete ultimity plural | may generate eligible causal or relational tasks outside $CAN_G$ |
| necessary independent source of a contingent order | rejects $CMU_G/MUU$ and sole-ground architecture | rival source or co-fundamental basis, not a local exception |
| second independent agent | excluded only if it occupies the divine-ground role; otherwise a plural terminal subject model | interaction, conflict, and joint action must be typed by source role |

The table also shows why a non-created concrete need not be a blocker. Causal isolation, causal accessibility, resistance, and agency are additional facts.

## 5. Universal concrete dependence

The natural strengthening is:

\[
\boxed{
UCD_G:=
\Box\forall x[(Concrete(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow DependsExistentially(x,G)]
}
\]

It can be read as a conditional: in any admissible world, if a concrete distinct from $G$ exists, its existential dependence terminates in $G$. It does not assert that another concrete exists in every world or that $G$ necessarily performs the actual $OEC^+$ act.

### Independent support

1. **One terminal concrete explanation.** The existing argument prefers one underived source rather than partitioned, joint, or redundantly total ultimate grounds. Extending that preference to every concrete domain gives a single stopping point instead of a protected unexplained remainder.
2. **Well-founded dependence.** Derived concrete chains require a terminus. Under $UCD_G$, every such chain terminates in the already selected underived $G$ rather than in an additional terminal concrete.
3. **No independent positive role for $L$.** The ontology introduced no second concrete to explain modality, matter, consciousness, or agency. Abstract constraints do not require a concrete bearer, matter is a created instantiated profile, and created agents receive real derivative sourcehood.
4. **Plural-active costs.** An active independent $L$ requires partition, conflict, reciprocal limitation, or primitive necessary concordance. These are the same comparative costs that supported $U_G$, now applied to concrete reality outside the actual contingent order.
5. **Power-independent stability.** These reasons would favor $UCD_G$ even if unrestricted $OMP_Q$ were abandoned. The thesis is therefore not introduced merely to delete a counterexample from `Task_G`.

### Limits

The inference remains abductive. Logical consistency alone does not establish an $L$, but neither the contingency argument nor role-limited uniqueness demonstrates that two independent necessary concreta are impossible. A rival may treat $L$'s necessity as its stopping explanation and accept plural ultimity.

For that reason:

\[
UCD_G\quad\text{is proposed preferred at F2, not demonstrated.}
\]

It is stronger than $CMU_G$ because it covers concrete relata with no created-order grounding role. It is weaker than unrestricted necessary-entity uniqueness:

\[
UCD_G\not\Rightarrow
\Box\forall x\forall y[(Necessary(x)\land Necessary(y))\rightarrow x=y].
\]

Necessary truths, properties, abstracta, and concrete necessary dependents are not excluded. If the latter exist, they are created in the broad dependence sense.

## 6. Actions concerning a rival $L$

Suppose for the audit that $UCD_G$ is false and $NCC_G(L)$ is genuinely possible. Define:

\[
LTask_G(a,L):=
Task_G(a)\land
[PatientOf(L,a)\lor RelatumOf(L,a)\lor BoundarySource(L,a)].
\]

`LTask` does not say that $G$ can perform $a$. It identifies actions attributable to $G$ whose content genuinely involves $L$. The cases divide as follows:

| Description | Eligibility result | Range consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| know that $L$ exists or know truths about $L$ | ACO state rather than a further action; if $L$ is actual, it belongs to complete actual reality | epistemic scope issue, not a causal power contest |
| attend to or address $L$ | divine executive contribution can fall under $FEG_G$; uptake by $L$ is separate | no threat unless the task assigns an effect in $L$ to $G$ |
| create this very $L$ while preserving its essential non-created status | incoherent description | fails $Task_G$ |
| annihilate this very necessary $L$ while preserving its necessity and identity | incoherent result | fails $Task_G$ |
| make autonomous $L$ freely choose $q$ as $G$'s produced contrast | source-role contradiction | fails $Task_G$; enabling, invitation, or influence must be stated instead |
| cooperate with $L$ in a joint result | potentially eligible, with contributions source-indexed | $G$ must strongly produce its own contribution, not $L$'s autonomous settlement |
| modify, move, constrain, disclose to, or directly affect $L$ | potentially eligible if compatible with the independently established essences of both relata | requires a non-created-relational range bridge absent from $CAN_G$ |
| produce a source-neutral result despite opposition by $L$ | potentially eligible if the result and preservation profile are jointly compossible | failure would be a genuine blocker of unrestricted $OMP_Q$ |

An essence clause cannot be invented after discovering an inability. If a rival independently establishes that causal isolation or invulnerability belongs to $L$'s identity, the corresponding description may fail `Task_G`; if it merely redescribes $G$'s failure as $L$'s essence, C263 rejects the move.

## 7. The exact blocker condition

A genuine non-created blocker requires more than $NCC_G(L)$:

\[
\begin{aligned}
NCCBlock_G(L,a):={}&NCC_G(L)\land LTask_G(a,L)\\
&\land IndependentBoundary(L,a)\\
&\land \neg StrongCan(G,a).
\end{aligned}
\]

`IndependentBoundary` says that $L$ or its underived power profile supplies the final reason the result cannot obtain. It excludes:

- a contradiction or identity failure in the action description;
- $L$'s autonomous contribution wrongly assigned to $G$;
- a preservation clause that the task itself requires;
- mere causal isolation with no action concerning $L$;
- a dependent obstacle whose limiting profile ultimately derives from $G$.

If an $NCCBlock$ exists, unrestricted qualified omnipotence fails:

\[
\exists L\exists a\,NCCBlock_G(L,a)
\rightarrow \neg OMP_Q(G).
\]

If $L$ also grounds a contingent order, sole divine ultimity fails independently. If $L$ blocks only a non-created domain, $U_G$ may survive in its narrow actual-contingent scope while the broader picture becomes one of plural concrete ultimates and a bounded $G$.

## 8. What has and has not closed

Under the proposed $UCD_G$:

\[
UCD_G\rightarrow\neg\Diamond\exists L\,NCC_G(L).
\]

The non-created-relatum branch of the domain-exhaustion audit is then closed at F2, with exactly the same abductive force as the new universal-dependence premise. This does not demonstrate $UCD_G$ and does not establish power by definition. The thesis first fixes the modal ontology; S32's `Task_G` and the independently developed range bridges then assess actions within it.

Without $UCD_G$, the audit still advances. Any live countermodel must contain a necessary independent concrete, add a causal or relational bridge to $G$, specify an independently eligible action, and make the independent relatum the final boundary. A causally idle second concrete, a necessary abstractum, or a malformed task is insufficient.

Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ therefore remains preferred but provisional. The next independent branch is whether a genuinely possible divine action kind or qualitative intrinsic mode can extend $\mathcal K_G^{est}$ without being generated by the established powers or excluded through a power-relative definition.

## Adversarial tests

### Does C04 already prove universal concrete dependence?

No. C04 concerns contingent reality. $UCD_G$ extends the terminus claim to every concrete, including a hypothetical independent necessary $L$. Its support is comparative and must be recorded separately.

### Does a necessary created being make creation necessary?

Only conditionally upon such a being's existence, and $UCD_G$ asserts no such being. A necessary dependent concrete would exist through $G$ in every world and would not be the result of the contingent actual $OEC^+$ selection. The actual ontology currently posits none.

### Has a second necessary agent been shown contradictory?

No. If it is a second ultimate source of contingent order, it conflicts with the preferred uniqueness architecture. If it is causally isolated or occupies another necessary domain, its impossibility needs $UCD_G$ or stronger premises. Its independent motivation remains poor, but poor motivation is not contradiction.

### Is $UCD_G$ circular because it helps omnipotence?

It would be circular if its only reason were that $OMP_Q$ requires it. The offered reasons concern the number and organization of concrete explanatory termini and survive the rejection of $OMP_Q$. The power consequence is downstream.

### Could $G$ annihilate an independent necessary $L$?

Not while $L$ remains the same necessarily existing individual. The complete result is metaphysically inconsistent. The relevant power tests concern contingent modes, relations, or results compatible with the independently established identity of $L$.

### Could $G$ force $L$'s free choice?

$G$ could perhaps determine a token outcome, but then that token would not be freely originated by $L$ in the stipulated libertarian sense. A task requiring both is source-role inconsistent. Cooperation and address remain coherent because each agent supplies its own contribution.

### Does causal isolation protect omnipotence by definition?

Only if isolation is independently grounded in the proposed nature of $L$. That may remove a task from the domain, but it also yields two non-interacting terminal concreta and leaves the comparative meaning of unrestricted power less informative. It does not supply an active blocker.

## Literature and precedent

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 44, aa. 1–2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1044.htm) argues that every being apart from God is caused by the one first being and explicitly considers both necessary beings and prime matter. His proof uses participation, subsistent being, and stronger simplicity premises not adopted here; it is precedent for universal concrete dependence, not a proof imported into this project.
- [Brian Leftow, *God and Necessity*, Introduction](https://academic.oup.com/book/9855/chapter-abstract/157121625) frames traditional theism as requiring God to be the source of everything not included in God, while exposing the pressure from necessary truthmakers outside God. The present thesis is restricted to concreta and therefore does not settle that abstracta problem.
- [Eric Steinhart, “On the Plurality of Gods”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412512000285) constructs necessary concrete grounds paired with distinct universes. It is direct precedent that plural-source models can be systematically described; relative to this project, such a model rejects $CMU_G$ rather than supplying a local exception to it.
- [R. Harwood, “Polytheism, Pantheism, and the Ontological Argument”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412599005016) argues that maximal greatness need not entail uniqueness and that multiple maximally great beings need not automatically restrict one another. This cautions against treating the S13 conflict argument as a deduction.
- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 11, a. 3](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1011.htm#article3) supplies simplicity, perfection, and world-order routes to divine unity. As S13 already found, only the last supplies direct abductive support without importing the stronger classical package.
- The [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on divine simplicity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/) records the emanationist distinction between God's necessity *in se* and creatures necessary *ab alio*. The project uses only the modal distinction: dependence, not temporal beginning, marks creaturehood; it does not adopt necessary emanation or divine simplicity.

The component arguments—universal divine sourcehood, necessary beings through another, polytheistic countermodels, and task-relative omnipotence—have clear precedent. The possible project-specific contribution is their integration into a typed audit in which `NCC`, $UCD_G$, `LTask`, and `NCCBlock` occupy distinct inferential levels. No novelty claim is made before wider comparison.
