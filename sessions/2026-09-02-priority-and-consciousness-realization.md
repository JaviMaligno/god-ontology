## Author correction: questions do not set priorities

The previous session promoted CH16 because it was the author's newest question. That inference was invalid. Questions arise during exploration and must be preserved, but recency is not evidence of architectural priority.

Use:

\[
Priority(q)=F(
LoadBearing(q),
Dependency(q),
Fragility(q),
Reuse(q),
DepthCost(q),
ExplicitOrder(q)
).
\]

Do not use:

\[
Priority(q)=F(AskedMostRecently(q)).
\]

A new question therefore receives one of three dispositions:

1. **banked:** recorded without changing the queue;
2. **integrated:** treated inside an already selected higher-priority module;
3. **promoted:** moved into active work only after an independent priority argument or explicit authorial ordering.

An author's permission to explore a question authorizes the work but does not imply that every later question should displace the queue.

## Comparison protocol

The candidates are not scored as if metaphysical theories had an objective numerical ranking. Each is tested against the nine constraints fixed in S36:

1. phenomenal presence;
2. subject unity;
3. temporal unity and persistence through conscious gaps;
4. causal efficacy without duplication;
5. individuation of this bearer;
6. fit with creation and secondary causes;
7. a truthmaker for ASA's subject index;
8. graded development across conscious layers;
9. empirical compatibility through explicit bridges.

The symbols in the matrices mean:

- `+`: the family has a natural resource for the constraint;
- `±`: it can satisfy the constraint, but only with a substantive additional bridge;
- `−`: the family faces a characteristic unresolved pressure here.

These marks locate burdens; they are not proofs or aggregate scores.

## Candidate analyses

### 1. Reductive identity

On a strict identity view:

\[
c = NeuralOrPhysicalProcess(n).
\]

It avoids two independently sufficient causes because the experience and its physical realization are one event described differently. It also makes biological development and lesion, stimulation, or anesthesia evidence straightforwardly relevant.

The residual problem is not interaction but intelligibility: an asserted a posteriori identity does not yet explain why this process has phenomenal character or why its contents are presented to one bearer. The project also cannot infer determinism from physicalism. A physical whole could in principle have indeterministic and agent-level powers. The conflict arises only if a lower-level physical antecedent is sufficient for the exact token independently of the subject's exercise; that stronger thesis would defeat C08 for the token.

**Status:** live competitor, not preferred. It handles causal economy well but presently under-describes phenomenal presence, the subject index, and libertarian origination.

### 2. Weak emergence or functionalism

Weak emergence says that the higher-level profile is in principle derivable or fully realized by lower-level structure and dynamics. This is useful for access, report, discrimination, integration, and control. It does not by itself establish:

\[
FunctionalOrganization(F)ightarrow Phenomenal(F).
\]

If the implication is treated as definitional, phenomenal consciousness has been redescribed rather than explained. If it is a contingent law, the model is no longer weak in the relevant explanatory sense.

**Status:** disfavored as a complete realization theory, retained as an account of many access-conscious functions.

### 3. Non-reductive embodied powers

Here the conscious bearer is the organized embodied subject rather than a brain-part, mental module, or disembodied observer:

\[
UnifiedEmbodiedSubject(A,t)
\land Supports(Org_A(t),CapCon_A)
\rightarrow CapCon(A,t).
\]

$CapCon_A$ is a real power of $A$. Neural, bodily, affective, sensorimotor, and environmental processes constitute or enable its exercise. They are not a second agent standing beside $A$.

This model preserves the S29 whole-agent result and permits the conscious episode to enter causal control after preparation begins. Its decisive burden is to specify why the relevant organization bears a phenomenal power and how whole-level efficacy avoids causal exclusion.

**Status:** preferred working family.

### 4. Thin hylomorphic powers

The hylomorphic version strengthens the preceding family by treating organization or form as ontologically real and power-conferring:

\[
A = OrganizedLivingSubstance(M_A,F_A),
\]

where $M_A$ is the subject's material composition and $F_A$ its unifying organization or form. $F_A$ is not an extra spatial component or a second person. It explains why these materials compose one persisting power-bearing individual rather than an aggregate.

“Thin” matters. The project does not thereby import Thomistic intellectual psychology, immortality, natural teleology, divine simplicity, or a complete Aristotelian category system. The proposal uses only the claim that real organization can constitute an individual and ground its powers.

This gives the best current account of bearer unity and development. It still cannot merely pronounce that form produces phenomenality; a psychophysical power principle remains required.

**Status:** preferred F2 interpretation of the embodied-powers family.

### 5. Dual aspect and Russellian monism

On a dual-aspect model, the physical and phenomenal are aspects of one underlying event, process, or power:

\[
PhysicalAspect(x)land PhenomenalAspect(x).
\]

This can supplement the preferred model: the intrinsic nature of some organized subject-level powers may be phenomenal while their relational or structural manifestation is physically described. That avoids interaction between two substances and does not require experience to appear from properties wholly alien to it.

The supplementation is not yet adopted. If pushed to microphysical fundamentals, it inherits combination and boundary problems. If restricted to the organized subject, it needs a principled explanation of why dual aspect begins there.

**Status:** strongest live deeper bridge for phenomenal presence; open rather than incorporated.

### 6. Substance dualism

Substance dualism identifies the bearer with a non-physical subject paired or united with the body. This handles first-personal ownership directly and demonstrates that creation of one substance by another is not semantically incoherent.

Its price is a new production chain:

\[
Creates(G,Mind_A)
\land Pairs(Mind_A,Body_A,t)
\land Interacts(Mind_A,Body_A).
\]

Each relation needs individuation, timing, persistence, and causal laws. Contemporary delegation or pre-emption models show that interaction and non-overdetermination are not obviously impossible, but their extra laws are substantial theoretical commitments. The model also sits poorly with the existing judgment that bodily and affective processes are contributions of the acting subject rather than inputs to a separate inner subject.

**Status:** live but disfavored.

### 7. Fundamental experience or panpsychism

This family places experience or protophenomenal properties at the base. It avoids the appearance of phenomenality from a wholly nonphenomenal ontology, but then must explain how many micro-level items yield one macro-subject, one phenomenal field, and one agent.

The project has no independent reason to attribute experience to every fundamental created item. Doing so would widen the ontology without yet grounding the human bearer or its sourcehood.

**Status:** admissible but presently disfavored; the combination problem is a major unresolved burden.

## Preferred F2 model: the embodied power-bearing subject

The selected working model combines non-reductive embodiment with a thin hylomorphic powers ontology:

\[
EPBS(A)=
\langle
M_A,F_A,Cap_A,Life_A
\rangle.
\]

- $M_A$: changing material constituents;
- $F_A$: real unifying organization or form;
- $Cap_A$: the subject's power profile, including conscious capacities where present;
- $Life_A$: its temporally extended organized history.

The subject is the whole $A$, not $M_A$ considered as a heap, $F_A$ considered as another substance, or a conscious subsystem.

### Capacity onset

For each consciousness layer $k$:

\[
BeginsCapCon_k(A,t_c)
\leftrightarrow
Eligible_k(A,t_c)
\land CapCon_k(A,t_c)
\land
\forall t<t_c\,\neg CapCon_k(A,t).
\]

$Eligible_k$ must ultimately combine an empirically constrained organization profile with a psychophysical power principle. It cannot be reduced to report, intelligence, or future developmental potential by definition.

### Episode manifestation

\[
Manifests(
A,
CapCon_k,
RB_A(c,[t_1,t_2]),
c
).
\]

The realization base $RB_A$ is part of how $A$ exercises its power, not an independently complete producer plus a redundant mental producer.

### Non-competition

Distinguish:

\[
ConstitutesOrRealizes(RB_A,c)
\]

from:

\[
OriginatesOrCauses(A,c,e).
\]

The first answers how a whole and its episode are materially implemented. The second answers what subject or event makes a difference to the token outcome. A lower-level description may be complete as a description of the physical process without proving that its antecedent microstate is an independently sufficient cause or that the whole subject is unreal.

This is a compatibility model, not a completed solution to causal exclusion. It accepts a clear defeater:

\[
MicroPast_{<t}+Laws
\models Necessarily(TokenOutcome_t)
\]

through a justified empirical–metaphysical bridge would defeat libertarian origination for that token.

### Ultimate phenomenal ground

The model currently stops at a qualified primitive:

\[
CertainOrganizedSubjects
\text{ possess fundamental phenomenal powers.}
\]

This is more informative than saying only that consciousness “emerges,” because it identifies the bearer, power, organization, manifestation, and causal roles. It does not derive phenomenality from nonphenomenal premises. A dual-aspect account may later explain the intrinsic nature of the power, but that extension remains open.

## Relation to creation and divine consciousness

The created sequence is:

\[
G
\xrightarrow{OEC}
\langle S,Matter,LifePermittingPowers\rangle
\xrightarrow{secondary\ causes}
A
\xrightarrow{development}
CapCon_k(A,t)
\xrightarrow{manifestation}
c.
\]

This does not make $G$ the direct cause of every conscious token or its content. Perception, bodily processes, other agents, and the subject's own exercises can have typed causal roles.

$G$ is not assigned $M_G$ or $F_G$. The analogy between divine and creaturely consciousness lies in subject-level phenomenal and rational powers, not in a shared material realization. Divine consciousness remains underived and atemporal conditionally on essential divine conscious agency; created episodes remain temporal manifestations.

## Connection to the person

S36's bearer–presentation–integration relation now receives a more determinate candidate bearer:

\[
Bears(A,c,t)
\land PresentedTo(c,A)
\land IntegratedWith(c,Life_A,t),
\]

where $A$ is the organized embodied power-bearing subject.

This supports persistence through ordinary unconscious intervals. It does not settle whether every such living subject is already a *person* before the first phenomenal or agentive capacity. That is a further identity and status question and remains outside the present F2 stopping point.

CH16 is locally tested at F2. Its open F3 burdens are:

## Literature and novelty notes

- [J. J. C. Smart, “Sensations and Brain Processes”](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2182164) is a classic primary statement of contingent experience–brain-process identity and its parsimony motivation.
- [David Papineau, “The Case for Materialism”](https://doi.org/10.1093/0199243824.003.0002) develops the causal-completeness argument that conscious causes must be physical or risk epiphenomenalism.
- [J. Kevin O'Regan and Alva Noë, “A Sensorimotor Account of Vision and Visual Consciousness”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X01000115) treats seeing as an embodied exploratory activity structured by sensorimotor contingencies rather than activation of an inner representation alone.
- [William Jaworski, “Hylomorphism and the Metaphysics of Structure”](https://media.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/assets/pdf_file/0014/40190/Res_Philosophica.pdf) develops structure as a real power-conferring principle responsible for the persistence and capacities of organized individuals.
- [David Chalmers, “Strong and Weak Emergence”](https://consc.net/papers/emergence.pdf) separates derivational emergence from strong emergence requiring further fundamental principles.
- [David Chalmers, “The Combination Problem for Panpsychism”](https://consc.net/papers/combination.pdf) distinguishes subject, quality, and structural combination burdens.
- [Bradford Saad, “A Dualist Theory of Experience”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3) constructs a contemporary delegatory dualism designed to preserve phenomenal efficacy, observational closure, and non-overdetermination, showing that interactionist alternatives deserve more than dismissal by slogan.

Every component family and every major burden is established. The local contribution is the priority protocol and the integration of a thin hylomorphic embodied-powers model with E+, OEC, ASA, typed causation, and libertarian sourcehood. This is a project synthesis and working choice, not yet a novelty claim.

The comparative judgments and preferred realization remain proposals pending the author's next assessment under the default-acceptance rule. The priority correction itself is already explicit authorial direction.
