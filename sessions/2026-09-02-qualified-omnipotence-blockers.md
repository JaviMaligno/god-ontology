# Session: Qualified omnipotence, range, strength, and blockers

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S32
**Primary question:** Does the current architecture establish maximal power over the created order or unrestricted qualified omnipotence, and what would count as a genuine blocker?
**Dependencies:** C71–C76, C142, C222–C253, CH01, CH05, S11, S30–S31
**Target depth:** F2 blocker analysis

## Result in one sentence

The architecture conditionally excludes every created entity, law, or arena as an **ultimate** limiter of $G$ and strongly favors created-order maximality, but it does not deductively derive unrestricted qualified omnipotence. $OMP_Q(G)$ remains the preferred hypothesis because no coherent independently grounded internal or non-created concrete blocker has been identified, while its evidential status remains more provisional than $G$'s explicitly established powers.

This preserves the author's preference for a genuinely powerful creator without treating preference, definition, or absence of a known obstacle as demonstration.

## Preserve the decision about *ex deo*

The author accepts the first three S31 senses:

- causal source $ED_C$;
- virtual or exemplar source $ED_V$;
- participatory derivation $ED_P$.

Strict constitutive $ED_S$ remains open for deeper comparison, alternative models, and later evidence. The power analysis may constrain its coherent variants but must not close it by stipulation.

## Three evidential levels

| Level | Claim | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $P_{est}$ | $G$ possesses actualizing, originative existential, grounding, enabling, and at least some direct intervening powers | established or preferred through earlier modules |
| $OMP_C$ | $G$ has maximal qualified power concerning created targets | preferred at F2, not demonstrated |
| $OMP_Q$ | $G$ has maximal qualified power across every coherent divine action | preferred but provisional |

The entailments run downward:

\[
OMP_Q(G)\Rightarrow OMP_C(G)\Rightarrow P_{est}(G),
\]

but the reverse implications require bridge arguments.

## The independently constrained task domain

Omnipotence should quantify over actions attributable to $G$, not over every grammatically describable state of affairs. Let $E_G^{ind}$ contain only essential features supported independently of omnipotence. Current candidates include necessary existence, underived subjecthood, unified agency, $UPSS$, actuality-complete cognition as a cross-world schema, and fundamental non-spatiality. The set does not include goodness, absolute simplicity, or omnipotence itself.

Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Task_G(a)\leftrightarrow{}&WellFormedAction(a)
\land AgentOf(a,G)\\
&\land Coherent(a,N_L,N_M,N_G)\\
&\land CompatibleWith(a,E_G^{ind}).
\end{aligned}
\]

The last condition is legitimate only when the essential feature has an independent argument. An incapacity cannot be inserted into $E_G^{ind}$ merely to remove a counterexample.

This avoids two errors:

1. **Power-relative circularity:** “$G$ can do everything that $G$ can do.”
2. **State/action conflation:** “Every possible state must be something $G$ can strongly produce.”

A creature's originating $a$ is a possible event, but it is not thereby an action attributable to $G$. The divine task may instead be to create and preserve the arena in which the creature can originate an act.

## Range and strength

Power has at least two dimensions:

- **range:** which eligible tasks fall within the agent's capacities;
- **strength or control:** whether the agent can produce its specified contribution without the result depending contrastively on another autonomous source.

Define $StrongCan(G,a)$ so that when $G$ settles $a$, the obtaining of the contribution assigned to $G$ does not depend on another agent supplying that same contrastive content. This is not a claim that every divine effect lacks secondary causes.

The created-task domain is:

\[
\mathcal A_C(G)=
\{a:Task_G(a)\land CreatedTarget(a)\}.
\]

Then:

\[
OMP_C(G)
\leftrightarrow
\forall a\in\mathcal A_C(G)\;StrongCan(G,a),
\]

and unrestricted qualified omnipotence is:

\[
OMP_Q(G)
\leftrightarrow
\forall a[Task_G(a)\rightarrow StrongCan(G,a)].
\]

$OMP_C$ ranges over created structures, entities, powers, and events when the described contribution can coherently be $G$'s. It does not require $G$ to be the contrastive author of an act while that same act remains creature-originated.

## Strong and enabling actualization

The project should not count creaturely co-production as disguised exhaustive divine control. Distinguish:

\[
StronglyProduces(G,q)
\]

from:

\[
Enables(G,A,q)
\land Originates(A,q).
\]

When $q$ is a libertarian creaturely act, the second relation does not imply the first. $G$ strongly produces the arena, powers, circumstances, or intervention that belong to its own contribution. The creature supplies the contrastive settlement.

This preserves asymmetric co-production and prevents omnipotence from reintroducing the exhaustive divine authorship rejected in C17 and C76.

## What the current architecture establishes

### 1. No created ultimate blocker

Conditional on sole divine ultimity and existential sovereignty:

\[
Created(L)
\rightarrow
\neg UltimateBlocks(L,G).
\]

A physical law, field, object, agent, or spacetime region depends directly or through its arena on $G$. Such a relatum can condition a particular action description—especially one requiring preservation of its nature or agency—but it cannot independently impose the final boundary of divine power.

This conclusion does not say that $G$ actually overrides every created constraint. Capacity and exercise remain distinct.

### 2. Broad positive power evidence

The current power base is unusually generative:

- $P_{OEC}$ originates new bearers of powers without a prior material patient;
- $S$-specification ranges across laws, boundaries, primitives, and structures;
- intervention can target laws, boundaries, states, circumstances, powers, and token acts;
- $RI$ places every actual created relatum within direct causal availability.

These powers cross ordinary created causal categories and therefore support $OMP_C$ more strongly than a narrow “world-maker” power would.

The evidential dependence must nevertheless be tracked. $RI$ and universal causal availability were partly supported through provisional C75, so they cannot independently prove C75 without circularity. $P_{OEC}$ and the intervention taxonomy provide the cleaner positive evidence.

### 3. The missing universal-range bridge

From:

\[
HasPower(G,P_{OEC})
\land CanInterveneAcrossTypes(G)
\]

it does not deductively follow that:

\[
\forall a\in\mathcal A_C(G)\;StrongCan(G,a).
\]

The space $\Sigma_G$ was intentionally defined through independently established powers, so it cannot now be used to prove that those powers range over every coherent $S$. A genuine inability to originate some coherent, essence-compatible created structure would defeat $OMP_C$ even if nothing external actively blocked $G$.

$OMP_C$ is therefore preferred abductively at F2: the breadth and qualitative character of the positive powers fit maximal range, and no principled internal boundary has been found. It is not an entailment of necessary agency or ultimity alone.

## Blocker classification

| Candidate | Classification | Effect on omnipotence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| contradiction or identity failure | no eligible task | no limitation |
| role mismatch | possible state, but not an action attributable to $G$ as described | no limitation |
| created resistance or law | dependent condition, not ultimate limiter | no fundamental limitation |
| preservation condition inside the task | part of what the task asks $G$ to preserve | narrows that token task without limiting other actions |
| independently established essential feature | legitimate qualification of $Task_G$ | compatible with $OMP_Q$, though comparative “absolute maximality” may remain disputed |
| brute incapacity called essential | circular pseudo-exclusion | defeats $OMP_Q$ unless independently justified |
| necessary abstract truth or structure | modal/semantic constraint without causal agency | not an external power contest |
| non-created concrete limiter | genuine external blocker | defeats sole divine ultimity or $OMP_Q$ and requires a rival architecture |

## Internal constraints

An independently established essential feature can make a description fail to identify an action by the same $G$. Examples include:

1. $G$ both necessarily exists and makes it the case that $G$ never existed;
2. $G$ remains the same underived subject and is created by another source;
3. one token act is sufficiently settled by $G$ and remains contrastively originated by an autonomous creature;
4. an obtaining past event both occurred and never occurred in the same history;
5. $G$ ceases to be $G$ while numerically the same $G$ performs a later act.

These descriptions fail through contradiction, identity, source-role, or temporal truth conditions.

Other alleged internal limits remain substantive. If $G$ cannot originate a coherent red object, communicate with one kind of conscious subject, or actualize a coherent structure for no reason supplied by $E_G^{ind}$, the inability is a genuine counterexample. Calling it “part of the divine nature” does not solve it.

The project has not earned divine goodness or impeccability, so moral inability is not currently available as an essence-based restriction. If goodness is later derived, the familiar question whether inability to choose evil is lack of power, absence of a liability, or a legitimate essential restriction must be reopened rather than answered retroactively.

## Non-created external conditions

Logical and identity necessities do not stand opposite $G$ as rival agents. Mathematical necessities constrain a specified structure; they do not require that $G$ actualize that structure rather than another. Necessary truths and abstracta need not possess causal powers capable of blocking an action.

A concrete non-created limiter is different. Suppose:

\[
Necessary(L)
\land Independent(L,G)
\land Blocks(L,G,a).
\]

Then at least one of the following follows:

- the ultimate basis is $\langle G,L\rangle$ rather than $G$;
- $L$ is more fundamental than $G$ in the relevant domain;
- $G$ is an extremely powerful but bounded demiurgic agent.

These models are coherent competitors in the same broad sense as co-fundamental matter. They are disfavored by the current sole-ground architecture and explanatory economy, but not deductively impossible. Therefore unrestricted $OMP_Q$ remains more provisional than $OMP_C$.

## Exact self-copy challenge

The strict version combines:

\[
CreatedBy(G,G')
\rightarrow Depends(G',G)
\]

with:

\[
ExactCopy(G',G)
\rightarrow Underived(G')\land UltimateGround(G').
\]

If underivedness and dependence are incompatible, the exact created duplicate is not a coherent task. Its impossibility does not limit $OMP_Q$.

A dependent qualitative near-copy is different. $G$ may be able to create a subject with extremely broad analogous knowledge and power while that subject remains derivative. Whether every intrinsic divine power can be duplicated without generating a second ultimate center remains open; this is not required to resolve the strict paradox.

## Consequences for strict *ex deo*

Actual constitutive disjointness is a feature of the selected creation model:

\[
CCD(G,\mathcal C_0^{actual}).
\]

It has not been established as an essential modal policy:

\[
CCD(G,\mathcal C_0^{actual})
\not\Rightarrow
\Box\forall\mathcal C[Actualizes(G,\mathcal C)\rightarrow CCD(G,\mathcal C)].
\]

Therefore qualified omnipotence helps preserve rather than eliminate the open branch. For a proposed strict-*ex-deo* act $m$:

\[
Task_G(m)\land OMP_Q(G)
\rightarrow StrongCan(G,m).
\]

The unresolved work lies in $Task_G(m)$:

- **destructive transformation**, in which necessary $G$ ceases to be the same subject, is incoherent with $E_G^{ind}$;
- **non-destructive manifestation or constitution**, in which $G$ remains the same underived subject while a divine reality also constitutes a created effect, is not yet shown coherent or incoherent;
- even if coherent and within divine power, capacity does not imply actual exercise or make overlap the preferred creation model.

Omnipotence thus narrows $ED_S$ without deciding it prematurely. It excludes self-annihilating formulations, conditionally includes coherent non-destructive formulations in the divine action range, and leaves $CCD+OEC^+$ as the preferred account of the actual creation.

## Literature and novelty notes

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 25](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1025.htm) rejects the circular definition “can do whatever lies within divine power” and ranges omnipotence over absolutely possible effects. His positive derivation relies on infinite being and perfection premises not adopted here.
- [Thomas Flint and Alfred Freddoso, “Maximal Power”](https://www3.nd.edu/~afreddos/papers/mp.htm) distinguish secular maximal power from possession of all power compatible with a specifically divine nature and develop strong/weak actualization distinctions. The present project instead protects creaturely sourcehood by refusing to count weak enabling as strong divine production of the creature's token choice.
- [Wes Morriston, “Omnipotence and necessary moral perfection”](https://spot.colorado.edu/~morristo/omnipotence-and-necesary-moral-perfection.pdf) presses the objection that essence-based exclusions can leave another agent with a genuine active power God lacks. The project cannot answer through goodness because divine goodness is not yet earned; it responds only with independent action typing and explicit residual provisionality.
- [Scott Hill, “Giving Up Omnipotence”](https://philpapers.org/archive/HILGUO.pdf) generalizes the pressure from essential properties and argues that immense divine power could survive abandonment of the label. This is retained as a live fallback if $OMP_Q$ fails.
- [Ben Page, “Eternal Omni-Powers”](https://philarchive.org/archive/PAGEOKv1) analyzes omnipotence through fundamental powers and separates maximal strength from maximal range while acknowledging the difficulty of the range question. That distinction closely supports the present two-dimensional model.

The principal definitions and paradoxes are established. The local contribution is their integration with $P_{OEC}$, typed interventions, actuality-indexed ACO, agent-originated acts, $CT/RI$, $CCD$, and the four *ex deo* levels. No novelty claim is made.
