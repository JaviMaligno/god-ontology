## The author's account in one paragraph

Numerical identity is absolute and fundamental, not reducible to qualitative, material, psychological, or causal similarity; kind-sensitive persistence profiles explain and constrain ordinary diachronic identity without replacing it. For human and animal subjects the preferred profile is anchored hylomorphic-organismic persistence: one living embodied power-bearing continuant, individuated by its integrated token organization, persisting through a non-branching immanent causal-organizational career while matter, consciousness, memory, and character change; the pre-conscious organism and the later agent are one individual, and “person” marks a capacity or phase. Ordinary continuity does not cross a genuine gap, but intermittent existence is not impossible: a complete admissible history may assign one individual a disconnected life-set, constrained by the identity fact, same origin anchor, essence compatibility, non-branching, and one grounded career. Numerical recreation is a preferred metaphysical possibility at F2; duplicate reconstruction is a distinct task; no persisting carrier is required; divine knowledge tracks identity facts and does not make them; $G$'s own identity profile is by role, and a divine self-copy is a distinct dependent being.

**Conflict check.** One apparent tension: the branch exempted subjects from continuity-based persistence (C517), the trunk gives them an organismic persistence profile (C442–C446). It dissolves on the trunk's own distinction between what *explains* an ordinary career and what *constitutes* numerical identity: the trunk's profile explains and constrains, and the trunk says explicitly that it does not reduce $=_N$. The branch's claim was that sourcehood requires the identity unit to be prior to the history it produces; the trunk's primitive identity is that prior unit. What the branch must give up is the suggestion that a subject's ordinary persistence is *nothing but* being the same source: in this life it is also, and explanatorily, the career of one living organism. The accessible answer is revised accordingly.

## Consequences

1. **Ledger.** C506 retained as a refinement (“derivation from the agency model”); C507, C508, C509, C510, C514, C518 marked merged into the trunk rows; C517 restated; C555 marked answered. New row C556 records the reconciliation.
2. **Accessible answers.** “¿Qué me hace ser yo?” gains the organismic point: in this life what persists is one living being, the same organism from before it was conscious until after, with memories and character as what it carries and not what makes it the same; “persona” names what that being can do, not a second thing inside it. “¿Hay vida después de la muerte?” loses the hedge “si algo de mí existe entre medias o si vuelvo a existir sin más”: the framework now prefers that nothing need exist in between, and that the same individual can have a life with a gap, under the constraints the trunk states. The “Lo que no sé” bullet is revised to match.
3. **Treatise.** Part VII Chapter 28's identity paragraph and Part VIII Chapter 31's list of stops are updated: identity now has a preferred F2 account, and the branch's results are its agency-side refinement. Part IV Chapter 12 is not rewritten in this pass; a note is added to the outline.
