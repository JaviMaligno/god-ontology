## The two sessions in one paragraph each

The author's S73 fixes creation as existential dependence on $G$ (C586), so that a necessary concrete existing *through* $G$ is a creature *ab alio* and any genuine non-created concrete must be necessary and independent (C588). It shows that $U_G$, $U_D$, $CMU_G$, and $MUU$ quantify over grounding and origin roles and so do not by themselves exclude a role-idle independent necessary concrete (C589), proposes universal concrete dependence $UCD_G$ as the strongest justified F2 continuation on power-independent grounds (C591–C592), and, for the case in which $UCD_G$ is withheld, isolates the exact defeater form $NCCBlock$: an eligible task concerning $L$, an underived boundary supplied by $L$, and failure of strong capacity (C599). The branch is conditionally closed under $UCD_G$ (C600).

The branch's S84 partitions non-created concreta by modal status and dependence, defines a limiter at the level of facts rather than tasks (a contingent fact concerning $L$ with no ultimate ground in $G$, C690), states extended modal grounding coverage $MGC^+$ explicitly (C691), and proves the no-limiter lemma $NLL$ from $MGC^+\land MUU$ (C692). It treats emanations as non-created, excludes them from $\mathcal O_*$ by the contingency of every divine exercise, and reduces the non-created-relatum condition on unrestricted $OMP_Q$ to $MGC^+\land MUU$ (C695).

## What the branch adds, stated once

The one thing S84 contributes beyond S73 is the bridge from the task-level defeater to a fact-level condition on the relatum, and the lemma that follows from it. $NCCBlock_G(L,a)$ requires an underived boundary supplied by $L$; that boundary is a contingent fact about $L$ that no chain of dependence traces to $G$; so $Limiter_G(L)$. Under $MGC^+$ that fact has an underived concrete ground, and under $MUU$ read over such grounds the ground is $G$, which contradicts the boundary's independence. Hence no $NCCBlock$ is possible under premises weaker than $UCD_G$. The author's session is right that $UCD_G$ is the strongest justified thesis and that it is what closes the *existence* question; the branch's lemma shows that the *power* question closes already one step earlier, at the cost of reading $MUU$ over fact-grounds. Which reading of $MUU$ S39 licenses is the residual, and it belongs to the author's open question 2.
