# Session: Root creation and qualified creative plenitude

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S40
**Primary question:** Do essential $P_{OEC}$, patient-independence, specification, and agent-relative accessibility turn S39's invariant ultimate source into direct capacity to create every admissible fundamental specification without assuming that capacity in advance?
**Dependencies:** C04–C05, C32–C34, C55–C60, C73, C97–C111, C223–C280, C343–C366, CH05, S17–S18, S30, S32–S33, S38–S39
**Target depth:** F2 closure of the root-creation range bridge; retain separate $CC_G$, $HI_G$, and unrestricted $OMP_Q$ burdens

## Result in one sentence

The bridge can be closed at F2 for **essence-compatible root specifications**: constitutive admissibility supplies a possible root instantiation, conditional modal ultimity fixes $G$ as its ultimate source, roothood excludes a prior created intermediary, and the already selected agentic $OEC^+$ model makes that world's production an intentional exercise of the same $G$; patient-independence supports this result but does not itself prove universal range, and the conclusion remains abductively dependent on S39 rather than becoming a demonstration of unrestricted omnipotence.

The author's “a por ello” and subsequent “continua” accept S39 at its stated F2 and abductive statuses under the default-acceptance rule and authorize this next bridge test.

## Three claims previously compressed into transparency

The phrase `specification-transparent` had covered different claims:

| Claim | Content | Does patient-independence support it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| specification fidelity | if an $OEC^+$ act specifies $S$, its immediate effect instantiates $S$ | yes; this is already a condition of $OEC^+$ |
| absence of an external material filter | no prior patient or created medium imposes its own material type on the root effect | yes |
| universal manifestation range | for every eligible $S$, $G$ can perform such an $OEC^+$ act | only indirectly; it does not follow from the first two claims alone |

A power can be patient-independent yet have an intrinsically narrow manifestation profile. Therefore:

\[
PatientIndependent(P_{OEC})
\not\Rightarrow
\forall S[Adm_C(S)\rightarrow CanSpecify(P_{OEC},S)].
\]

Similarly, actual efficacy for $S^*$ does not entail efficacy for independently different $S'$:

\[
Effective(P_{OEC},S^*)
\not\Rightarrow
Effective(P_{OEC},S').
\]

This confirms C277's warning. The universal result, if available, must come from the modal-source architecture rather than from lack of a patient alone.

## Restricting the target to genuine root specifications

S33 used “fundamental created basis” ambiguously. Define a **root created basis** without mentioning $G$:

\[
\begin{aligned}
RCB(C,S)\quad\text{iff}\quad &Created(C)\land Instantiates(C,S)\\
&\land CompleteRootSpec(S)\\
&\land \neg\exists y[Created(y)\land ExistentiallyPrior(y,C)]\\
&\land GroundsCreatedDomain(C,S).
\end{aligned}
\]

$C$ may be a structured plurality rather than one simple object. `GroundsCreatedDomain` says that $C$, its instantiated powers, later secondary causes, and autonomous contributions provide the generative basis of the created domain. It does not say that $G$ directly produces $C$ and therefore does not build B2d into the definition.

A created producer may generate a later domain or entity. In that case the later object is not the root basis of total creation. The producer itself, or the more basic created structure on which it depends, belongs downstream from an $RCB$.

This gives a clean domain for creative plenitude. A complete $S$ in S38 is now understood as specifying such a created root and its generative profile. Partial structures and local derivative domains inherit possibility through complete extensions but are not separate witnesses to root-creation capacity.

## The direct-root lemma

Assume:

1. $RCB(C,S)$;
2. $UltimateOrigin(G,C)$ from S39's $CMU_G$;
3. existential-origin chains are asymmetric and well founded at their ultimate end;
4. no underived concrete ultimate source distinct from $G$ is admitted under the preferred modal architecture.

Then:

\[
RCB(C,S)\land UltimateOrigin(G,C)
\rightarrow DirectRootOrigin(G,C).
\]

Suppose an intermediary $L$ instead supplies the whole existential origin of $C$.

- If $L$ is created and existentially prior to $C$, $C$ fails the third conjunct of $RCB$.
- If $L$ is a member of $C$ and originates the whole basis including itself, existential priority becomes circular.
- If $L$ is an underived concrete source distinct from $G$, the preferred MUU component of S39 is false.
- If $L$ acts only instrumentally while its originative efficacy is wholly an exercise of $G$'s power, it does not replace $G$ as the direct metaphysical source; whether it counts as a direct *causal* instrument is terminological rather than a new ultimate contribution.

The conclusion concerns the root basis as root. It does not assert that $G$ immediately produces every later entity, event, organism, or free choice.

## From direct root origin to agentic creation

`DirectRootOrigin` would still be insufficient if the order emanated mechanically from $G$. The existing architecture has already rejected that model for the actual creative relation and treats creation as a contingent exercise of an agent's originative power:

\[
\begin{aligned}
AOR(G,C,S):={}&UPSS(G)\land RCB(C,S)\land Contingent(C)\\
&\land DirectRootOrigin(G,C)\\
&\rightarrow \exists a_C[
Originates(G,a_C)\\
&\qquad\land ExerciseOf(a_C,P_{OEC})
\land Selects(G,S)\\
&\qquad\land Specifies(a_C,S)
\land OEC^+(G,C,S)].
\end{aligned}
\]

Call this the **agentic-origin realization principle** $AOR$. It is not inferred merely from $G$ being an agent. It integrates already accepted C99–C100 and C109: $OEC^+$ is the selected production relation, and exact specification terminates in $G$'s reason-guided settlement. If $AOR$ were rejected, the project would have to reopen its agentic creation model, not merely adjust omnipotence.

## Essence-compatible eligibility

Creative plenitude should not quantify over descriptions that are possible in abstraction but incompatible with independently established essential features of the acting subject. Define:

\[
Elig_C^G(S):=
Adm_C(S)
\land CompleteRootSpec(S)
\land RoleComp(S,E_G^{ind})
\land Task_G(Create(S)).
\]

`RoleComp` asks whether the description of $G$ intentionally creating $S$ is coherent with the independently established divine essence profile. It contains neither $CanCreate(G,S)$ nor the conclusion that $G$ has adequate power. `Task_G` supplies the action-attribution rules already adopted in S32.

This qualification matters prospectively. If the project later earns an essential divine feature—moral or otherwise—that makes a proposed act non-attributable to $G$, excluding that act from the task domain is not a power failure. The feature must satisfy C263's independent-establishment rule; a brute incapacity cannot be relabeled as essence.

At the current stage, no complete theory of divine goodness has been earned, so the qualification does not silently add a moral filter. It preserves the correct logical shape for later development.

## Possibility must contain a root witness

S38's constitutive-admissibility account is a truth condition for genuine created possibility, not merely a list of abstractly consistent properties. For a complete eligible root specification:

\[
Elig_C^G(S)
\rightarrow
\Diamond\exists C\,RCB(C,S).
\]

This does not require a concrete non-actual universe. The possible-world notation represents an admissible way actual reality could be. If $Adm_C(S)$ did not license any possible instantiation, it would describe an uninstantiable profile rather than created metaphysical possibility.

Because $G$ necessarily exists and S39 fixes the ultimate source in every possible root instantiation:

\[
\Diamond\exists C[RCB(C,S)\land UltimateOrigin(G,C)].
\]

The direct-root lemma and $AOR$ then yield:

\[
\Diamond\exists C\exists a_C[
RCB(C,S)
\land Selects(G,S)
\land OEC^+(G,C,S)].
\]

## Agent-relative accessibility

A possible performance witnesses robust ability only under relevant conditions. Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
RelevantWitness(w,G,S):={}&SameAgent(w,G)
\land Preserves(w,E_G^{ind})\\
&\land MetaphysicallyAccessible(w)\\
&\land \exists C\exists a_C[
RCB_w(C,S)\\
&\qquad\land UltimateOrigin_w(G,C)
\land DirectRootOrigin_w(G,C)\\
&\qquad\land Originates_w(G,a_C)
\land ExerciseOf_w(a_C,P_{OEC})\\
&\qquad\land Selects_w(G,S)
\land Specifies_w(a_C,S)\\
&\qquad\land OEC^+_w(G,C,S)
\land SettlementBy_G(w,S)].
\end{aligned}
\]

The witness obtained above satisfies these conditions within the selected architecture:

1. necessary $G$ supplies transworld identity rather than a merely similar producer;
2. essential $P_{OEC}$ is not acquired from a created environment;
3. $AOR$ makes the production an intentional exercise by $G$;
4. creation has no prior created history that must be held fixed;
5. $S$ rather than an independent event or other agent supplies the contrast selected by $G$.

Thus the result does not infer ability from a world where the outcome happens accidentally. It uses a world in which the same agent performs the act through its own essential power.

## Qualified creative plenitude

Define the refined target:

\[
CP_G^{elig}:=
\forall S[Elig_C^G(S)\rightarrow CanCreate(G,S)].
\]

The bridge is most compactly stated through one bound modal witness:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Elig_C^G(S)
&\rightarrow \exists w\,RelevantWitness(w,G,S)
&&\text{(B1--B3)}\\
&\rightarrow CanCreate(G,S)
&&\text{(B4)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

B1 supplies the possible $RCB$; B2r and the direct-root lemma add the first source relations; essential $P_{OEC}$ and $AOR$ add the agentic exercise; and the retained-agent conditions make the witness relevant rather than accidental.

Universal generalization yields $CP_G^{elig}$.

The result is **preferred at F2**, not demonstrated independently of the architecture. Its weakest major premise remains S39's abductive $CMU_G/MUU$ preference. The direct-root lemma and agent-relative step make explicit what follows once that preference and the already accepted $OEC^+$ model are in place.

If no independently established divine essence constraint excludes an $Adm_C$ root specification, $CP_G^{elig}$ and the earlier $CP_G$ have the same current extension. The refined notation prevents that contingent extensional equivalence from becoming a permanent definition.

## What happened to specification transparency?

Universal specification transparency is no longer needed as an independent premise. Two narrower claims remain. The first is effect fidelity:

\[
OEC^+(G,C,S)\rightarrow Instantiates(C,S),
\]

while $AOR$ separately connects the exercise $a_C$ to $Specifies(a_C,S)$. The second is patient-independence:

\[
OEC^+(G,C,S)\rightarrow
\neg\exists m\,PriorMaterialPatient(m,C).
\]

These express fidelity and patient-independence in each possible witness. The universal range comes from quantifying over independently admissible root witnesses plus $CMU_G$, not from declaring one power content-neutral by definition.

Patient-independence still does useful work:

- it blocks a prior material patient from narrowing the root effect-type;
- it distinguishes root creation from transformation;
- it supports the coherence of $AOR$ across heterogeneous created profiles;
- it leaves any residual restriction intrinsic to $G$ or $P_{OEC}$, where C263 requires independent justification.

It remains abductive support, not the universal bridge by itself.

## Adversarial tests

### A created demiurge makes the root

If the demiurge is created and prior to the relevant object, that object is a derivative domain rather than total $RCB$. The model is allowed but outside root creative plenitude. The demiurge's own created root still requires analysis under $CMU_G$.

### The intermediary is created simultaneously with the rest

It may help generate later members or later states, but it cannot existentially originate the whole root set including itself without circularity. A minimal root basis removes internally derivative members from the root description.

### A possible world containing $S$ is not yet an ability

Correct in general. The inference succeeds here only because the world preserves the same necessary agent and essential power and contains that agent's intentional $OEC^+$ exercise. These are the relevance conditions, not consequences of mere occurrence.

### $Elig_C^G$ hides inability in the essence

C263 prevents this. `RoleComp` may use only independently earned essential features and action typing; it cannot contain `cannot create $S$` or be revised merely to protect omnipotence.

### $CMU_G$ already assumes creative plenitude

It assumes invariant ultimate sourcehood, a strong modal claim selected abductively in S39. It does not assert immediate production, intentional exercise, or agent-relative ability. The new argument shows how those further relations follow for root bases under $AOR$. The conclusion inherits the strength and revisability of $CMU_G$.

### One broad power proves all other powers

It does not. $CP_G^{elig}$ concerns originating root specifications. It supplies neither power to revise an obtaining creation while preserving its history nor an exhaustive classification of every eligible divine action.

## Consequences and limits

The current power structure becomes:

\[
CP_G^{elig}\subseteq OMP_C(G)\subseteq OMP_Q(G),
\]

where the symbols mark action-range inclusion, not yet established equalities. $OMP_C$ still needs at least:

1. constitutive control $CC_G$ over already created constituents and powers;
2. history-sensitive intervention range $HI_G$;
3. preservation-sensitive task typing when an action concerns autonomous agents.

Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ additionally needs domain exhaustion and answers concerning non-created relata and self-directed acts.

## Literature and precedent

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 45, a. 5](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1045.htm) argues that creation concerns the whole being presupposed by later effects and cannot be delegated instrumentally to a creature. His proof depends on universal causation, participation, and infinite power not fully adopted here; the narrower root/prior-patient structure is relevant precedent.
- [Kenny Pearce, “Infinite Power and Finite Powers”](https://writings.kennypearce.net/infinitePower.pdf) argues that perfect efficacy with respect to one proposition or narrow class does not entail perfect efficacy with respect to another. This supports the project's refusal to infer universal range from one actual $OEC^+$ manifestation.
- [Wolfgang Schwarz, “Ability and possibility”](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/ability-and-possibility/) argues that possible performance supports stronger ability attribution only when both the relevant worlds and what counts as performance are appropriately restricted. S40's `RelevantWitness` serves that role.

The non-delegability of creation, possible-world analyses of ability, and the independence of different efficacy ranges are established issues. The project-specific synthesis lies in using root-created specifications, conditional modal ultimity, $OEC^+$, and essence-qualified task typing to close a local creative-plenitude bridge while keeping secondary causation and libertarian agency. No general novelty claim is yet warranted.
