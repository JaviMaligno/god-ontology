# Session: Self-executive action and intrinsic manifestation

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S72
**Primary question:** Can the residual self-executive divine action types, including non-destructive intrinsic manifestation, be normalized and assigned a range without defining eligibility through divine power or omnipotence?
**Dependencies:** C55–C60, C223–C231, C255–C265, C371–C376, C420–C430, C451–C455, C492, C559–C571; S44, S45, S51, S71
**Origin:** First trunk priority after the author's parallel work was merged into `main`
**Target depth:** F2 domain closure within the preferred ontology; unrestricted cross-ontology closure remains open

## Outcome

The current ontology contains an exhaustible grammar of divine action. Its self-executive part is generated by the independently established cognitive, rational-volitional, communicative, originative, and interventive aspects of $G$. An eligible self-executive act is directly sourced as an exercise or manifestation by $G$, requires no numerically distinct bearer or causal patient for its executive result, and preserves $PP_G$. This definition contains no capacity or omnipotence predicate.

Selection, intention, attention, address, exercise, and intentional refraining are not six unexplained powers. They are combinations of an established manifestation kind, intentional content, positive or negative polarity, and an action role. Knowledge, creatorhood, grounding, and actuality-dependent relations remain states or relations unless an additional exercise is independently identified.

The range bridge is correspondingly limited. The established profile supports formal executive generality over independently admissible tokens generated by that grammar. It does not support arbitrary intrinsic qualities, new power kinds, or every self-description that can be stated. This closes the self-executive fragment of the preferred ontology at F2 without converting compatibility into ability.

An intrinsic divine mode also divides. If the mode is the occurrent token or intrinsic aspect of exercising an already established power, it is executive manifestation and is covered. If it adds a new constitutive quality, capacity, part, or bearer-state not generated by the established profile, it is self-modification and remains an ontology-extension question. For strict *ex deo*, a created root constituted by the first kind may be treated as one mixed-aspect act only when the constitution relation and joint specification are independently admissible. The second kind cannot be smuggled into root plenitude.

Together with S44 and S71, this supports ontology-relative qualified omnipotence $OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}$ at F2. Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ remains preferred but provisional because a rival ontology might contain a distinct non-created concrete relatum or a genuinely possible action kind absent from $\mathcal O_*$. That cross-ontology question is now the next trunk priority.

## 1. Action before power range

S44 defined self-executive action through `ExerciseOf(a,Power_G)`. That was sufficient to distinguish it from self-reconstitution, but it is too close to a power vocabulary for the present range test. Replace it with the action-side definition:

\[
\begin{aligned}
SelfExec_G(a):={}&ActToken(a)\land AgentOf(a,G)\\
&\land DirectExecSource(G,a)\\
&\land \neg RequiresDistinctExecPatient(a)\\
&\land Preserves(a,PP_G).
\end{aligned}
\]

`DirectExecSource` says that the token is attributable to $G$ as its contrastive executive source rather than merely occurring in a reality grounded by $G$. It does not assert that $G$ can perform it. `RequiresDistinctExecPatient` concerns the bearer or direct patient of the executive result; an intentional object, represented content, addressee, or consequent created effect is not thereby that patient.

The definition permits an act to have created effects. In that case it has a self-executive aspect and a created-directed aspect. Pure self-execution has no created result; mixed-aspect action is assigned to both relevant normal forms without multiplying the action token.

## 2. The established executive grammar

Let the independently earned manifestation kinds be:

\[
\mathcal K_G^{est}=\{
K_{cog},K_{vol},K_{addr},K_{oec},K_{int}
\}.
\]

These name cognitive-attentional, rational-volitional, communicative-address, originative-existential, and interventive manifestation. They are grounded respectively in ACO/MKO/ASA, conscious reason-guided will, asymmetric communication, $P_{OEC}/AOR$, and the intervention profile. They are not inferred from $OMP_Q$.

Represent a self-executive token by:

\[
SEForm_G(a)=\langle G,k,q,\epsilon,\rho,\pi\rangle,
\]

where:

- $k\in\mathcal K_G^{est}$ is the manifestation kind;
- $q$ is the content, object, or action specification;
- $\epsilon\in\{+,-\}$ marks exercise or intentional non-exercise;
- $\rho\in\{pure,mixed\}$ marks whether the token also has a distinct created-directed terminus;
- $\pi$ states preservation, source-role, and joint-compossibility conditions.

The familiar descriptions normalize as follows:

| Description | Normalized role | Further result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| selection, decision, intention | $K_{vol}$, positive settlement of eligible $q$ | may govern a created-directed exercise |
| refraining | $K_{vol}$, intentional negative settlement concerning an optional eligible act | no positive effect is manufactured by the omission |
| attention or regard | $K_{cog}$, selective orientation toward content already accessible to cognition | does not enlarge ACO's truth-scope |
| address or disclosure | $K_{addr}$, expressive contribution with content $q$ | mixed when a created manifestation or uptake is involved |
| originating creation | $K_{oec}$ under $AOR$ | root-created aspect already covered by $CP_G^{elig}$ |
| intervention | $K_{int}$ under an eligible $HI_G$ witness | fixed-history created aspect already covered by $HI_G^{elig}$ |

Deliberation is not added as a temporal inner process. Reasons-apprehension, comparative ordering, settlement, and execution can be logically distinguished within one atemporal act. Nor does every description pick out a further token: selecting $S$, intending its origination, and exercising $P_{OEC}$ may be aspects of one unified action.

## 3. Independent eligibility

Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
SEAdm_G(a):={}&SelfExec_G(a)\land k(a)\in\mathcal K_G^{est}\\
&\land ContentAdm(q(a))\land RoleComp(a,E_G^{ind})\\
&\land ReasonElig(a)\land JointComp(a)\\
&\land \neg NewConstitutiveBasis_G(a).
\end{aligned}
\]

None of these clauses says that $G$ can perform $a$.

- `ContentAdm` requires logical, metaphysical, and identity coherence and, where appropriate, actual or modal cognitive accessibility through ACO/MKO.
- `RoleComp` excludes descriptions inconsistent with underived subjecthood, rational agency, or the source role attributed to $G$.
- `ReasonElig` requires the action to be intelligible within the plural reason profile; it does not require a unique sufficient reason.
- `JointComp` excludes internally conflicting simultaneous settlements and incompatible mixed action specifications.
- `NewConstitutiveBasis_G` marks the introduction of a new power kind, part, external bearer, or intrinsic basis rather than a token manifestation of the established profile.

For negative polarity, an omission becomes an intentional refraining only when the corresponding positive act is an eligible optional exercise and the non-exercise is itself settled by $G$. Mere non-occurrence is not a second act, and inability to perform $b$ cannot be redescribed as refraining from $b$.

## 4. Formal executive generality

The bridge is:

\[
FEG_G:\quad
\forall a\,[SEAdm_G(a)\rightarrow StrongCan(G,a)].
\]

This is an F2 abductive result rather than a theorem of generic agency. Its support is cumulative:

1. each manifestation kind was established independently and has positive same-agent exercises in the architecture;
2. ACO/MKO removes ignorance of an eligible content while the reason profile makes the settlement intelligible;
3. $G$'s integrated rational will supplies contingent settlement and intentional non-exercise rather than mechanical necessitation;
4. pure self-execution introduces no external patient, material filter, new causal category, or mediating instrument;
5. `SEAdm_G` excludes essence loss, incoherent contents, incompatible joint resolutions, and new constitutive bases before ability is inferred.

The conclusion is content-general only within the established grammar. This avoids the invalid inference rejected for $P_{OEC}$:

\[
OneManifestation(p)\not\Rightarrow
\forall m[Compatible(m,p)\rightarrow CanManifest(p,m)].
\]

`FEG_G` instead says that when the ontology has already individuated a divine manifestation kind and an action token meets its independent satisfaction conditions, no further self-executive power type is missing. A counterexample must identify either an admissible token within this grammar that the cumulative bridge cannot cover or an independently motivated new action kind.

## 5. States, relations, and apparent extra acts

The following do not enlarge the range by themselves:

- knowing an actual truth under ACO;
- standing as creator, ground, or source after creation;
- acquiring a Cambridge-like relation when a created relatum begins or ceases;
- having an essential power or stable reason-orientation;
- being described under several teleological or causal descriptions by one act.

Attention is different because it can be a selective cognitive exercise, even though the object was already known. Universal exercised attention still does not follow: `FEG_G` supplies capacity for eligible attentive acts, not a divine intention to exercise them toward every subject.

Promises, forgiveness, command, covenant, and other institutionally or interpersonally thick actions are normally non-basic descriptions. Their divine contribution decomposes into volitional settlement, address, and any created-directed effect, while their full obtaining may require normative conditions or creaturely uptake. This does not prejudge fuller divine personhood or goodness.

## 6. Atemporal execution

Self-execution does not require a before-state in which $G$ has not decided and an after-state in which $G$ has. Under E+:

\[
AtemporalAct_G(a)\land ManifestedAt(a,e,t)
\not\Rightarrow BeginsToAct(G,t).
\]

The act can be contingently specified and its created manifestation temporally located while its divine source is atemporal relative to created time. Logical order remains: reasons make the settlement intelligible, the settlement specifies the act, and the created manifestation realizes its temporal terminus. This is an order of dependence, not a divine temporal sequence.

## 7. Intrinsic mode versus self-modification

Define an executive intrinsic mode:

\[
\begin{aligned}
ExecMode_G(m,a):={}&IntrinsicModeOf(m,G)\land TokenAspectOf(m,a)\\
&\land SEAdm_G(a)\land Preserves(a,PP_G)\\
&\land \neg NewConstitutiveBasis_G(m).
\end{aligned}
\]

Such a mode is an occurrent way an established power is exercised. It need not be a constituent added to $G$, a new capacity, or a transition in intrinsic constitution. Since aspect-powers are individuated by manifestation type (C492), their exercise tokens may differ contingently while the essential power profile remains fixed.

By contrast:

\[
QualMode_G(m):=IntrinsicModeOf(m,G)\land NewConstitutiveBasis_G(m).
\]

This covers a genuinely added quality, power, part, substrate-state, or other intrinsic determinant not reducible to an established exercise token. S45 does not exclude every such variation because full immutability is unearned. But neither minimal agency nor `FEG_G` supplies its range. The proposal needs its own kind profile, identity conditions, and modal ground before it can enter $Task_G^{\mathcal O_*}$.

## 8. The intrinsic-mode *ex deo* case

Split C571's residual variant:

\[
ED_{IM}=ED_{IM}^{exec}\cup ED_{IM}^{qual}.
\]

For $ED_{IM}^{exec}$, a complete mixed specification says that an executive mode $m$ of the very act originating $x$ also constitutes $x$:

\[
ExecMode_G(m,a)\land Originates(a,x)
\land Constitutes(m,x).
\]

If the constitution relation passes C565's substantive-content test and the complete divine-created specification independently satisfies `JointComp`, the descriptions do not require two separately composed acts. The one token has an intrinsic executive aspect and a root-created terminus. `FEG_G` covers the former and $CP_G^{elig}$ the latter; token unity supplies the bridge rather than an unrestricted rule that separately possible acts are jointly possible.

This makes $ED_{IM}^{exec}$ a conditional capacity at F2. It does not make it actual, and actual $OEC^+_{CCD}$ remains preferred. It also does not establish $ED_{IM}^{qual}$. A qualitative mode that constitutes $x$ adds a genuine divine intrinsic result and remains outside the current ontology until its proposed kind is independently developed.

## 9. Result for qualified omnipotence

Within the preferred ontology:

1. S44 closes pure created-directed tasks through $CAN_G$;
2. S45 excludes identity-breaking pseudo-tasks and supplies $PP_G$;
3. S71 covers relational $ANDC$ and rejects destructive strict-*ex-deo* variants;
4. S72 closes the established self-executive grammar and conditionally covers executive-mode $ED_{IM}$;
5. abstract necessities constrain task coherence without being causal patients;
6. $\mathcal O_*$ contains no distinct non-created concrete relatum.

Therefore:

\[
OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}(G):=
\forall a[Task_G^{\mathcal O_*}(a)\rightarrow StrongCan(G,a)]
\]

is preferred at F2, conditional on the earlier modal and composability bridges. This is not a verbal victory: any counterexample licensed by the current kinds reopens it.

The unrestricted claim remains stronger:

\[
OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}(G)\not\Rightarrow OMP_Q(G).
\]

A rival ontology may motivate a distinct non-created concrete $L$, or a coherent intrinsic action kind not generated by $\mathcal K_G^{est}$. The next audit asks whether such $L$ is compatible with the strongest justified uniqueness and ultimity theses and what tasks concerning it would be eligible.

## Adversarial tests

### Does the grammar define action range through power?

No. Its kinds come from independently established cognition, will, communication, creation, and intervention. `SEAdm_G` is stated through action structure, content, identity, reasons, roles, and compossibility. Ability occurs only in the conclusion `FEG_G`.

### Does complete cognition make every thought or attention an action?

No. ACO and MKO are standing epistemic scope claims. Attention is actional only when there is a selective exercise; simply knowing $p$ is not another token act.

### Can $G$ refrain from an impossible or unavailable action?

No. Mere non-performance is insufficient. Intentional refraining presupposes an otherwise eligible optional exercise and a settled non-exercise.

### Can $G$ intend incompatible contents separately?

Separate descriptions may each be intelligible while their conjunction fails `JointComp`. Formal executive generality does not imply simultaneous realization of incompatible intentions.

### Does atemporal decision collapse into necessary action?

No. Atemporality removes succession; it does not remove modal contrast. The same necessary agent may settle different eligible contents or refrain across relevant possible histories.

### Is every contingent intrinsic property of $G$ an action?

No. A state, Cambridge relation, qualitative modification, and occurrent exercise are distinct. Only the last is covered by `ExecMode_G`.

### Does $ED_{IM}^{exec}$ collapse actual creation into strict *ex deo*?

No. It is a conditional capacity requiring an additional substantive constitution relation. The actual act may have an intrinsic executive aspect without that aspect constituting its created result; actual $CCD$ denies the latter relation.

### Does one agent's capacity for each act entail capacity for their conjunction?

No. `JointComp` and token unity are required. The mixed $ED_{IM}^{exec}$ case is covered only when its one complete specification identifies one admissible act-token, not by a general closure of separately possible capacities.

### Has unrestricted omnipotence now been demonstrated?

No. The result is ontology-relative and inherits the F2 status of $CP_G^{elig}$, $HI_G^{elig}$, `FEG_G`, and their modal bridges. Distinct non-created concreta and independently motivated new action kinds remain cross-ontology tests.

## Literature and precedent

- [Robert Ellis, “God and ‘Action’”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500019557) argues that ordinary action analysis must precede its theological use. This supports defining action and patient structure before asking about divine range.
- [Frank G. Kirkpatrick, “Is the Notion of a Divine Basic Act a Necessary and Sufficient Way of Talking about God's Actions in the World?”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500001475) directly questions whether all divine actions must be basic. The present model is narrower: self-executive settlement is direct, while address and created-directed descriptions may be non-basic or mixed.
- [Don Lodzinski, “The Eternal Act”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412598004478) supplies precedent for distinguishing deliberation, decision, and execution without concluding that divine choice must be temporally successive. The project's dependency-order treatment avoids adopting Lodzinski's pantheistic execution model.
- [Randolph Clarke, *Omissions: Agency, Metaphysics, and Responsibility*](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199347520.001.0001) distinguishes omission, intentional omission, ability, and refraining. This supports refusing to identify mere non-occurrence with a divine negative act.
- [Jonathan Payton, “The Logical Form of Negative Action Sentences”](https://doi.org/10.1080/00455091.2016.1193406) defends an event treatment of omissions and refrainments. S72 remains neutral between event and absence ontologies; it requires only a source-indexed intentional settlement when refraining counts as actional.
- [Tomis Kapitan, “Agency and Omniscience”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500001359) treats knowledge, will, intentions, and plans as jointly relevant to divine agency. The present grammar separates their roles so omniscient awareness does not itself become action.

The literature contains theories of divine basic action, timeless action, intention, and omission. The project-specific contribution, if any, is the integration of an anti-circular self-executive normal form with aspect-powers, $PP_G$, created-action normal forms, and typed *ex deo*. No novelty claim is warranted before a systematic action-theory comparison.
