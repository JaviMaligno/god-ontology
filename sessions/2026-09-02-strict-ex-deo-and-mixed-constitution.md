# Strict *Ex Deo* and Mixed Constitution after Identity

**Session ID:** S71

**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Primary question:** Can one numerically identical and underived $G$ non-destructively constitute a numerically distinct dependent creature, and if so which established divine power range covers the task?

**Dependencies:** S30–S32, S38–S45; $UPSS$, $MCS$, $CT$, $RI$, $CCD$, $PP_G$, $OEC^+$, $CP_G^{elig}$, $NF_G$, and $CAN_G$

**Target depth:** F2 application of identity to the mixed strict-*ex-deo* boundary; enough to classify the viable variants and their power coverage without constructing a complete panentheist ontology

## Outcome

> **Later authorial selection (S79–S80):** This session's taxonomy and non-destructive filters remain in force, but its preference for actual $CCD$ is superseded. The author explicitly prefers genuine *ex deo* continuity, so $ANDC$ is now the principal actual-creation hypothesis and disjoint $OEC^+_{CCD}$ the fallback. [S79](2026-09-04-shoemaker-ex-deo-and-identity-origin-corrections.md) makes finite determination the active burden; [S80](2026-09-04-onset-without-a-first-moment.md) requires the bearer side to use onset-neutral $CTI_G^{\Omega}$ rather than a punctual origin anchor.

S45 removes the numerical-identity objection but does not make every strict *ex deo* description coherent. The family divides sharply.

Partition, transfer, and whole-subject transformation conflict with $UPSS$, $MCS$, or $PP_G$. A literal world-body model in which contingent creatures constitute the underived subject reverses the accepted dependence direction. An agential world-body metaphor is coherent but reduces to relational immanence and is not strict *ex deo*.

The strongest surviving variant is **asymmetric non-depleting constitution** ($ANDC$): the undivided $G$ constitutes a numerically distinct dependent created bearer without transferring a proper part of itself, losing anything, acquiring an external bearer, or receiving its own identity or powers from the creature. The creature has its own origin anchor, unity, persistence profile, and source roles. Constitution is asymmetric, and neither the creature nor its acts are numerically identical with $G$ or with divine acts.

$ANDC$ is retained as a power-independent candidate for F2 admissibility, conditional on constitution doing real work beyond causal grounding. Subject to that constitutive-content test, it supplies a coherent non-destructive strict-*ex-deo* witness. It remains unexercised in the preferred actual model, which retains $CCD$ and disjoint $OEC^+$. Capacity does not entail actual overlap.

Once the divine side of $ANDC$ is understood as a merely relational change that preserves $PP_G$, its creation is not an intrinsic self-reconstitution task. It normalizes to a root-created specification with an additional preservation condition and is covered by $CP_G^{elig}$. No new mixed power is required for this relational whole-presence variant. A genuinely intrinsic contingent divine mode remains a self-executive or self-modificatory case and passes to the next residual power audit.

## A correction to the earlier strict definition

S31 used:

\[
ED_S(G,x)\leftrightarrow
\exists d[DivineToken(d,G)\land
(Constitutes(d,x)\lor PersistsIn(d,x)\lor Becomes(d,x))].
\]

This was useful for separating overlap from causal or participatory dependence, but it grouped relations with different identity consequences and left `DivineToken` underspecified. The present analysis distinguishes:

| Variant | Core relation | Identity effect | Present status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $ED_{ST}$ | a proper divine constituent is transferred to or persists in $x$ | partitions or depletes $G$ | incompatible with current $UPSS/MCS$ |
| $ED_{BT}$ | the whole $G$ becomes $x$ | replaces the divine career with a created one | incoherent with $PP_G$ |
| $ED_{WB}$ | the created world or creature constitutes a body or proper part of $G$ | makes divine constitution depend on the created relatum | incompatible with current $CT/UPSS$ when literal |
| $ED_{IM}$ | $x$ is or is constituted by a contingent intrinsic mode of $G$ | may preserve the subject but changes its intrinsic mode profile | coherent candidate; residual self-executive burden |
| $ED_{ANDC}$ | the whole unchanged $G$ asymmetrically constitutes distinct $x$ | adds a relation while preserving both identities | preferred coherent strict witness |
| $ED_O$ | $x$ shares divine being under an inflationary building conception of grounding | depends on what “shared being” denotes | adjacent panentheist interpretation, not automatically token overlap |

The refined strict family is:

\[
ED_S^*(G,x):=
ED_{ST}\lor ED_{BT}\lor ED_{WB}\lor ED_{IM}\lor ED_{ANDC}.
\]

$ED_O$ is kept adjacent rather than included automatically. If “shared being” identifies a numerically divine constituent, it must be translated into one of the strict variants. If it names only asymmetric existential grounding, it belongs to $ED_C$ or a strengthened participatory level. This prevents terminology from deciding the ontology.

## Identity and dependence filters

### Preservation of the divine subject

For a non-destructive case:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Pres_G(a):={}&SameNumericalSubject(G,a)\\
&\land Preserves(a,PP_G)\\
&\land \neg Divides(a,G)\\
&\land \neg Depletes(a,G)\\
&\land \neg GivesExternalBearer(a,G)\\
&\land \neg CreatedGroundsBasicProfile(a,G).
\end{aligned}
\]

Necessary existence alone is insufficient. The completed action must still be attributable to the same underived unified subject with its essential power profile.

### A genuinely created other

The result must also preserve created otherness:

\[
\begin{aligned}
CreatedOther(x,G):={}&Created(x)\land x\neq G\\
&\land Depends(x,G)\\
&\land OwnOriginAnchor(x)\\
&\land OwnUnityProfile(x)\\
&\land OwnPersistenceProfile(x).
\end{aligned}
\]

`Own` means proper to the created bearer's career, not independent of $G$ in every respect. Existential dependence and constitutive overlap do not erase numerical distinction.

### Separation of source roles

Constitution must not collapse agency:

\[
\begin{aligned}
SourceSep(G,x):={}&
PowersOf(x)\not\equiv PowersOf(G)\\
&\land ActsOf(x)\not\equiv ActsOf(G)\\
&\land Originates(x,q)\not\Rightarrow Originates(G,q).
\end{aligned}
\]

This extends asymmetric co-production into an overlap case. If every creaturely manifestation were numerically a divine act, the model would lose the autonomous sources it was meant to contain.

Define the general non-destructive filter:

\[
NSED(G,x,a):=
ED_S^*(G,x)\land Pres_G(a)\land CreatedOther(x,G)\land SourceSep(G,x).
\]

## Eliminated variants

### Partition or transfer

If a proper divine constituent exists before creation and can be detached, $G$ contains an independently identifiable separable ingredient. That conflicts with the current $UPSS/MCS$ profile. If the constituent comes into existence only with the creature, either it is created and therefore not an underived divine token, or $G$ acquires a new intrinsic part and requires a different constitution theory.

Transfer also introduces depletion. If $d$ leaves $G$, the post-transfer subject lacks what earlier constituted it; if $d$ remains wholly in both, the case has become whole-presence rather than transfer. Thus $ED_{ST}$ supplies no non-destructive witness under the current architecture.

### Whole-subject becoming

If the whole $G$ becomes a contingent created $x$, either $x=G$ and no numerically distinct created other has been produced, or $x\neq G$ and the original necessary subject has been replaced. Calling either result by the same name does not satisfy both $CreatedOther(x,G)$ and $PP_G$.

A reversible transformation does not help. Treating a later $G$ as a numerical recreation adds a gappy divine career, contrary to its current atemporal necessary-existence profile, while the intermediate $x$ still cannot be both the same $G$ and a distinct created other. $ED_{BT}$ is therefore not an eligible creation task of the same current $G$.

### Literal and agential world-body models

Two readings must be separated:

1. On the **literal constitutive** reading, created parts compose or partly constitute $G$. Basic divine constitution then varies with a contingent created order. This reverses the accepted direction in $CT$ and makes an underived subject constitutively dependent on derived relata.
2. On the **agential** reading, the world is a body because $G$ acts through, knows, and is available throughout it. This can preserve $G$ and the creatures, but it is already expressed by $RI$, causal *ex deo*, and participatory *ex deo*. No shared constituent follows.

A body analogy can remain useful for governance or intimacy. It cannot establish strict overlap while shifting between these readings.

## The surviving whole-presence witness

Define asymmetric non-depleting constitution:

\[
\begin{aligned}
ANDC(G,x):={}&ConstitutesWhole(G,x)\\
&\land G\neq x\land Depends(x,G)\\
&\land \neg Constitutes(x,G)\\
&\land \neg ProperPartTransfer(G,x)\\
&\land IntrinsicProfile(G,H_{ANDC})=EssentialIntrinsicProfile(G)\\
&\land CreatedOther(x,G)\\
&\land SourceSep(G,x).
\end{aligned}
\]

`ConstitutesWhole` does not mean that $G$ is a divisible material portion or an ordinary proper part of $x$. It says that the same undivided non-spatial subject is a real constitutive ground of $x$. Created organization and kind supply $x$'s own unity and powers. Because $G$ is non-spatial, presence in several relata is not spatial multiplication or distribution into fractions.

The identity result is:

\[
ANDC(G,x)\rightarrow
Preserves(PP_G)\land PP_x\neq PP_G\land x\neq G.
\]

This is not relative identity. $G$ and $x$ are simply distinct; the asymmetric constitution relation explains intimate dependence without collapsing them.

### Constitutive-content test

$ANDC$ counts as strict only if:

\[
ANDC(G,x)\not\equiv
[Grounds(G,x)\land CausesExistence(G,x)].
\]

The model must state what follows from `ConstitutesWhole` that does not follow from ordinary grounding and production. Candidate consequences include a genuine common constituent, constitutive rather than merely causal dependence, and counterfactual constraints on what $x$ can be while so constituted.

If no such difference can be stated, $ANDC$ collapses into $ED_C$, $ED_V$, or $ED_P$ and ceases to witness strict *ex deo*. The present F2 verdict therefore establishes admissibility conditional on a substantive constitution relation; it does not pretend that the relation has been reduced to more familiar primitives.

## Matter and physicality

Non-material overlap does not satisfy $PMC_U$:

\[
ANDC(G,x)\land NonMaterial(G)
\not\Rightarrow MaterialCause(G,x).
\]

The physical character of $x$ can belong to its created organization, laws, and power profile while its divine constituent remains non-spatial and non-material. That is formally coherent, but it leaves the same cross-category origination question already faced by disjoint $OEC^+$. If a defender instead calls $G$ material because $G$ constitutes $x$, the defender must abandon or revise the independently preferred physical immateriality of $G$.

Strict *ex deo* therefore provides greater ontological intimacy, not a free solution to material production.

## Grounding overlap is a theory choice

Some inflationary or building conceptions of grounding treat a grounded fact as constructed from, and therefore ontologically overlapping with, its grounds. On such a view, divine grounding may already imply a form of $ED_O$. Making that move would globally reinterpret the project's grounding relation: $GSDA$, bounded grounding, created dependence, and relational immanence would all acquire constitutive-overlap consequences.

The current project uses grounding minimally for asymmetric existential dependence and does not build overlap into it. This is deliberate. Otherwise actual $CCD$ would be contradicted by the very grounding relation used to support creation, making the disjoint/overlap comparison verbal rather than substantive.

$ED_O$ remains a coherent rival semantics. It must specify whether the shared item is an existence fact, an act of being, a constituent, or only a dependence relation before it can be compared with $ANDC$.

## Power classification

Let $S_{ANDC}$ be a complete root specification containing $x$, its created powers, and the asymmetric relation to the same $G$. Its independent eligibility requires:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Elig_{ANDC}^G(S):={}&Adm_C(S)\land CompleteRootSpec(S)\\
&\land ANDC(G,x)\land Pres_G(Create(S))\\
&\land RoleComp(S,E_G^{ind})\\
&\land Task_G(Create(S)).
\end{aligned}
\]

No capacity predicate occurs in this definition.

Because the intrinsic profile of $G$ remains fixed, the only produced bearer is $x$. The new fact about $G$ is relationally consequent on the existence and constitution of $x$. The task therefore normalizes as:

\[
Elig_{ANDC}^G(S)\rightarrow RootForm(Create(S)).
\]

Applying the established range:

\[
CP_G^{elig}\land Elig_{ANDC}^G(S)
\rightarrow CanCreate_{ANDC}(G,S).
\]

This is the requested bridge for non-destructive whole-presence *ex deo*. It is conditional in exactly the same way as $CP_G^{elig}$: the constitutive specification must first pass independent admissibility and the earlier $CMU_G/OEC^+$ route remains abductive at F2.

For this inference, $CP_G^{elig}$ uses the minimal core of $OEC^+$—direct intentional origination of a distinct dependent root without a prior material patient—not the actual policy $OEC^+_{CCD}$. S32 already refused to place $CCD$ inside $E_G^{ind}$ as an essential incapacity. If disjointness were built into creative power itself, the overlap case would be excluded by definition and no range test would have occurred.

### Why the task looked mixed

Its description mentions both a created relatum and a divine relation, so S44 conservatively classified it as mixed. S45 now distinguishes intrinsic reconstitution from relation acquisition. A Cambridge-like relation that follows from creating $x$ does not require an additional power to rebuild $G$.

This does not generalize to $ED_{IM}$. If creating $x$ requires $G$ to acquire a genuinely intrinsic mode $m$, then the task has both a root-created result and a self-executive or self-modificatory result:

\[
IntrinsicModeOf(m,G)\land Constitutes(m,x).
\]

Its coverage depends on the forthcoming self-executive range audit and on whether such modes are compatible with $UPSS$. It cannot be smuggled into $CP_G^{elig}$ by calling every divine change relational.

## Actuality and capacity

The actual model retains:

\[
OEC^+_{CCD}(G,\mathcal C_0,S).
\]

The capacity result permits:

\[
CanCreate_{ANDC}(G,S_{ANDC}).
\]

There is no inconsistency:

\[
CanCreate_{ANDC}(G,S_{ANDC})
\not\Rightarrow Actualizes(G,S_{ANDC}).
\]

$CCD$ remains a selected feature of the actual creation, not an essential incapacity of $G$. The disjoint model stays preferred because it achieves creation, autonomy, omnipresence, and intervention with fewer constitutive primitives and clearer source separation. The overlap capacity matters to the qualified-power range and to possible future incarnation-like questions, not to the actual creation thesis.

## Adversarial tests

### Is $ANDC$ just a relabelled contradiction?

No identity contradiction follows from one entity constituting another while remaining numerically distinct; constitution is not identity. The serious burden is substantive content, addressed by the constitutive-content test.

### Does one whole $G$ in many creatures make the creatures identical?

No. Sharing a constituent is not sufficient for identity. Their origin anchors, created organization, power profiles, spatial careers, and other constituents distinguish them. The model nevertheless accepts widespread constitutive overlap as a cost.

### Does the creature become part of God?

Not under asymmetric $ANDC$. $G$ constitutes $x$; $x$ does not constitute $G$. Reversing that direction produces the rejected literal world-body model.

### Does every creaturely evil become a divine act?

No. Constitution of a bearer does not identify its token manifestations with manifestations of the constituent. `SourceSep` preserves creaturely origination. A theory that cannot preserve that separation conflicts with the established agency architecture.

### Can God withdraw from the creature without losing something?

Yes at the level of the complete history. The creature can have a bounded existence or constitution profile while $PP_G$ remains unchanged. No temporal extraction of a divine part occurs.

### Is an intrinsic divine mode necessarily destructive?

No. The project has not accepted absolute immutability. A mode could preserve the divine subject, but it requires an account of intrinsic divine variation and a covering self-executive power. It remains open for the next audit rather than being rejected by identity alone.

### Does $ANDC$ make strict *ex deo* necessary for omnipotence?

It makes capacity relevant if the task is independently eligible. Omnipotence does not require its exercise, and the actual disjoint model can remain preferable.

## Literature and precedent

- [Thomas Oberle, “Grounding Panentheism”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412525100978) develops an inflationary grounding model intended to be asymmetric, quasi-mereological, and generative. It is the clearest recent precedent for $ED_O$ and for requiring overlap to do more than ordinary dependence.
- [Georg Gasser, “God's Omnipresence in the World”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11153-018-9695-9) argues for an agential interpretation of divine presence: God is present where God acts. This closely supports the project's $RI$ and its non-strict world-body reading.
- [Oliver Crisp, “Against Mereological Panentheism”](https://doi.org/10.24204/EJPR.V11I2.2969) emphasizes both the demarcation problem and the costs of treating the world as a literal divine part. The present partition/body rejection is narrower because it depends on the project's $UPSS$, $CT$, and dependence direction rather than a complete classical-theist package.
- [J. J. Lipner, “The World as God's ‘Body’: In Pursuit of Dialogue with Rāmānuja”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500015912) shows that world-body language can encode a technical controller–controlled and supporter–supported relation rather than ordinary material composition. This motivates separating literal, technical-constitutive, and agential readings.
- [Daniel Soars, “Creation in Aquinas: *ex nihilo* or *ex deo*?”](https://doi.org/10.1111/nbfr.12603) argues that emanation “from God” and creation without pre-existing material need not contradict one another. This supports keeping causal/exemplar derivation apart from transfer of divine material.

Mereological panentheism, grounding panentheism, emanation, world-body language, and non-material constitution are established families. The project-specific contribution, if any, is the integration of $ANDC$ with $PP_G$, created persistence profiles, source-indexed agency, $CP_G^{elig}$, and the action normal form. No novelty claim is warranted before a deeper constitution and panentheism comparison.
