# Session: Searching for the universal power-range bridge

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Session ID:** S33
**Primary question:** Can the gap between established divine powers and universal qualified range be bridged without defining possibility or divine essence through the conclusion?
**Dependencies:** C04–C05, C11–C12, C48, C74–C76, C80–C81, C97–C111, C223–C232, C255–C270, CH05, S18, S30–S32
**Target depth:** F2 bridge search; no premature promotion of $OMP_Q$

## Result in one sentence

The strongest current route is a **modal-source bridge**: if a created specification is genuinely possible independently of the power attribution, the same necessary $G$ is the invariant sole origin of every possible fundamental created order, and $G$'s originative powers are essential and choice-accessible, then a possible exercise by $G$ witnesses a present capacity to originate that specification. This is a valid conditional route to creative range, but its modal-invariance and non-circular-possibility premises are not yet fully established, and it does not alone establish every intervention or unrestricted $OMP_Q$.

The author accepts S32's evidential ordering and explicitly approves searching for this bridge.

## What the bridge must not do

The target is not:

\[
Can(G,a)\leftrightarrow Can(G,a).
\]

Nor may the argument begin with “every possible created order is one $G$ can create” when the conclusion is precisely universal creative range. Three domains must remain independent enough to perform argumentative work:

1. the criterion by which a specification is genuinely metaphysically possible;
2. the transworld identity and essential constitution of $G$;
3. the agent-relative accessibility relation that turns a possible performance into an ability.

## Decompose the target

$OMP_C$ currently compresses several kinds of range:

| Subrange | Question |
| --- | --- |
| creative plenitude $CP_G$ | Can $G$ originate every independently admissible fundamental created specification? |
| constitutive control $CC_G$ | Can $G$ directly vary, replace, suspend, or remove every eligible created constituent or power? |
| history-sensitive intervention $HI_G$ | Given an obtaining history, can $G$ perform every eligible intervention compatible with that fixed past and the task's preservation conditions? |

Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ additionally requires a domain-exhaustion claim: every eligible divine action must either fall within these created-order ranges or within an independently covered class of self-directed or non-created-relational action.

Therefore:

\[
CP_G\not\Rightarrow OMP_C(G)
\]

without further control and intervention premises, and:

\[
OMP_C(G)\not\Rightarrow OMP_Q(G)
\]

without a justified exhaustion of $Task_G$.

## The modal-source bridge

Let $Poss_C^{ind}(S)$ mean that $S$ is a genuinely possible specification for a fundamental created order, with its modal status established without presupposing that $G$ can actualize it.

The candidate bridge has four premises.

### B1 — Independently grounded created possibility

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S).
\]

Logical consistency alone is insufficient under the project's account of metaphysical possibility. The positive ground may involve necessary structural constraints, compossible power-types, or another non-circular modal basis. C48 currently grounds modal knowledge partly in actualizing powers, so it cannot by itself support B1 without revision or finer typing.

### B2 — Modal sole-source invariance

For every possible fundamental created basis, the same necessary $G$ is its ultimate originative source:

\[
\Box\forall \mathcal C
\bigl[FundCreatedBasis(\mathcal C)
\rightarrow OEC^+(G,\mathcal C,Spec(\mathcal C))\bigr].
\]

C04, C05, C80, and C81 concern the preferred actual architecture. B2 strengthens them into a transworld claim. Necessary existence of $G$ alone does not entail it: a world in which $G$ exists without grounding a created basis, or in which another source grounds one, must be excluded by an essential-role or modal-ultimity argument.

### B3 — Essential originative power

The relevant originative competence belongs to $G$'s essential power structure rather than being acquired from a created environment:

\[
Exercise_w(G,P_{OEC},S)
\rightarrow EssentialCapacity(G,P_{OEC},S).
\]

$UPSS$, underivedness, the absence of a prior substrate, and the pre-creative role of $P_{OEC}$ strongly support this premise. It remains important not to infer that every specification parameter is covered merely from the existence of one essential originative power.

### B4 — Agent-relative choice accessibility

The witnessing world must represent an alternative open to the same agent, not merely a metaphysically possible world in which the result happens:

\[
WitnessesAbility(w,G,S)
\leftrightarrow
SameEssentialAgent(w,G)
\land SameNecessaryBase(w)
\land Originates_w(G,S)
\land DifferenceFromActualGroundedIn_w(Settle(G,S)).
\]

This condition fits C97–C111: $G$ apprehends live structures, reasons may leave several eligible, and $G$ supplies the contrastive settlement. Because creation has no prior created history, the shared-base requirement is cleaner here than for later interventions.

### Conditional conclusion

From B1–B4:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)\rightarrow CanCreate(G,S).
\]

Generalizing over independently possible specifications yields:

\[
\forall S
\bigl[Poss_C^{ind}(S)\rightarrow CanCreate(G,S)\bigr],
\]

which is creative plenitude $CP_G$.

The inference is informative because B1 concerns modal eligibility, B2 concerns source identity, B3 concerns essential constitution, and B4 concerns agentive accessibility. None simply repeats $CanCreate(G,S)$ in isolation.

## Why this route is promising

It combines already selected features rather than importing maximal perfection:

- $G$ is necessary and underived;
- the one-ground model is preferred;
- creation is an intentional exercise of $P_{OEC}$ under $S$-specification;
- the divine choice model already requires live alternatives and reason-guided settlement;
- $P_{OEC}$ does not depend on a created patient whose constitution would restrict its output.

It also explains why actual non-exercise is irrelevant. A specification can belong to divine capacity even when $G$ selects another $S^*$ or does not create.

## The principal circularity risk

C48 says that modal knowledge is provisionally grounded partly in $G$'s actualizing powers. If $Poss_C^{ind}(S)$ is then read as “$S$ is possible because $G$ can actualize it,” B1 and the conclusion collapse into one claim.

Two repairs remain live:

1. **typed modal grounding:** structural possibility is grounded by necessary consistency and compossible power profiles, while capacity answers which agent can originate an instance;
2. **mutual-constraint account:** power and possibility are jointly characterized by a fixed point constrained independently by logic, identity, essence, and compossibility rather than defined unidirectionally.

The first is clearer but requires a better account of the metaphysical possibility of uninstantiated power-types. The second may be more realistic but needs formal safeguards against a merely verbal fixed point.

## Specification transparency

A second route treats $P_{OEC}$ as specification-transparent:

\[
Poss_C^{ind}(S)
\land Selects(G,S)
\land P_{OEC}(G)
\rightarrow Produces(G,\mathcal C_S).
\]

The supporting thought is that $P_{OEC}$ originates a bearer without working through a patient, material channel, or created genus. Its effect-type is supplied by $S$, so no independent medium has yet appeared that would restrict the output to one physical profile.

This is useful explanatory support, but it cannot be made part of the definition of $P_{OEC}$ without simply inserting creative plenitude into the power. Actual creation plus broad intervention demonstrates flexibility, not universal specification transparency. The thesis remains an abductive bridge candidate.

## Why absence of a limiter is insufficient

A being can lack a capacity without another entity actively blocking it. A fundamental power may simply possess a restricted manifestation profile. Therefore:

\[
\neg\exists L\,UltimateBlocks(L,G,a)
\not\Rightarrow StrongCan(G,a).
\]

A principle requiring every incapacity to have a deeper explanation would also conflict with allowing $G$'s own fundamental power structure to be an explanatory stopping point. Non-arbitrariness counts in comparison, but it is not a deductive bridge.

## Why the Aquinas route is not yet ours

Aquinas derives unlimited active power from an existence that is unlimited by any receiver or genus and contains the perfection of being. That is a genuine bridge from a prior metaphysics of divine actuality to power range, rather than a definition of omnipotence. The present project has not adopted pure act, exhaustive perfection, identity of essence and existence, or unrestricted plenitude of being. Its useful local analogue is narrower: $P_{OEC}$ is not mediated by created matter or typed to a created causal genus.

Adopting the full argument now would violate C06, C82, and R03. It remains a reference route that could become relevant if later work independently derives stronger divine infinity or perfection.

## Why primitive Infinite Power is not the bridge

Kenny Pearce proposes treating Infinite Power as prior to particular finite powers and analyzes it through perfect freedom and perfect efficacy of will. This honestly makes unrestricted power a fundamental posit rather than deriving it from a list of capacities.

That inversion does not presently fit the project for two reasons:

1. the project is explicitly deriving attributes from the necessary-agent architecture rather than placing omnipotence in the starting concept of God;
2. absolute control over every proposition would threaten the source-role distinction by making creaturely libertarian outcomes contrastively up to $G$.

The route remains an alternative ontology, not evidence for the missing bridge inside this one.

## Extending beyond creation

### Grounding-to-control bridge

To infer constitutive control, the project would need something like:

\[
DirectlyOriginatesConstitution(G,x)
\land AdmissibleVariation(x,m)
\rightarrow CanDirectlyVary(G,x,m).
\]

Grounding alone does not entail this. A source may produce something with autonomous or stable powers it cannot later alter. $P_{OEC}$ plus the intervention taxonomy supplies evidence, but universal re-addressability remains an additional premise.

### History-sensitive bridge

An intervention ability requires a world sharing the relevant obtaining past and preservation conditions in which $G$ performs the intervention. A possible world with a completely different creation is not enough:

\[
PossiblyCreates(G,S')\not\Rightarrow CanIntervene(G,a\mid H_{<t}).
\]

Eternalist truth conditions, identity through history, and creaturely sourcehood further restrict this domain. The shared-history analysis must therefore remain separate from creative plenitude.

### Domain exhaustion for $OMP_Q$

Even complete $OMP_C$ would establish unrestricted $OMP_Q$ only if every eligible divine task is shown to be:

- a creative act;
- an act concerning a created target;
- an exercise of an independently established self-directed essential power; or
- an act concerning a non-created relatum whose ontological status and accessibility are separately established.

No such exhaustive action ontology has yet been demonstrated.

## Status

The bridge search improves the architecture without yet proving $OMP_C$ or $OMP_Q$:

- $OMP_C$ remains preferred at F2;
- $CP_G$ now has a precise conditional derivation target;
- $CC_G$ and $HI_G$ require distinct bridges;
- $OMP_Q$ additionally requires domain exhaustion;
- the modal basis in C48 must be refined before the modal-source bridge can become non-circular.

The next trunk task remains CH03. The power bridge stays on the selective F3 list and should be revisited when the modal ontology or the full action taxonomy is deepened.

## Literature notes

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 25](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1025.htm) derives infinite active power from divine actuality and unlimited being, then extends the range to whatever can have being. The project records the structure without adopting its stronger premises.
- [P. T. Geach, “Omnipotence”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0031819100060381) distinguishes power over all things from ability to do everything. This anticipates the project's need to distinguish created-order sovereignty from unrestricted task range.
- [Kenny Pearce, “Infinite Power and Finite Powers”](https://writings.kennypearce.net/infinitePower.pdf) treats Infinite Power as prior to finite powers and develops perfect freedom plus perfect efficacy. It supplies a clear rival strategy but is not a derivation from the present ontology.

No novelty claim is made for modal ability, shared-history conditions, infinite-power theories, or the distinction between sovereignty and ability to do everything. The potentially useful synthesis is the four-premise modal-source bridge plus the decomposition of universal range into creative plenitude, constitutive control, history-sensitive intervention, and task-domain exhaustion.
