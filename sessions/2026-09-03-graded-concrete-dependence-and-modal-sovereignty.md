# Session: Graded concrete dependence and modal sovereignty

The strongest warranted result is graded: actual concrete dependence $ACD_G$ is preferred as the one-terminus account of actual concrete reality; this excludes an essentially independent necessary concrete conditional on its necessary actuality and stable dependence status; full modal $UCD_G$ remains the cleaner sovereignty completion but requires a modal total-ground or dependence-essentiality bridge not supplied by the actual explanatory argument, so its use in unrestricted omnipotence remains explicitly conditional.

## 1. The scope mismatch

S73 introduced:

\[
UCD_G:=
\Box\forall x[(Concrete(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow Depends_E(x,G)].
\]

Its support was explanatory unity, well-founded dependence, the absence of a positive role for a second concrete terminus, and the costs of plural active sources. Those considerations favor the working ontology over a rival that actually contains another independent concrete. They do not by themselves establish the modal operator $\Box$.

The distinction is not between certainty and uncertainty. It is between two different claims:

1. **actual completeness:** what the concrete termini of actual reality are;
2. **modal sovereignty:** what concrete termini could exist in any admissible reality.

Failure to separate them makes an invalid transition tempting:

\[
\text{no justified actual }L
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\Diamond\exists L\,NCC_G(L).
\]

The weak explanatory preference of S47 makes this especially important. The project did not adopt a strong principle requiring every possible contingent fact or being to have a sufficient explanation. A brute-contingency rival remains epistemically live even when it is comparatively disfavored.

## 2. Actual concrete dependence

Introduce the narrower thesis:

\[
\boxed{
ACD_G:=
\forall x[(Actual(x)\land Concrete(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow Depends_E(x,G)]
}
\]

This is the natural completion of the actual one-terminal-ground model. Its support has two layers:

1. C04 and C73 place every actual contingent concrete within the total contingent reality grounded by $G$.
2. The S13/S73 comparative case favors no additional actual necessary independent concrete: no such item explains matter, modality, consciousness, agency, or a second contingent order within the selected architecture.

The second layer remains abductive. A causally isolated necessary concrete is not contradictory; it is an unexplained additional terminus. An active one incurs further costs of partition, conflict, reciprocal limitation, or brute coordination. A plural model can accept those costs, but it no longer offers the most economical account currently available.

$ACD_G$ says neither that every abstractum depends on $G$ nor that every actual effect is immediately produced by $G$. Concrete secondary causes and autonomous subjects remain derivative real sources within the grounded order.

## 3. The necessary-independent rival

The strongest S73 rival can be typed more strictly as:

\[
ENCC_G(L):=
Concrete(L)\land L\neq G\land
\Box Exists(L)\land
\Box(Exists(L)\rightarrow\neg Depends_E(L,G)).
\]

This is an **essentially independent necessary concrete**, not merely something independent at one history. Under the normal factivity of actuality and necessity:

\[
\Box Exists(L)\rightarrow Actual(L).
\]

Therefore:

\[
ACD_G\rightarrow\neg\exists L\,ENCC_G(L).
\]

The implication is deductive once $ACD_G$ is assumed. The support for $ACD_G$ remains abductive, so the conclusion inherits that epistemic status. This matters: no further modal universal is required to exclude a being whose necessary existence and essential independence already place it in actual reality.

This also shows the limit of several plural-god models. A system of necessarily existing concrete gods is not hidden in a merely non-actual world; if its necessity uses our $S5$-style semantics, it rivals the actual one-terminus ontology directly. Steinhart's ordinal polytheism is coherent only by replacing central parts of this project: it combines many necessary concrete grounds with concrete modal realism, a one-god/one-universe mapping, and no maximally perfect god. It is a systematic rival architecture, not a local counterexample retained inside ours.

## 4. The stronger countermodel to modal sovereignty

The most difficult countermodel is not another necessary god. It is:

\[
\Diamond\exists w\exists b[
Contingent_w(b)\land Concrete_w(b)\land
\neg Depends_{E,w}(b,G)].
\]

Here actual reality still satisfies $ACD_G$, but some non-actual admissible history contains a brute contingent concrete independent of $G$. The model does not multiply actual beings. It instead denies that grounding every contingent concrete is an essential and modally invariant role of $G$.

Three existing claims do not exclude it:

- $G$'s necessary existence places $G$ in that history but does not make every coexistent concrete depend on $G$.
- $P_{OEC}$ establishes a creative capacity, not that nothing can exist without its exercise.
- actual total-contingent grounding does not become modal total-contingent grounding merely by adding $\Box$.

Qualified omnipotence cannot supply the missing bridge because $UCD_G$ is itself being used to close an external branch of omnipotence. Nor can “created” be defined to cover $b$ and then used to prove that $b$ is created.

The countermodel pays a price. It makes existential sovereignty world-variable and admits a brute concrete exception for which no positive role is given. Yet that is a comparative cost, not a contradiction—especially because S47 explicitly leaves brute contingency available as a rival stopping point.

## 5. Decomposing full `UCD_G`

Write:

\[
CTD_G:=
\Box\forall x[(Concrete(x)\land Contingent(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow Depends_E(x,G)]
\]

for **modal contingent-total dependence**, and:

\[
NCD_G:=
\Box\forall x[(Concrete(x)\land Necessary(x)\land x\neq G)
\rightarrow Depends_E(x,G)]
\]

for **modal necessary-concrete dependence**. Given an exhaustive contingent/necessary classification:

\[
UCD_G\leftrightarrow CTD_G\land NCD_G.
\]

$ACD_G$ supports neither conjunct by pure logic. Two bridges are available:

1. **Modal total-ground bridge:** grounding all contingent concrete reality belongs essentially to $G$, so C04 has the scope expressed by $CTD_G$.
2. **Dependence-status bridge:** for a numerically identical concrete, existential origin or independence relative to $G$ cannot vary arbitrarily across histories.

Both fit the architecture, but neither is fully earned. The first strengthens the weak actual explanatory principle into essential sovereignty. The second approaches the origin-essentialism deliberately left open by S45. They must be stated rather than hidden.

## 6. Why the classical routes do not transfer automatically

Aquinas derives universal created dependence from the stronger thesis that God is uniquely subsistent being and other beings possess existence by participation. That supplies exactly the bridge our thinner power-structured agent model lacks. Avicennian routes similarly derive uniqueness from a Necessary Existent whose necessity, non-composition, and mode of being rule out another of the same status. Importing either route would require prior acceptance of pure act, strong simplicity, existence/essence theses, or an equivalent doctrine.

Divine aseity is also insufficient on its own:

\[
\neg Depends_E(G,y)
\not\Rightarrow
\forall x(x\neq G\rightarrow Depends_E(x,G)).
\]

Contemporary accounts therefore distinguish God's independence from progressively stronger doctrines of sovereignty over everything else. That distinction matches the present result: $UPSS$ and underivedness characterize $G$; $UCD_G$ is an additional universal scope claim.

Finally, maximal power must remain downstream. An argument from “another independent being would limit omnipotence” can show tension with an already accepted unrestricted omnipotence, but it cannot establish the modal ontology used to infer that omnipotence without circularity.

## 7. Comparative options

| Option | Main gain | Main cost | Present verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| full $UCD_G$ as a theorem from C04 | immediate modal sovereignty and external power closure | invalidly modalizes an actual/provisional grounding thesis | reject the derivation |
| $ACD_G$ only, with unrestricted modal openness | states only the best-supported actual ontology | leaves world-variable sovereignty and brute possible concreta completely unconstrained | viable austere alternative |
| graded dependence: $ACD_G$ plus conditional $UCD_G$ | preserves actual one-terminus explanation, isolates the modal bridge, and keeps the cleaner sovereignty completion available | unrestricted power and full modal sovereignty remain conditional | preferred |
| classical universal participation | derives every non-divine being from one subsistent source | imports simplicity and being-by-participation not established here | reference route, not adopted |
| plural terminalism | can divide explanatory labor among independent necessary sources | needs a positive division, connecting structure, or brute concordance and weakens single-source ultimity | coherent but disfavored |
| ordinal polytheism | supplies a systematic many-gods/many-worlds architecture | rejects modal actualism, maximal divinity, and common-source creation | genuine rival framework, not a local repair |

The graded view is not merely hesitation. It assigns each consequence to the premise that actually supports it:

- actual creation, providence, knowledge, and intervention use $ACD_G$ and the established created-order relations;
- exclusion of an essentially independent necessary $L$ uses $ACD_G$ plus necessary actuality;
- exclusion of every brute contingent independent possibility uses $CTD_G$;
- unrestricted cross-ontology power closure uses full $UCD_G$ or an independent range bridge for non-created relata.

## 8. Consequences for omnipotence and uniqueness

Ontology-relative maximality is unchanged:

\[
OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}(G)
\]

already ranges over the kinds and relata admitted by the working ontology. Actual $ACD_G$ also preserves existential sovereignty over everything concrete the model says actually exists.

What changes is the description of the cross-ontology result. Full $UCD_G$ may close the distinct-non-created-concrete branch only conditionally:

\[
UCD_G\rightarrow
\neg\Diamond\exists L\,NCC_G(L).
\]

Without $UCD_G$, the prior `NCCBlock` analysis remains correct. A brute possible $b$ defeats unrestricted omnipotence only if a coherent action involving $b$ is independently eligible, $b$ supplies the final underived boundary, and $G$ lacks the relevant strong capacity. Mere modal coexistence is still not a power failure.

Divine-ground uniqueness also survives. $U_G$ and $U_D$ concern the actual total contingent order. What remains conditional is the stronger claim that $G$ is the sole possible concrete terminus in every admissible ontology.

## Adversarial tests

| Objection | Result |
| --- | --- |
| If $G$ grounds every actual contingent concrete, it does so necessarily | Unsupported without an essential-role bridge. A relational grounding scope can vary even when $G$'s intrinsic powers remain essential. |
| A possible non-created concrete must be necessary by S73 | Only if C04's total-ground scope is already applied in that possible history. Using it against a brute-contingent countermodel assumes $CTD_G$. |
| Any necessary independent $L$ is merely possible and need not trouble actuality | False under the adopted necessity semantics: necessary existence entails actuality. $ENCC_G$ rivals $ACD_G$ directly. |
| Parsimony cannot compare necessities or possibilities | Too strong. Theory simplicity and modal uniformity are legitimate abductive virtues, but they yield preference rather than contradiction. |
| A second idle terminus has no effect, so it is harmless | It may be causally harmless but still worsens concrete explanatory completeness. Harmlessness to power and economy of ontology are separate tests. |
| Two active necessary agents must conflict | Not deductively. They may have necessarily concordant wills or divided domains, at the cost of unexplained coordination or partition unless a further ground is supplied. |
| Aseity entails universal sovereignty | Rejected. Independence of $G$ and dependence of everything else have different quantifier directions. |
| Classical theism proves $UCD_G$, so the project may use the result | Only with its stronger being, participation, or simplicity premises. Those arguments remain available for a future premise comparison. |
| Weakening $UCD_G$ destroys omnipotence | False. Created-order and ontology-relative maximality remain; only unrestricted cross-ontology closure retains the already acknowledged condition. |

## Literature and novelty notes

- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 44, aa. 1–2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1044.htm) explicitly argues that every being apart from God is caused by the uniquely subsistent being, including necessary beings and prime matter. The route supplies a real universal-dependence argument, but through participation and divine-simplicity premises not earned here.
- [William E. Mann, “Divine Sovereignty and Aseity” (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199370764.003.0007) distinguishes God's independence from progressively stronger sovereignty claims concerning creatures, spacetime, contingent facts, and necessary truths. This supports treating $UCD_G$ as an added scope thesis rather than a restatement of `UPSS`.
- [Gregory J. Robson, “Reconsidering the Necessary Beings of Aquinas’s Third Way” (2012)](https://doi.org/10.24204/ejpr.v4i1.315) emphasizes the distinct place of derivative necessary beings in the Third Way. It supports the project's separation of necessary existence from underivedness, without establishing the present modal bridge.
- [Eric Steinhart, “On the Plurality of Gods” (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412512000285) constructs necessary concrete grounds paired one-to-one with concrete worlds in an ordinal hierarchy without a maximally perfect god. It is evidence that plural terminalism can be systematic, while also displaying how much of this project's modal and divine architecture it must replace.
- [Jonathan Schaffer, “Monism: The Priority of the Whole” (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1215/00318108-2009-025) separates how many things exist from how many are fundamental. Although his target is the concrete cosmos rather than a divine ground, the distinction reinforces the present focus on terminal dependence instead of existence monism.

Universal dependence, divine sovereignty, necessary existence *ab alio*, and monist/pluralist fundamentality all have substantial precedent. The project-specific contribution remains the graded integration of `ACD`, `ENCC`, `CTD/NCD`, `UCD`, and `NCCBlock` with ontology-relative omnipotence. No independent novelty claim is made.
