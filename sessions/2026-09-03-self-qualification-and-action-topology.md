# Session: Self-qualification and action topology

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Session ID:** S74
**Primary question:** Does an independently motivated divine action kind or qualitative intrinsic mode extend the established range without violating $PP_G/UPSS$ or defining eligibility through power?
**Dependencies:** C55–C60, C75–C76, C223–C231, C255–C265, C283–C296, C420–C433, C451–C455, C492, C559–C600; S32, S34, S44–S45, S71–S73
**Origin:** First trunk priority after S73; the author's parallel work remains outside this session
**Target depth:** F2 action-topology and residual-task audit; no unrestricted modal proof

## Outcome

The established list of cognitive, volitional, addressive, originative, and interventive manifestations does not gain a sixth member merely because divine action receives a new thick description. Loving, forgiving, promising, judging, imagining, enjoying, and responding must first be analyzed into epistemic access, volitional settlement, expression, production, relation, and any contribution made by another subject. Most introduce no new basic action kind.

Action-domain exhaustion becomes clearer when it is organized by the immediate patient or result-bearer rather than by verbs. An eligible divine action either has no distinct patient, has a result in the created domain, concerns a distinct non-created concrete, has $G$ itself as result-bearer, or is merely constrained by an abstractum that is not a causal patient. S72 closes the first class through $FEG_G$; S40–S44 close the second through the created-order ranges; S73 conditionally closes the third through $UCD_G$; and abstracta constrain coherence without receiving causal action. The fourth class is the only structurally distinct residue.

S72's division between an executive intrinsic mode and a new power, part, quality, or substrate was too coarse. A third case is conceptually available: a **contingent non-basic intrinsic mode** that is not merely an aspect of the action token, yet neither replaces $PP_G$ nor adds an essential power, proper part, external bearer, or substrate. A divine act whose immediate result is such a mode is **non-destructive self-qualification**. It is not existential self-causation: the underived subject and its essential powers are presupposed, while only a contingent mode is settled. Under E+, the modal contrast need not be a temporal transition within $G$.

This supplies a well-formed residual task schema but not a positive witness. Affective or phenomenal response is the strongest candidate family. Yet consciousness alone does not entail variable affect, and actuality-indexed subject acquaintance $ASA$ requires qualitative discrimination without requiring $G$ to instantiate the creature's phenomenal token. The actual marker in ACO can likewise be relational or content-indexed rather than a new non-executive intrinsic state. No currently accepted premise therefore establishes a particular $m$ satisfying the schema.

Nor does rational-volitional capacity establish self-qualificatory efficacy. Being able to intend or prefer that one be in mode $m$ does not entail being able to produce $m$ in oneself. $FEG_G$ covers the settlement; a further self-affective range bridge would be needed for the result. Introducing that bridge merely to close omnipotence would be circular.

The novel-kind audit is therefore complete at F2 with a bounded result. No independently established new basic action kind defeats the current range, and ontology-relative $OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}$ remains intact. Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ remains preferred but non-demonstrative. It has an exact residual defeater: an independently established contingent non-basic intrinsic mode, an eligible self-qualificatory task concerning it, and failure of divine strong capacity. Until such a witness is supplied, self-qualification is a live schema rather than an additional power or an actual divine feature.

## 1. Exhaustion by immediate patient topology

For an action token $a$, let $IP(a)$ be its immediate executive patient or result-bearer. The relevant topology is:

\[
\mathcal T_G(a)\in
\{\varnothing,\mathcal D_G^{cr},G,L,Abs\},
\]

where $\mathcal D_G^{cr}$ is the created result domain—including created bearers, events, states, and relations—$L$ is a distinct non-created concrete, and $Abs$ marks an abstract constraint rather than a genuine patient. The classes are:

| Topology | Normal form | Present result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $IP(a)=\varnothing$ | self-executive settlement or manifestation | $SEAdm_G+FEG_G$ |
| $IP(a)\subseteq\mathcal D_G^{cr}$ | root, constitutive, or fixed-prefix created-directed action | $CAN_G$ and its range bridges |
| $IP(a)=L$, $NCC_G(L)$ | non-created-relational action | conditionally absent under $UCD_G$; otherwise `NCCBlock` test |
| $IP(a)=G$ | self-directed result | executive aspects already covered; non-destructive qualitative result isolated below |
| $IP(a)=Abs$ | apparent action on a proposition, number, possibility, or other abstractum | reclassify as content or coherence constraint unless a concrete bearer is identified |

This partition concerns the immediate result, not every intentional object or remote consequence. Addressing a subject may be pure on the divine executive side and mixed when it produces a created manifestation. Creating an order has $G$ as agent but the order as patient or terminus. A thought about a number does not act causally upon the number.

The partition is independent of omnipotence. It follows from identity between agent and patient and from the created/non-created/abstract classification already used by the ontology. It therefore gives a stronger basis for task-domain exhaustion than an indefinitely expandable verb list.

## 2. Thick descriptions do not automatically add basic kinds

Candidate predicates divide as follows:

| Candidate | Decomposition before any new kind is admitted |
| --- | --- |
| imagine or entertain | cognitive representation of content; CH14 governs the represented target's status |
| remember or anticipate | cognitive content with temporal index; inapplicable as temporal acquisition under E+ unless divine temporality is separately added |
| enjoy, grieve, suffer, or feel compassion | phenomenal or affective state; action only if an executive settlement or produced response is separately identified |
| love | orientation or relation plus attention, willing, address, and created-directed action; a felt mode is an additional question |
| forgive, command, promise, or covenant | institutionally thick act built from settlement, address, normative conditions, and sometimes creaturely uptake |
| judge or evaluate | cognition plus reasons-responsive settlement; execution of a consequence is created-directed |
| receive, undergo, or be affected | passive profile, not a divine action merely because it concerns $G$ |
| sustain or cease sustaining | grounding relation or created-directed outcome; it does not add a self-executive kind |

A candidate becomes a new basic action kind only if its token cannot be generated by the established grammar, cannot be reduced to an action plus a state or relation, and has independently stated success conditions. None of the listed thick predicates currently passes that test.

## 3. Correcting the intrinsic-mode partition

Retain S72's executive mode:

\[
ExecMode_G(m,a),
\]

where $m$ is an occurrent intrinsic aspect of an established exercise. Separate three further cases:

1. **Extrinsic or Cambridge mode:** its truth depends on a distinct relatum or effect and adds no intrinsic result to $G$.
2. **Basic reconstitution:** it adds, removes, or replaces an essential power, proper part, substrate, bearer, or identity condition. Loss of $PP_G$ makes the complete same-agent task ineligible; a purported optional essential-power change conflicts with the current $EssentialPowerProfile$.
3. **Contingent non-basic intrinsic mode:** it is a real intrinsic qualification of $G$ but does not alter the basic profile.

Define the third:

\[
\begin{aligned}
CNBIM_G(m):={}&IntrinsicModeOf(m,G)\land Contingent(m)\\
&\land Compatible(m,PP_G)\\
&\land \neg TokenAspectOfEstablishedExercise(m)\\
&\land \neg CambridgeMode(m)\\
&\land \neg AddsEssentialPowerPartSubstrate(m).
\end{aligned}
\]

`Compatible` is not `CanInstantiate`. It is fixed through the independently accepted identity and constitution profile. The last clause blocks a new constitutive basis while avoiding the stronger and unsupported claim that every intrinsic difference is a new basis.

This corrects C582. `QualMode_G` should be the broad genus of non-executive intrinsic qualitative modes; it divides into basic-reconstituting and contingent-non-basic variants rather than identifying every new quality with a new constituent or power.

## 4. Non-destructive self-qualification

Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
SelfQualAdm_G(a,m):={}&ActToken(a)\land AgentOf(a,G)\\
&\land ImmediateResult(a,m)\land CNBIM_G(m)\\
&\land DirectExecSource(G,a)\land Preserves(a,PP_G)\\
&\land ContentAdm(a,m)\land ReasonElig(a)\land JointComp(a,m).
\end{aligned}
\]

This is an action-side eligibility schema. It contains no `Can`, `StrongCan`, $OMP_Q$, or power-relative modal clause.

The action differs from pure self-execution because success includes a result in the agent beyond the executive token. It differs from self-reconstitution because the same underived bearer and essential power profile remain. It differs from being passively affected because $G$ supplies the executive settlement. It differs from existential self-causation because neither $G$'s existence nor its basic constitution is produced.

In an atemporal model:

\[
SelfQualAdm_G(a,m)\not\Rightarrow
\exists t_1<t_2[\neg m(G,t_1)\land m(G,t_2)].
\]

The relevant contingency may be transworld: the same necessary $G$ self-specifies $m$ in one admissible history and does not in another. If a temporal divine mode is later adopted, diachronic transition becomes an additional realization, not part of the present definition.

## 5. Why volition does not close the new range

`FEG_G` may cover intending or settling that $m$ obtain. It does not by itself cover the production of $m$:

\[
StrongCan(G,Intend(m))\not\Rightarrow
StrongCan(G,ProduceInSelf(m)).
\]

This is the self-directed analogue of the distinction between willing an external outcome and being able to realize it. Patient identity removes an external medium but does not prove a self-affective causal route. Deriving the latter from omnipotence would beg the range question.

A possible bridge would be:

\[
SQG_G:\quad
\forall a\forall m[SelfQualAdm_G(a,m)
\rightarrow StrongCan(G,a)].
\]

The project does not adopt $SQG_G$. It needs either:

- a positive instance of divine self-affection from an independently established power;
- a more general agent-causal principle covering self-directed qualitative results; or
- a constitutive account showing that the result is already an executive mode and therefore not a `CNBIM` after all.

Without one of those routes, $SQG_G$ would be a restatement of the desired range.

## 6. Candidate witnesses

### Actuality-indexed knowledge

ACO marks the actual history within the complete modal field. The known actual content varies across histories, but that does not decide whether the variation is intrinsic to $G$, partly extrinsic, or an executive content-aspect. All three interpretations preserve complete knowledge. ACO therefore supplies no `CNBIM` witness by itself.

### Subject acquaintance

$ASA$ is direct, factive, subject-indexed, and qualitatively discriminating. It does not require numerical sharing of a creature's phenomenal token or an isomorphic divine quale. Vicarious phenomenal representation and token sharing were explicitly left stronger and open. Consequently:

\[
ASA(G,s,e,t)\not\Rightarrow
\exists m[CNBIM_G(m)\land RepresentsPhenomenally(m,e)].
\]

### Affect and response

Joy, grief, compassion, suffering, or another affective response could supply a contingent intrinsic mode while preserving $PP_G$. They are conceptually compatible with thin conscious subjecthood, but neither consciousness nor rational agency entails that this divine subject has an affective determinable of the required kind. If externally induced, they also raise passibility and dependence questions; if self-settled, they raise the $SQG_G$ range question. They remain the strongest candidate family, not an accepted witness.

### Love and personal relation

The current architecture can represent love-like action through regard, reasons, volitional orientation, address, and effects. A phenomenal feeling of love would return to the affective candidate. The thick predicate therefore does not independently force a new basic action kind.

## 7. Exact residual defeater

Define:

\[
\begin{aligned}
SQBlock_G(a,m):={}&SelfQualAdm_G(a,m)\\
&\land IndependentlyPossible_G(m)\\
&\land \neg StrongCan(G,a).
\end{aligned}
\]

The second clause requires more than absence of contradiction or imaginative description. The proposed mode must have an independent metaphysical ground or a positive route from the accepted divine profile. Otherwise an ontology could generate arbitrarily many alleged powers by naming ungrounded qualities.

If a witness exists:

\[
\exists a\exists m\,SQBlock_G(a,m)
\rightarrow \neg OMP_Q(G).
\]

No present candidate satisfies all clauses. A state that is merely relational, an executive token aspect, an essential-profile contradiction, a passive affection, or an unspecified quale is not an `SQBlock`.

## 8. Result for the power program

The domain audit now has a structural rather than lexical closure:

\[
PatientTopology_G
=SelfExec\cup CreatedDirected\cup NCCDirected
\cup SelfQual\cup AbstractConstraint.
\]

All established positive tasks fall within the first two classes; proposed $UCD_G$ removes the third; the fifth is not causal action; and the fourth has no independently established positive witness beyond executive modes already covered by S72.

Therefore:

1. $OMP_Q^{\mathcal O_*}$ remains preferred at F2.
2. No new basic divine action kind is established in this audit.
3. The broad `QualMode` category is refined rather than erased.
4. Unrestricted $OMP_Q$ remains preferred but provisional, because open-ended cross-ontology possibility cannot be converted into a demonstration by inventory.
5. Future reopening requires either a concrete `SQBlock`, a successful `NCCBlock`, or an in-ontology counterexample to an existing range bridge.

This closes the current novel-kind priority at the project's normal comparative depth. The next trunk item is the creative-reason residue in C526, ahead of the lateral content/reference remainder of CH14.

## Adversarial tests

### Does the patient partition assume that every action has a patient?

No. $\varnothing$ explicitly covers direct self-executive action without a further patient. Mixed actions may belong to more than one class under different immediate aspects without becoming several tokens.

### Is $G$ a distinct patient in self-qualification?

No. The topology is reflexive: the agent and result-bearer are numerically identical. This is why created-directed control cannot simply be imported.

### Does atemporality make self-qualification incoherent?

No. Temporal change requires before and after; contingent intrinsic difference across admissible histories does not. Whether an atemporal intrinsic accident is ultimately satisfactory remains a constitutive question, not a formal contradiction.

### Does every contingent willing already make $G$ intrinsically different?

Only if willing is treated as intrinsic. Under S72 it is an executive mode already covered. Huneeus's extrinsic-action account is a live comparison showing that even contingent willing need not be modeled as an additional intrinsic state; the project need not decide that issue to isolate `CNBIM`.

### Is a phenomenal mode automatically actional?

No. A mode may obtain passively, constitutively, or as an accompaniment. `SelfQualAdm` additionally requires an act by $G$ whose immediate result is that mode.

### Is inability to grieve a defect of power?

Not unless grief is an independently possible, identity-compatible result and a self-production of it passes `Task_G`. If grief necessarily includes involuntary loss or deficient appraisal, the complete divine task may fail for reasons other than incapacity. That analysis must not be stipulated after the fact.

### Has unrestricted omnipotence been proved by failing to find a witness?

No. Absence of an established defeater raises comparative confidence but is not a universal modal proof. The result is a closed F2 audit with explicit reopening conditions.

## Literature and precedent

- [Robert F. Brown, “Divine Omniscience, Immutability, Aseity and Human Free Will”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500020989) distinguishes immunity from external influence from a subject's possible self-change and argues that atemporal knowledge of autonomous creaturely acts pressures strict immutability. This is direct precedent for separating passibility, self-modification, and actuality-indexed knowledge; the project's patient topology and `CNBIM/SQBlock` machinery are its own integration.
- [Clemente Huneeus, “Divine Simplicity and the Theory of Action”](https://doi.org/10.14428/thl.v9i1.82053) argues that contingent divine willing can be at least partly extrinsic when the agent remains intrinsically the same across different effects. This is a useful rival realization of S72's executive modes and blocks any automatic inference from contingent action to non-executive intrinsic variation.
- [Frank G. Kirkpatrick, “Is the Notion of a Divine Basic Act a Necessary and Sufficient Way of Talking about God's Actions in the World?”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0034412500001475) questions whether all divine actions need be basic. The present decomposition likewise allows thick and mixed actions without multiplying basic capacities.
- [Norman Kretzmann, “Omniscience and Immutability”](https://doi.org/10.2307/2023849) supplies the classic pressure from temporally indexed omniscience to change in the knower. The project's eternalist ACO avoids a changing present-index, while S74 asks the distinct transworld question whether actual-indexed content introduces an intrinsic mode.
- [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 9](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1009.htm) derives strict immutability from actuality and simplicity. Those stronger premises are absent here, so the source is comparison rather than a route available to the project.

The distinctions between basic and non-basic action, intrinsic and Cambridge change, immutability and impassibility, and active and passive response all have substantial precedent. The possible project-specific contribution is the combination of an exhaustive immediate-patient topology with the four-way intrinsic-mode partition and the exact `SQBlock` condition. No broader novelty claim is warranted.
