# Session: Individual onset without a privileged first moment

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Session ID:** S80
**Primary question:** Does the identity account still assume an unjustified moment at which the individual is formed?
**Dependencies:** C435–C456, C506–C519, C653–C654, C664–C668, CH18, CH22, CH24, S45, S53, S55, S79
**Target depth:** corrective F2 pass; remove punctual onset from the identity ground without prematurely choosing a biological or semantic theory of human beginnings

## Result in one sentence

The ontology should not say that identity is formed at a privileged instant: the temporal extension of a bearer may have a first member, only a lower boundary, an extended formation process, or a vague transition, while its individuation is grounded holistically in one complete unified source-bearing career and its atemporal divine constitution, with numerical identity still fundamental.

## 1. What remained wrong after S79

S79 correctly denied that a first stage explains identity, but it continued to speak as if every created individual must possess an earliest temporal cross-section at which a fully delimited bearer appears. That assumption has not been earned.

Let

\[
L_x:=\{t\in T:ExistsAt(x,t)\}.
\]

Even if $L_x$ has a lower bound $b_x$, it need not have a minimum:

\[
b_x=\inf L_x
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
b_x\in L_x.
\]

A dense temporal order permits, for example, $L_x=(b_x,d_x]$. In that case the individual has an onset boundary but no first instant of existence. Nor does temporal topology settle constitution. A bearer may be produced through an extended integrating process, and it remains open whether there is a sharp but unknown cutoff, genuine ontic vagueness, semantic indeterminacy in the sortal, or no unique cutoff at all.

Consequently, even the corrected phrase “earliest temporal cross-section” was too strong as a general claim.

## 2. Four questions that must not be collapsed

| Axis | Question | Live answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| temporal topology | Does $L_x$ contain its lower boundary? | first member; infimum only; no lower boundary |
| formation dynamics | How is organized unity realized? | punctually; through an extended process; through overlapping processes at different scales |
| determinacy | Is there a precise fact at every boundary time? | sharp but perhaps unknowable; semantically vague; ontically vague |
| bearer kind | What is beginning? | organism; conscious subject; agentive capacity; person-phase |

The last distinction is already important to the project. It provisionally treats the pre-conscious organism and the later conscious person as one bearer. Therefore the onset of consciousness or person-level agency cannot simply be substituted for the onset of the organism. Neither biology nor ordinary language supplies that metaphysical identification without a bridge argument.

The table records a space of options rather than adopting metaphysical vagueness. In particular, an ontically vague bearer boundary would have to be reconciled with the project's absolute and determinate numerical identity; a semantic or epistemic account may preserve that identity more easily. No choice is needed merely to withdraw the first-instant assumption.

## 3. Replace the punctual origin anchor with an origin structure

An origin **anchor** sounded like one event at one time. Replace it with an origin structure $\Omega_x$:

\[
\Omega_x=
\langle Lineage_x,Dependence_x,Integration_x,BoundaryShape_x\rangle.
\]

$\Omega_x$ is the concrete, token-specific way in which the career is connected to prior processes and becomes an integrated bearer. It may include an interval, several converging processes, or a branching lineage. Not every item in $\Omega_x$ is thereby a temporal part of $x$.

This matters in fission and fusion cases. A developmental lineage may branch before there are two definite bearers, or two lineages may contribute to one later bearer. Numerical identity itself does not branch or merge under the project's absolute identity relation. The model must say that the earlier bearer continues as at most one successor, ceases, or was not yet one of the later individuals; it cannot make one thing numerically identical with two. The biological facts constrain that choice but do not make it automatically.

## 4. Holistic constitutive token individuation

The corrected proposal is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
CTI_G^{\Omega}(x,\Omega_x,H_x):={}&UnifiedBearerCareer(x,H_x)\\
&\land OriginLinked(x,\Omega_x,H_x)\\
&\land CreatedOther(x,G)\\
&\land OwnSourceRole(x)\\
&\land IdentityNonBranching(x,H_x).
\end{aligned}
\]

The important direction is holistic. A first stage does not generate the identity of all later stages. Rather, one complete history contains one integrated source-bearing career; $\Omega_x$ relates that career to the processes from which it arises; and the atemporal creative or constitutive relation grounds the bearer with that entire temporal extent. Internally, unity may be progressively realized. Externally, $G$ does not wait for a formation instant and then add identity.

“Holistic” here describes grounding scope, not a decision between endurance and perdurance. An enduring continuant can have its complete career constituted tenselessly without being identified with a four-dimensional temporal worm.

This does not turn the complete career into a reductive criterion. `UnifiedBearerCareer(x,H_x)` already attributes the career to $x$, and deciding which borderline processes belong to it can require the numerical identity fact. The corrected result is therefore:

\[
CTI_G^{\Omega}
\text{ constrains unity, origin, and ordinary persistence,}
\qquad =_N\text{ remains fundamental.}
\]

The gain is narrower but real: no point event, fertilization date, first conscious episode, remembered experience, or divine act of recognition is made the identity-maker.

## 5. What eternalism contributes—and what it does not

E+ permits the complete career to be one tenseless constituent of actual history. This supports a whole-career ground without backward causation: later stages do not reach back and create the earlier individual, and the earlier boundary does not project identity forward. The whole identity fact obtains tenselessly.

E+ does not decide whether onset is sharp, vague, processual, open-boundary, or beginningless. It also does not make the creature atemporal or existent before its life. The three levels remain:

1. a temporal realization pattern $L_x$ and perhaps an onset process inside creation;
2. a tenseless fact that the admitted stages or processes belong to one bearer;
3. an atemporal relation by which $G$ constitutes or grounds that entire bearer-career.

Under principal $ANDC$, this increases rather than removes the finite-determination burden. The same undivided $G$ may constitute many creatures, so the model must explain how $\Phi_x$, $\Omega_x$, and $H_x$ jointly determine one created bearer without requiring a punctual divine modification or collapsing the creature into $G$.

## 6. Human onset remains a real open problem

The architecture can now state what an answer must distinguish, but it does not yet decide when a human organism, conscious subject, or person begins. Fertilization, compaction, implantation, primitive-streak formation, first consciousness, and stable agentive capacity answer different biological or normative questions. Twinning and fusion are genuine stress tests, although the literature disputes whether they rule out early embryonic individuality rather than merely complicate its persistence.

Science is informative here because it describes the relevant developmental organization and causal lineage. It cannot alone select the metaphysical bearer kind or decide whether boundary vagueness is semantic or ontic. Conversely, the ontology should not block biological evidence by defining the answer in advance.

## Literature note

- [Nicholson and Dupré, *Everything Flows*](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198779636.001.0001) develops the established process-ontological family in which living individuals are dynamically maintained processes and their boundaries may be non-sharp. This supplies a comparison family, not a premise imposed on the project.
- [Louis Guenin, “The Nonindividuation Argument Against Zygotic Personhood”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0031819106317044) tests and rejects the claim that pre-twinning divisibility straightforwardly proves the early embryo is not an individual.
- [Marc Ramsay, “Twinning and Fusion as Arguments against the Moral Standing of the Early Human Embryo”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0953820811000057) likewise shows that the metaphysical significance of fission and fusion is disputed. These sources support C675's restraint; they do not settle the project's onset question.

No novelty claim is made. The correction removes an unearned punctual assumption and makes the identity account compatible with substance, process, sharp-boundary, and vague-boundary developments until the bearer ontology earns a narrower choice.
