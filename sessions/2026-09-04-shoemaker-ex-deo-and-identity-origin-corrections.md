## Result in one sentence

Shoemaker does not challenge the claim that a material temporal world needs change somewhere in its history; the author's comparison with nothing favors genuine ontological continuity from $G$ and therefore promotes non-destructive *ex deo* from a modal side case to the principal creation hypothesis; and “the first stage of a career” is demoted to a temporal boundary because the identity-bearing work belongs to the origin of one unified token source and its one non-branching history, with numerical identity still an explicit irreducible remainder.

## 1. What S78 got wrong

Three errors were different in kind.

1. **Wrong target.** S78 answered a linguistic claim about reifying Nothing. The author's point was a comparative metaphysical preference: a necessary positive source, and especially production with ontological continuity *ex deo*, is more intelligible than disjoint production *ex nihilo*.
2. **Wrong scope.** S78 invoked Shoemaker as a reason to leave the whole-history dependence of time on change open. His construction presupposes a world with abundant change and targets a much stronger claim about every temporal interval.
3. **Non-answer.** “The first stage of the actual token career” locates an earliest temporal boundary. It does not say what individuates the bearer or makes later stages stages of that bearer. The phrase also introduced needless jargon into an answer that needed positive metaphysics.

The corrections below preserve distinctions that remain sound while withdrawing the uses made of them.

## 2. Shoemaker: three theses, one actual target

Let $Hist(S)$ be the complete history of created order $S$, $I$ a non-zero interval, and $r$ a region.

### Whole-history dynamicity

\[
WCh(S):=MaterialTemporal(S)\rightarrow
\exists e\,[Change(e)\land OccursIn(e,Hist(S))].
\]

This says that a material temporal world has some change somewhere in its complete history. It does not say that everything changes, that change is continuous, or that every interval contains change.

### Interval density of change

\[
ICh(S):=\forall I\,[Elapsed(I,S)\rightarrow
\exists e\,(Change(e)\land OccursDuring(e,I))].
\]

This says that no non-zero interval is wholly changeless.

### Local temporal change

\[
LCh(S):=\forall r\forall I\,[TemporalRegion(r,I,S)\rightarrow
\exists e\,(Change(e)\land OccursIn(e,r,I))].
\]

Shoemaker's local freezes immediately deny $LCh$. His synchronized-freeze inference is intended to deny $ICh$: during the alleged total freeze there is no intrinsic change anywhere. Yet the construction still satisfies $WCh$. Changes before and after the freeze, alternating freezes, records, clocks, predictions, and observations are indispensable to the thought experiment. The example therefore cannot be cited against $WCh$.

That does not make the example literally pointless. Its proper question is whether a finite changeless interval can be embedded between changing intervals and still possess a determinate duration. Even there the result is conditional: the inhabitants infer the total freeze from the surrounding pattern, while a relationist may object that the inference already assumes the absolute temporal structure in dispute. The project need not settle $ICh$ to answer the author's global point.

### Current position

The project now prefers $WCh$ at F2 for a material temporal order. The positive reason is relationist and economical: if a purported world has no qualitative, causal, relational, or organizational difference anywhere in its whole history, adding temporal coordinates contributes no internally realized before/after structure. A substantivalist can stipulate an empty metric duration, so this is not advertised as a framework-neutral logical proof. It is the preferred ontology of created time.

Nothing about $WCh$ entails that a living subject dies. Change minimally entails the replacement, acquisition, or loss of some state; death is the loss of the integrated life of a bearer, and evil is loss or corruption of a due good. Those require further premises. Structural vulnerability remains useful, but it cannot be promoted into universal mortality or token theodicy.

## 3. What the comparison with nothing was doing

The valid grammatical clarification remains:

\[
Nothing
\]

does not name a material, entity, or cause. It follows neither that atheists worship Nothing nor that theism has been demonstrated. The relevant non-theist competitors remain brute actuality, impersonal necessity, plenitude, or suspension of judgment.

But that was not the author's requested conclusion. The intended comparison has two steps:

1. a positive necessary agent is preferable to absolute absence or an unexplained brute terminus as the source-level account;
2. given $G$, creation with real continuity from $G$ is preferable to a wholly disjoint new concrete substance whose production terminates in the primitive operation of $P_{OEC}$.

The second is a comparison between two theistic production models. The project has accumulated exactly the reasons that make the author's preference intelligible:

| Model | Explanatory gain | Principal cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| constitutively disjoint $OEC^+_{CCD}$ | sharp creator–creature boundary and simple source separation | primitive cross-category production of a new bearer with no constitutive continuity |
| non-destructive strict *ex deo* through $ANDC$ | positive ontological continuity, immediate immanence, and no null or co-fundamental substrate | must preserve $G$, individuate the creature, block property and agency collapse, and state what constitution adds beyond causation |

The author now ranks the second above the first. Accordingly:

\[
\mathsf{ActualCreationModel}_G(\mathcal C_0)
:=ANDC(G,\mathcal C_0)
\quad\text{(provisional).}
\]

is the **principal working hypothesis**, while $OEC^+_{CCD}$ becomes the live fallback. This is a preference under active construction, not a declaration that every $ANDC$ burden is discharged.

There is a terminological caution. In much classical literature *ex nihilo* merely denies an independently pre-existing material and can coexist with causal, exemplar, participatory, or emanative “from God.” In this project, the author's rejection of *ex nihilo* is interpreted more strongly: rejection of **constitutively disjoint production** in favor of real divine–creature ontological continuity. Otherwise the stated preference would collapse into a verbal difference, since $OEC^+$ already had $G$ as efficient and exemplar source.

## 4. “First stage” translated, and why it was insufficient

A temporal career is just the stretch of a thing's existence through time. Its “first stage” means its earliest temporal cross-section: the first time at which that bearer exists. For a human organism this might eventually be assigned a biological threshold, but the ontology has not fixed it.

That answers only:

> When is the earliest time at which $x$ exists?

It does not answer:

> What makes there be this one $x$, rather than no bearer, two bearers, or another qualitatively identical bearer?

C653 is therefore retained only as a boundary statement and superseded as an explanation of identity.

## 5. A positive account of where created identity comes from

Four items must do different work:

| Item | Work |
| --- | --- |
| kind or profile $K$ | what sort of being can exist and which changes it can survive |
| token organization $U_x$ | why there is one integrated bearer at a time rather than an arbitrary collection |
| origin anchor $O_x$ | the non-repeatable originating constitution of this bearer rather than a qualitative copy |
| numerical identity $=_N$ | the fact that the bearer at two stages is literally one and the same |

The positive proposal is **constitutive token individuation**:

\[
\begin{aligned}
CTI_G(x,O_x,H):={}&OriginatesAsOne(x,O_x,H)\\
&\land UnifiedPowerBearer(x,O_x)\\
&\land CreatedOther(x,G)\\
&\land OwnSourceRole(x)\\
&\land OneNonBranchingCareer(x,H).
\end{aligned}
\]

The origin anchor is not a date or a description. It is the concrete event or boundary at which one new unified center of powers is constituted, with this dependence history and this immanent causal-organizational trajectory. A perfect duplicate has another origin anchor even if every qualitative predicate matches. The organization explains the one bearer; the anchor distinguishes its token origin; the non-branching immanent history carries that unity through ordinary change.

This is more informative than “$x$ is $x$,” but it is not a reductive definition of numerical identity. Any alleged reduction using “same origin,” “same organization,” or “same career” already needs criteria for which token and which career are the same. The irreducible remainder is now located exactly:

\[
CTI_G\text{ explains individuation and ordinary persistence constraints,}
\quad =_N\text{ remains fundamental.}
\]

That stopping point is a metaphysical claim, not an answer withheld. It says that identity is not made from information, matter, memory, recognition, or a detachable identity-object. A being becomes an individual in the same constitutive achievement by which one bearer comes to exist; identity is not manufactured in a second operation.

## 6. Is identity temporal or eternal?

The answer depends on what is being called identity.

| Question | Answer under E+ |
| --- | --- |
| When does the bearer exist? | only at the created times in its life-set, beginning at its earliest boundary and perhaps ending or containing a gap |
| When is its unity first physically realized? | at that earliest boundary, when the unified token bearer exists |
| Is the complete fact that these stages are one bearer tensed? | no; it is part of the complete actual history and is tenselessly true |
| Is there an eternal identity-object or pre-existing person? | not in the current model |
| What grounds the whole career? | one atemporal divine creative/constitutive relation to the temporally extended bearer |

Thus the creature is temporal; the complete identity fact is tenseless; and the divine grounding relation is atemporal. None transfers its mode to the other. From inside the history, the individual begins. From the external atemporal standpoint, the complete bounded history is grounded without first waiting for the individual to begin.

An eternal haecceity or individual idea in $G$ is a possible stronger model, but it adds precisely the entity-like identity carrier the current account does not need. Under strict *ex deo*, $G$ itself is eternal and may constitute $x$, but $G$ alone cannot individuate $x$: the same undivided $G$ may constitute many creatures. Creaturely identity therefore also requires the token-specific created determination, origin anchor, unity, and source role. Identifying the creature with the eternal divine constituent would erase created otherness.

Across a genuine interval of non-existence, $CTI_G$ no longer supplies continuous immanent propagation. S45's gappy identity remains coherent only through one complete non-branching identity fact, or through some stronger identity anchor not yet earned. The present correction therefore strengthens ordinary origin and persistence while openly leaving the deepest recreation ground primitive.

## 7. How identity sharpens the next $ANDC$ step

The principal *ex deo* model cannot say merely that $G$ causes and sustains $x$. It must contain:

\[
ANDC^*(G,x,\Phi_x):=
ConstitutesWhole(G,x)
\land FiniteDetermination(\Phi_x,x)
\land CTI_G(x,O_x,H)
\land Pres_G
\land SourceSep(G,x).
\]

$G$ supplies the underived constitutive actuality; $\Phi_x$ supplies the finite kind and organization under which the creature exists; $CTI_G$ supplies the creature's token unity and origin; and `SourceSep` prevents creaturely decisions from becoming divine decisions. $\Phi_x$ is not a second co-fundamental substance or a stored possible individual.

This exposes the exact next fork:

1. if $\Phi_x$ is only a feature of $x$ resulting from an external causal relation, `ConstitutesWhole` risks collapsing back into existential grounding;
2. if $\Phi_x$ is an intrinsic new mode of $G$, the model owes an account of contingent divine qualification, property inheritance, and why created agency remains genuinely other;
3. if constitution is a primitive building relation with typed non-inheritance, the project must justify its consequences strongly enough that it is more than renamed causation.

The next priority remains $ANDC$, now focused on this three-way fork. Identity has not solved it; it has shown what any solution must individuate.

## Literature note

- [Sydney Shoemaker, “Time Without Change”](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2023892) states the disputed thesis as the claim that something changes during any interval and builds the synchronized-freeze case to support belief in intervals with no change anywhere. This verifies C661 and shows why S78's whole-history use was mistaken.
- [Denis Corish, “Could Time be Change?”](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0031819109000199) argues that Shoemaker presupposes an effectively Newtonian absolute time where the relationist need not grant it. This keeps the interval-level result disputed without weakening $WCh$.
- [Daniel Soars, “Creation in Aquinas: *ex nihilo* or *ex deo*?”](https://doi.org/10.1111/nbfr.12603) argues that the two expressions need not be contradictory when *ex nihilo* denies an independent pre-existing material and *ex deo* marks procession from God. The project therefore states its stronger use explicitly: the author's preference concerns genuine constitutive continuity, not merely the causal phrase “from God.”

No novelty claim is made. The contribution of this correction is architectural: it aligns the scope of a standard thought experiment, the creation-model ranking, and the identity burden with the author's actual direction.
