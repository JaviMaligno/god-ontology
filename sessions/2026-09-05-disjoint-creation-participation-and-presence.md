## Starting position and authorial direction

The author finds the first three readings from S84 very acceptable: origin and dependence, derived resemblance or participation, and immediate personal presence. Literal divine constitution remains unconvincing and dispensable. The author now leans toward disjoint creation and requests an economical alternative that preserves any worthwhile ex-deo benefits.

Strict ex deo becomes a live non-preferred conditional alternative; its possible capacity and formal work are retained. This updates S84's open overall ranking without cancelling its comparative method. No new doctrine of universal exercised communion, phenomenal sharing, or divine goodness follows from the author's endorsement of the three relational readings.

## Proposed answer

Prefer **disjoint creation with participation and presence**. This is a synthesis within the disjoint branch, not a third answer to whether the same divine reality is a constituent of a given creature in a given respect. It combines the existing OEC/CCD, causal and exemplar derivation, finite sourcehood, relational immanence, ASA, and personal availability. No new substance or primitive overlap relation is needed for this integration. This economy is relative to the project's accepted framework, not a claim that those earlier premises have no cost.

## Argument from the existing commitments

1. Under C105–C106, the preferred creative profile favors actual finite others with their own reason-guided sourcehood. C113 leaves God's uniquely actual ultimate motive unknown.
2. Under C245–C246 and C254, originating and specifying a created bearer and its capacities need not transfer a numerically divine constituent. Under C248, disjointness preserves dependence and analogous participation.
3. Under C242, C252, and C284–C296, immediate grounding, subject-sensitive knowledge, personal availability, and possible reciprocal communication do not require shared constitution. Actual reciprocal communication requires creaturely participation, not merely standing availability.
4. S83–S84 established no explanatory advantage of overlap for the finite phenomenal bridge, numerical identity, or personal continuation. C695 remains a limit on inference, not a denial of those possibilities.
5. The author no longer requires literal constitutive continuity as a preferred explanatory endpoint.

**Conclusion:** removing the overlap requirement while retaining the established origin, finite powers, and personal relations preserves the identified benefits with fewer current unsupported commitments. The conditional compatibility inference follows from the listed premises; preferring this synthesis is an abductive and authorial judgment. It neither derives production from the definition of God nor closes the remaining realization problems.

## What participation means here

Participation is an abbreviation for genuine derivation together with a specified finite analogy, not an independently acting tie. The analogy must name a role borne by each subject: knowledge is directed at truth; agentive sourcehood concerns acting for reasons and contributing a settlement. The divine and creaturely realizations need not be identical mechanisms or share a numerically identical power. This says more than resemblance in a picture: the creature really exercises the relevant capacity.

Scope is restricted. Origin from God does not make every finite feature an image of a divine feature; error, bodily limitation, pain, and wrongdoing are not automatically divine predicates. A shared capacity description does not entail equal range, infallibility, goodness of every exercise, or necessary divine attributes beyond those already supported. A particular stronger analogy needs its own argument.

The exemplar role uses the known generative profile and its admissible finite powers. It introduces no concrete possible individuals, pre-existing souls, independent archetype realm, or exact predetermination of a creature's career. No universal requirement that God create is inferred from the preferred reasons.

Literal constitutive continuity is not recovered, because the proposed model denies it. That is the precise abandoned claim. Divine co-suffering, immortal divine fragments, automatic salvation, and complete phenomenal explanation were never established benefits to recover.

## Other candidate routes and their costs

**Divine action in a creature's life:** compatible with CCD when God addresses, informs, heals, or helps a creature through the existing intervention grammar. A real divine act with a created target does not thereby make the act a constituent of the target. If a proposal instead makes a numerically divine mode constitute the creature, it reopens strict overlap. Additional actual interventions need their own warrant; they are not introduced as a universal mechanism for every finite act.

**A shared created spiritual medium or additional soul-substance:** if distinct from God, it remains on the disjoint side and must justify its added entity, individuation, and causal work. It is not needed to secure the first three readings. This is a cost objection, not a proof that every such realization is impossible, and it does not overturn the existing open mind–body alternatives.

**A single divine power literally operating as all finite powers:** if this identifies creaturely settlements with divine settlements, it fails source separation. If the phrase instead means that God grants genuinely distinct finite powers, it is already the proposed participatory disjoint model. Semantic ambiguity cannot obtain overlap's intimacy while avoiding its commitments.

## Matched agency check

For a creature choosing A rather than B, distinguish: God originates and grounds the capacity; finite circumstances and reasons condition its exercise; the creature contributes the contrastive settlement. Being dependent does not mean that one's exercise is numerically another subject's exercise. This preserves the inherited source distinction but is not a new proof that such dependent sourcehood is metaphysically possible.

The next constructive burden is therefore disjoint production of genuine finite powers and the adequacy of that fundamental-power stopping point. Participation clarifies what is produced and how its role relates to the source; it does not provide the still-missing production mechanism. Strict ex deo remains a comparator if a positive bridge later does better, not a standing requirement to keep both branches equally preferred or equally developed.
