## What “necessary, not sufficient” means

1. God's necessary existence is not by itself sufficient for this contingent world: the creative exercise is not identical to necessary existence.
2. Divine grounding and the conditions that enable a free creaturely act do not by themselves sufficiently determine that act's contrastive settlement.
3. This does not mean that no divine exercise can be sufficient for an eligible effect. Producing a bearer or a capacity can have a determinate result without fixing every later free use of it.
4. It does not mean that every conscious state must be freely selected. C135 already allows determined phenomenal episodes; feeling and choosing are distinct roles.

These are scope clarifications of accepted premises. No new gap in agent causation is asserted. The causal and modal details of S86 remain available only if a later concrete challenge needs them.

## Creative power: what was and was not a problem

The familiar explanatory question “how?” can ask either which source and exercise produce the effect, or what deeper mechanism constitutes that exercise. The project answers the former by OEC and accepts the latter as a stopping point at its current depth. A demand that production reduce to prior material transformations would require an additional argument, rather than following from the word explanation.

The remaining caution is only that defining or naming an originative power does not prove its existence. The project adopts it as part of its independently motivated, preferred creator hypothesis. Its inference remains abductive and conditional, but it is not reopened merely for lacking a reduction. The author has accepted that sort of limit. No current defeater has been supplied in this exchange.

## God's role and the embodied account are different explanatory levels

The strongest economical candidate combines:

- **Ultimate productive ground:** God actualizes a created order containing real finite phenomenal powers. His own consciousness makes consciousness present in the ultimate explanatory basis; this is already compatible with disjoint creation.
- **Creature-intrinsic realization:** one finite embodied subject has and manifests those powers through its own organization. A subject-level dual-aspect account might deepen what that organization is, without supplying a second person or identifying its experience with God's.

Thus divine grounding and a dual-aspect realization account are not exclusive alternatives. The latter competes with other accounts at the realization level; each candidate must also be assessed for its integration with the divine source. No generic claim that God creates the subject determines which realization theory is true.

## Minimal constructive candidate

Propose a **divinely actualized psychophysical power profile**: within an independently admissible created order, the real constitution of certain unified embodied bearers includes finite phenomenal powers, and appropriate manifestations belong to those very bearers. God originates the order and its powers; ordinary creaturely processes realize capacities and episodes under the existing GSDA distribution.

This is more specific than generic dependence because it identifies a candidate explanatory terminus: a created power-bearing organization with both bodily and phenomenal roles. It is still a hypothesis about that terminus, not an explanation of why a particular organization satisfies it. Its positive bridge must eventually specify:

| Requirement | Work the bridge must do |
| --- | --- |
| bearer | distinguish one subject from an arbitrary aggregate without defining a subject merely as whatever is conscious |
| capacity and manifestation | distinguish having the capacity from a conscious episode occurring |
| perspective and content | assign a finite first-person field to this bearer, not merely functional outputs or a universal divine perspective |
| causal role | integrate experience with the subject's own powers without independent duplicate causes or a universal divine source override |

The profile must not define its physical eligibility solely by conscious report or by the experience it is supposed to explain. No numerical threshold or actual biological law is supplied here. C333's need for a psychophysical principle and an independently constrained realization profile remains.

God's being conscious does not entail that every creature is conscious, determine finite phenomenal character, or solve why there are many finite perspectives. The proposed gain is an integrated conscious source of finite conscious powers, assessed abductively. The finite-realization bridge still needs content. A rival can agree that a conscious creator exists yet claim that the extra psychophysical profile only relocates the unexplained relation; that objection remains the immediate test.

## Constraints and alternatives

**Disjointness:** the experience is the creature's. No divine phenomenal token is divided, transferred, or made a constituent. God's subject acquaintance is a different epistemic relation.

**Ordinary causation:** divine actualization of a power profile is not a new divine intervention at each episode. Directly supplying every ordinary experience would change the current GSDA distribution and require an additional argument. No such revision is proposed.

**Modality:** an actual created psychophysical profile is not whatever God declares logically possible. It must satisfy independent constitutive admissibility. Neither divine will nor the mere presence of divine consciousness settles all necessary psychophysical truths. A non-volitional divine-essential ground of that modal domain remains the distinct open C652 question; this proposal does not silently answer it.

**Agency:** having or undergoing an experience need not be a free act. The agentive subset must still preserve creaturely settlement. An account that determines an episode's content does not automatically refute all freedom, whereas one that sufficiently determines every choice would conflict with C31–C34.

The current qualified phenomenal-power primitive remains the baseline. The dual-aspect candidate is tested for a positive explanation of bearer unity and phenomenal realization, not selected simply because it is called deeper. A separately created mental substance would remain disjoint but would owe the already-recorded embodiment and individuation costs; no return to that branch is required here.
