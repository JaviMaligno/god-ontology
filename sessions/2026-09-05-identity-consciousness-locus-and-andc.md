# Session: The locus of identity and consciousness inside principal ANDC

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Session ID:** S82  
**Primary question:** Where do identity and consciousness “live,” and what does that imply for the finite determination of a creature under principal $ANDC$?  
**Dependencies:** C212–C220, C310–C341, C435–C456, C506–C519, C559–C572, C660, C668–C676, S29, S36–S37, S45, S53, S55, S71, S79–S81  
**Target depth:** F1 clarification with local F2 integration into the active creation fork

## Result in one sentence

“Where does it live?” must be split into spatial location, bearer, realization base, and ontological ground: numerical identity is not an extra located ingredient, human consciousness is temporally and bodily realized by the one embodied subject without yet being reduced to matter, and the best current $ANDC$ route combines a relation on $G$'s side, a finite form intrinsic to the creature, and a primitive typed constitution bridge whose exact career is partly co-produced by the creature.

## 1. Four different locus questions

For any feature $q$ of a subject $x$, asking where $q$ lives can mean:

| Question | Relation sought |
| --- | --- |
| spatial location | in which region or bodily process is a token realized? |
| bearer | which subject has the feature or undergoes the episode? |
| realization base | which organization, powers, and processes manifest it? |
| ontological ground | what ultimately makes that bearer, power, or fact obtain? |

An answer to one does not settle the others. A conscious episode can be spatially and temporally realized through a body while its phenomenal character remains non-reduced. An identity fact can be true of a spatial bearer without itself being another object located beside that bearer.

## 2. Identity does not add a hidden component

The material/immaterial contrast applies most directly to substances, bearers, constituents, and realization bases. Numerical identity is instead the fundamental fact or relation that two references concern one individual. On the present account it is neither a physical part nor a separable immaterial substance. Calling it “immaterial” would misleadingly turn a logical-metaphysical fact into another occupant of the person; calling it “material” would misleadingly identify being one subject with some repeatable material profile.

The human bearer is actually embodied. Its organization, origin structure, and one non-branching career constrain individuation and ordinary persistence. The deepest identity fact remains fundamental. This leaves no eternal identity-object waiting before the subject begins: the bearer's career is temporal, the complete identity fact is tenseless, and its divine ground is atemporal.

## 3. Consciousness is embodied without a completed reduction

The current working model already locates the relevant roles:

- the bearer is the one embodied subject $A$;
- a token conscious episode $c$ occurs during a temporal bodily process;
- the realization base includes neural, physiological, affective, sensorimotor, and wider organismic organization;
- the ultimate phenomenal bridge remains a qualified primitive, with a subject-level dual-aspect account the strongest deeper candidate.

Thus “bodily realized” is preferred, while “nothing but matter described physically” and “a second immaterial person inside the body” are not. The former remains live but non-preferred because it owes an account of phenomenal subjectivity, unified sourcehood, and simple persistence; the latter remains coherent but disfavored because it owes individuation, embodiment, causal interaction, and a reason not to duplicate the agent.

Something non-spatial can belong to a material-world subject without being contained at a coordinate if the relevant tie is constitution, power, or grounding rather than spatial inclusion. That possibility does not solve interaction. A separately acting immaterial substance would still need a causal bridge to bodily events. The one-bearer powers model instead treats physical and phenomenal descriptions as aspects or roles of one subject's process; whether that non-competition can be deepened without primitiveness remains open.

## 4. Consequence for the active ANDC fork

The locus distinction suggests that the relational, intrinsic, and primitive options are not exhaustive rivals. The best current candidate is a constrained combination.

Let the creaturely finite profile be:

\[
\Phi_x^C=
\langle K_x,U_x,F_x,Cap_x,\Omega_x,AdmCareer_x\rangle.
\]

$\Phi_x^C$ is a representation, not a further constituent. The properties it represents are instantiated intrinsically by $x$, not by $G$. It fixes the created kind, synchronic unity, finite organizing form, power profile, origin structure, and constraints on an admissible career. It does not fix the creature's exact contrastive settlements:

\[
\Phi_x^C\rightarrow AdmCareer_x(H),
\qquad
\Phi_x^C\not\rightarrow H=H_x^*.
\]

The exact career is actual through the joint contribution of root creation, secondary causes, circumstances, and the creature's own settlements. Whole-career $CTI_G^{\Omega}$ can therefore ground one actual bearer tenselessly without converting $G$'s contribution into a prior script of that bearer's acts.

Refine the candidate as:

\[
\begin{aligned}
ANDC^{\Phi}(G,x):={}&ANDC(G,x)\\
&\land FiniteFormOf(\Phi_x^C,x)\\
&\land InstantiatesProfile(x,\Phi_x^C)\\
&\land\neg InstantiatesProfile(G,\Phi_x^C)\\
&\land ConstitutivelyEssentialTo(G,x).
\end{aligned}
\]

The constitution relation is relational on $G$'s side: creation adds no intrinsic divine mode and preserves $PP_G$. The finite determination is intrinsic on the creature's side: $x$ genuinely has its own organization and powers. The bridge remains primitive and typed at this depth. To have content beyond ordinary existential grounding, `ConstitutivelyEssentialTo(G,x)` says that $G$ is a non-replaceable whole constituent of this bearer, without being a transferable part and without making $x$ a part or mode of $G$. Neither $G$ alone nor a repeatable $\Phi_x^C$ alone individuates $x$.

This proposal has a clear cost. The primitive bridge still needs a deeper account of “whole constituent,” and if no counterfactual or explanatory consequence distinguishes it from grounding, $ANDC^{\Phi}$ collapses back into verbal relabeling and $OEC^+_{CCD}$ regains the advantage. Its current gain is that it locates the three jobs separately instead of asking one divine mode to do all of them.

## 5. What remains unknown

The session does not determine:

- whether phenomenality is reductively physical, a fundamental subject-power, dual-aspect, or the activity of a distinct substance;
- the exact psychophysical power principle or onset threshold;
- whether the proposed non-replaceable whole-constitution relation is metaphysically coherent beyond its F2 role;
- how a distinct immaterial substance would interact with matter, should that alternative be revived;
- what secures identity across total cessation beyond the current constrained primitive.
