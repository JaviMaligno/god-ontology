**Date:** 2026-09-05

**Dependencies:** reason-guided originating sourcehood; grounding, secondary production, and ACO

## Starting position

The author's “ok ve a por ello” advances S85's stated task. Under U18, the participation-and-presence proposal is retained at its proposed level; the disjoint preference stays revisable. No accepted agency or phenomenal primitive becomes independently proven by this continuation.

**Proposed result:** originative power can serve as a substantive but non-reductive causal explanation within the preferred ontology. It identifies a source, a real power, an exercised act, its eligible terminus, and limits on causal attribution. It is neither a mechanism beneath all powers nor independent proof that the posited creative power exists. The production-versus-exercise test receives bounded conditional closure; finite phenomenal realization remains a separate task.

## Four explanatory questions

| Question | Required account | What the present model supplies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why does a bearer with this capacity exist? | causal origin and existential dependence | root origination of a generative order, followed where appropriate by real secondary production |
| What makes this bearer have this capacity? | constitutive/realization relation within the creature | organized embodied bearer and its power profile; the phenomenal bridge remains qualifiedly primitive |
| Who exercises the capacity in this episode? | actual source and manifestation | the whole creature in an autonomous episode, not a separate faculty or universal direct divine producer |
| Why this option rather than another? | contrastive explanation of settlement | the creature's actual exercise for reasons, without an antecedently sufficient selector |

An answer to the first is not a complete answer to the second or fourth. Conversely, lack of a deeper realization mechanism does not by itself erase an identified causal origin. Causal explanation, reduction, and evidential justification must be assessed separately.

## Argument for the stopping point

1. The existing ontology allows fundamental powers and reason-guided sourcehood (C48, C55–C60); explanations therefore need not always reduce an exercise to an antecedently sufficient event mechanism.
2. The necessary-agent hypothesis already bears the burden of existential origination. C230 explicitly posits a specific $P_{OEC}$, stronger than generic agency. Naming it does not derive it from omnipotence, consciousness, or the familiar fact of human action.
3. A concrete originative explanation asserts an actual exercise producing a specified eligible bearer or root order. It is stronger than “the outcome exists” or “someone could produce it”: specification, actual exercise, and causal success are distinct requirements.
4. The model has substantive constraints: independent constitutive admissibility, preserved divine identity, distinct created bearers, real secondary causation, and source-sensitive free acts. It does not permit contradictory outcomes, power-relative definitions of possibility, or a divine substitute to count as the same creature-originated act.
5. No independently justified requirement that every originative explanation reduce to a material transformation has been established in this project. An independently justified universal material-cause principle would remain a defeater; lack of that defeater is not positive proof of $P_{OEC}$.

**Conclusion:** the model supplies a non-empty causal hypothesis and a permissible stopping point under the project's explanatory standard. The preference is abductive and coherence-based. Its relational content is not yet independent evidence for the hypothesis's truth, and its compatibility conditions are not a complete proof of metaphysical possibility.

### The circular-power objection

“It produces agents because it has the power to produce agents” merely repeats the claim if possession of the power is inferred only from the very production being explained. A substantive theory needs an independently characterized source, a stable scope, an actual exercise, integration with other explanatory commitments, and possible defeaters. The current source hypothesis has prior architectural motivation, but its precise originative efficacy still remains a positive metaphysical posit. Prior motivation is not independent verification.

The strongest rival can therefore accept the distinctions while arguing that brute finite powers or an impersonal generative basis are more economical than the divine source. S47's preference for reason-guided origination is retained; this pass supplies no new demonstration against that rival. Similarly, ex deo would regain force if its constitutive bridge earned a better total explanation. Neither rival wins merely because it locates a primitive elsewhere.

## Producing a power is not exercising it for its bearer

Under C380, root creation does not become immediate divine production of every later person, capacity, or action. A generative order can include genuine secondary processes through which finite bearers and their capacities arise. The exact formation and eligibility conditions are not supplied by calling the result a power; S37's embodied account and its open thresholds remain in force.

Capacity ownership is assigned to the bearer whose organization sustains the capacity and whose exercise produces the relevant manifestation. It does not mean existential self-sufficiency, uninterrupted conscious access, independence of embodiment, or immunity from interference.

The key inference is invalid without an additional bridge:

\[
GroundsExistence(G,x)\land Originates(x,a)
\not\Rightarrow Originates(G,a).
\]

Even when indirect causal dependence is transitive in an appropriate sense, source identity and sufficient contrastive determination do not thereby transfer. This leaves God relevant to the arena and its risks; it does not erase the permission or responsibility questions by changing relation names.

Accordingly, “God grounds all created existence” cannot here mean that God alone supplies an independently sufficient explanation of every token contrast in the complete history. The latter would absorb the created settlements. The sole underived source of the arena is retained, while the actual history's particular content has the heterogeneous contributions already specified in S03. If an independently justified account of ultimate grounding required the stronger exclusive determination, this compatibility result would have to be revised rather than protected by terminology.

## Local F2 test: exactly what remains fixed

Use S06's $C_x^-$: the action-relevant prior/enabling conditions, including the embodied power, accessible reasons, the relevant past, generative specification, and divine productive or sustaining conditions relevant to the capacity, but excluding the token settlement and facts that track or respond to it.

For an episode asserted to be autonomous, the intended condition is:

\[
\exists h_a,h_b\in\mathcal H_{adm}\;[
C_x^-(h_a)=C_x^-(h_b)
\land Settles_{h_a}(x,a)
\land Settles_{h_b}(x,b)
\land a\ne b].
\]

This is a conditional diagnostic using the independently constrained history domain, not a construction proving that such histories exist. It also retains the identity assumptions already built into the prior source model; no new cross-model identity result is derived.

**Do not freeze the entire divine factual description.** If $x$ chooses differently, factive ACO must have correspondingly different true content, and an outcome-sensitive divine response may differ. Fixing $K_G(a)$ while requiring $b$ would manufacture a contradiction. Those differences do not establish that knowledge or response was an antecedently sufficient cause of $a$.

Conversely, one cannot hide an independent prior divine selector by excluding it from $C_x^-$ merely because it fixes the outcome. The exclusion criterion is explanatory dependence on the settlement, not convenience for freedom. A demonstrated productive decree independently sufficient for the exact act belongs among the determining conditions and defeats autonomy for that episode. If that decree is universal, it conflicts with the selected agency architecture.

Structural divine creation may fix that a capacity exists without fixing every exercise. At the same time, the actual complete history includes both the divine structural contribution and the creature's actual settlement. No second divine selection of a completed history is added after the creature has acted, and no temporal acquisition of knowledge in God is assumed.

## Why openness is not enough

| Candidate episode | Alternatives under the relevant conditions | Actual contribution | Result under the inherited agency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Programmed outcome | not originable in the required sense, even if several movements are physically executable | antecedently sufficient selector | fails autonomous settlement |
| Unowned lottery | multiple outcomes may remain | chance supplies variation without the subject settling for reasons | fails sourcehood |
| Creature-owned choice | multiple originable alternatives | the whole subject settles for reasons | satisfies the intended schema, conditional on the real source relation |
| Divine substitute with matching bodily result | the same external result can occur | God supplies the settlement | not the same creature-originated free act |

The third row is not a proof obtained by writing `Settles`. Its positive primitive is the subject's reason-responsive exercise. Characterizing bearer, practical content, motivational appropriation, and efficacy gives the model more content than bare indeterminism, but a rival may still challenge whether that primitive is instantiated or explanatorily adequate.

The contrastive answer is therefore: the subject settled A for these reasons. It does not add a condition already sufficient to ensure A rather than B. Under C60, demanding such a further selector would reject the chosen kind of explanation rather than reveal a missing selector within it. A competing theory may reject that explanatory standard; the project should state the disagreement instead of declaring the competitor incoherent.

## Non-circular power range

S40's conditional creative plenitude can cover a root specification containing real finite sourcehood only after independent admissibility and source-sensitive eligibility are supplied. It cannot prove finite sourcehood coherent by assuming God can create anything coherent and then inferring coherence from the desired divine capacity.

The constructive sequence is: independently assess the power-bearing profile and its source constraints; establish eligibility at the inherited conditional level; then apply the existing power bridge. Actual creation or a particular motive additionally requires exercise, not capacity alone. C113's limit on knowing God's uniquely actual creative motive remains.

## Status, residuals, and priority

**Bounded result proposed at F2:** existential dependence need not transfer token authorship; originative production and finite exercise are distinct explanatory contributions; the epistemic variation required by ACO does not manufacture prior control; and fundamental powers can be explanatory stopping points under the preferred standard. No additional divine constituent is required for that result.

**Not established:** independent proof of $P_{OEC}$, proof of the real instantiation of libertarian sourcehood, a complete finite phenomenal realization principle, exact biological onset, or the full evidential superiority of the divine hypothesis. Nonempty explanatory structure and confirmed truth are different accomplishments.

If retained under U18, stop repeating the bare “production or mere label?” fork unless an independent defeater appears. The next selective task is the finite realization burden: whether the already-open subject-level dual-aspect candidate improves on S37's qualified phenomenal-power primitive, particularly on why there is one embodied perspective and one bearer of action. This is a comparison of an acknowledged deeper candidate, not a reopening of the accepted embodied bearer merely through recency. Onset, survival, and the P-gap remain downstream unless the comparison makes them prerequisites.
