## Preferred answer for this pass

The useful comparison separates three possible effects of a candidate bodily intervention:

1. a change in the target experience;
2. a change in access or reporting shared across experiential contents;
3. a change confined to access or reporting of the target relation.

Preserved comparison accuracy cannot separate these effects by itself. Reports about other contents can test a shared report failure. They do not automatically exclude selective loss of access to the relation. This is a positive localization gain with a remaining, explicitly modeled alternative.

Reports are defeasible evidence about experience. Their fallibility does not justify eliminating first-person evidence or replacing the question about experience with a task score.

## Local F2 causal comparison

This is an idealized logical model, not an experiment or an established neural mechanism. Let:

- z=0 mean baseline and z=1 mean a candidate bodily perturbation;
- J indicate the target lived relation;
- M indicate an effective experiential access/report route shared with a control content;
- L indicate a further access/report condition specific to the target relation;
- Q indicate an affirmative usable report of the target experience;
- C indicate a successful experiential control report;
- A indicate correct task output through an independently preserved informational route.

The control content is stipulated to remain experienced in this comparison. Its successful report therefore has the rule C=M. The other rules are:

\[
Q=JML,\qquad A=1.
\]

These binary rules are deliberately idealized. Q=0 means absence of a usable affirmative report; it does not distinguish silence, uncertainty, inability to express, and an explicit denial. Actual assessment must distinguish those responses. The model contains no false-positive reports, not because they are impossible but because a counterexample in this restricted class already defeats the proposed general inference.

M is not a global motor switch. It is shared by the two experiential reports; the correctness output has its stipulated separate route. If an actual task uses the same impaired output machinery, A=1 is no longer warranted.

| Hypothesis | J | M | L | Baseline (A,Q,C) | Perturbation (A,Q,C) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H_experience: target experience changes | 1-z | 1 | 1 | (1,1,1) | (1,0,1) |
| H_shared: common experiential access/report changes | 1 | 1-z | 1 | (1,1,1) | (1,0,0) |
| H_selective: relation-specific access/report changes | 1 | 1 | 1-z | (1,1,1) | (1,0,1) |

### Derived result

With only A and Q, all three hypotheses have the same predictions. Adding C distinguishes H_shared from the other two under the stated rules. H_experience and H_selective remain equivalent on these observations.

Consequently:

- preserved accuracy plus changed target reports alone does not locate the change in experience;
- a preserved control report can reject this particular shared-failure model;
- that success does not also reject a selective access/report explanation.

This is a counterexample to an inference from these observations under this weak specification. It does not establish metaphysically possible phenomenal duplicates, actual unconscious cognition, or universal empirical indistinguishability.

### What would improve the comparison

A candidate account must specify the bodily organization it treats as relevant, how an intervention would affect it, and which other pathways remain available. Assessment can then vary report demands, test access to the individual contents, distinguish delayed memory from present experience, and use several appropriately calibrated indicators.

An additional indicator N would distinguish H_experience and H_selective only if there are independently warranted reasons for different predictions about N. Merely writing N=J builds the desired result into the measurement rule. An indicator can also be affected directly by the bodily perturbation or share the selective-access pathway. More measurements are useful insofar as their dependencies constrain the alternatives.

A candidate supported by such convergence can become the better explanation without deductively excluding every imaginable rival. Existing preference is not suspended while that work remains incomplete.
