## Cosmological support and its limits

The scientific component is an integrated preliminary audit, not a new main research branch. A hot early expanding universe, a boundary of a spacetime description, an absolute first physical reality, and an ultimate explanatory ground are different claims. A temporal beginning alone does not settle the last three steps.

## Love, worship, and refusal

C124 and C723–C726 support the author's core: a loving agent can offer relationship and care without installing the other subject's reciprocation. Worship does not repair a divine deficiency. Freedom to decline, lack of divine need, and absence of any moral reason to worship are nevertheless different propositions; no blanket conclusion about the last is adopted.

The proposed interpretation of hell is privation of accepted communion. C208 already requires that a continuing subject remain grounded by, known to, and within the standing availability of God. Total absence of every good or of divine grounding would not describe that subject's continuing life. The evil is a privation in an existing bearer, not a second substance to warehouse.

Four qualifications matter:

1. Nonbelief, uncertainty, rejection of a religious representation, and informed refusal of a known relationship are not interchangeable. A truth-directed believer cannot simply be assumed to choose every belief at will.
2. Rejecting relationship at one time does not entail rejecting it at every later time. A finite-life refusal is not automatically an irrevocable eternal choice. Continued voluntary refusal, an irreversible character, and a divinely closed opportunity are distinct hypotheses; the last two require additional premises.
3. Respecting refusal need not mean ceasing to care, clarify, invite, or heal. Under the preferred model these can support agency without replacing consent.
4. Protecting others from harm is distinct from forcing love. “Confinement of evil” needs a separate account of what is constrained, why, and with what proportionate effects. Removing torture language alone does not settle whether the remaining alternatives are coercive.

The author's voluntary-communion reading is recorded as a theological preference. Existence, duration, finality, and conditions of a hell-like state remain open under C209. Endless unrelieved suffering, even if described as self-exclusion, must still be tested against the believer's undefeated-goodness commitment; free will does not discharge that burden by name.

## Personal continuation: a more precise result

### The bearer, its identity, and its realization

The survival question concerns the numerically same subject having further conscious life. Identity is not a detachable immaterial item that can survive instead of that subject (S82). Memory in God is knowledge of the person, not the person's further experience. A qualitatively perfect replica is a different possible result.

The existing economical preference is an admissible re-realization of the same embodied subject, possibly after a gap and possibly with a new realization base. This does not assert an intrinsically immortal disembodied soul; uninterrupted survival of a subject is a live alternative with its own bridge burden. Bodily realization is not thereby ruled out for a further life.

### Bounded F2 non-entailment test

Let a complete history contain subject x on a bounded life-set ending at bodily death. Let x's identity be fundamental, God know that whole life, and every actual stage be grounded. This assignment is compatible with the identity and E+/ACO commitments alone. None of those premises requires another conscious episode of x after death.

A second candidate history may contain a further conscious phase of the same x. It must satisfy the independently admitted cross-gap identity profile: the same token origin structure, kind constraints, non-branching identity, and one career. It also needs powers and a realization capable of further experience, and an actual grounding or re-realizing act. Calling a later phase “x's” records the identity claim; it does not prove it.

Therefore fundamental identity and complete knowledge neither entail immortality nor establish that a particular reconstruction is the original. This preserves C448's preferred bounded possibility while applying C447 and S80's explicit anti-circularity limit. A duplicate with matching memories remains a counterexample to qualitative sufficiency; two distinct successors cannot both be x by transitivity of identity.

### What kind of life and what further premise?

Continuation, its duration, its embodiment, experienced welfare, and communion are separate axes. A subject can continue without accepting communion. A post-mortem arena need not share our spatial coordinates; if it includes learning and reconsideration, it must provide an order of experiences. Atemporal knowledge of that order does not make the subject timeless.

The same-person requirement remains indispensable for the project's bearer-indexed hope: benefits to a replica do not repair the original's life. The further reason to expect continuation remains the author's belief in divine goodness and the preferred standing concern for each subject, with C485/C721's conditional status. Capacity does not entail exercise. Mere survival without a fitting horizon does not resolve the P-gap.

The remaining priority is concrete: can the whole-career origin/form account ground a later phase of this very subject without presupposing the identity to be explained? Compare that with a continuously persisting bearer, making each additional ontological commitment explicit. This is deeper work on the existing preferred possibility, not a claim that a complete survival theory has now been proved.
