## Starting position

The author explicitly authorizes the comparison left pending in S100–S101. Under U18 those sessions remain at their proposed statuses. The working preference is renewed realization of the same subject, permitting a genuine existence gap without requiring a hidden survivor (C448–C450). That is a preferred possibility, not evidence that survival occurs or a claim that a gap is necessary. Uninterrupted subject persistence remains a live alternative.

S100's demand for a deeper bridge must not silently reverse C436 by requiring a reduction of primitive identity to memories, matter, or another entity. There are two legitimate demands: state the fundamental identity fact transparently, and justify which careers a bearer of this kind can have. The latter remains substantive even when identity is primitive. This pass makes that burden precise rather than pretending to derive an identity criterion.

## Definitions and three independent losses

For a bearer x and a local temporal ordering T, write E(x,t) for existence, C(x,t) for an occurrent conscious episode, and B(x,t) for realization through the present bodily base. The interval notation below is diagnostic; it does not impose earthly clock time on a post-mortem domain.

- C(x,t) entails E(x,t): an episode has a bearer.
- E(x,t) need not entail C(x,t), under C315.
- Whether E(x,t) can hold after loss of B(x,t) is the disputed realization bridge, not a consequence of the first two relations.

Death of the present biological realization must not be defined as annihilation of the whole subject when that is the question under comparison. Conversely, bodily death cannot be declared harmless to the subject just because conscious interruptions are already allowed. The ordinary non-conscious persistence model does not establish survival through destruction of its sustaining organization.

Renewed conscious realization and uninterrupted bearer existence are therefore not mutually exclusive. A bearer might persist without episodes and subsequently regain them. A genuinely gappy career makes the stronger claim that E itself fails in an interval and holds later for that very individual.

## The uninterrupted route

The strongest version preserves one finite subject with a real unity and power profile throughout the transition. Its experiences may stop while its capacities remain unmanifested. Its realization might be preserved, transformed, or replaced through an admissible continuous career; no separable soul is entailed by the definition of this route.

The route needs a substantive extension of the ordinary persistence profile:

1. the individual's finite unity and kind-essential features persist through the relevant transition;
2. some admissible constitution or realization supports its existence during the interval, even if no episode occurs;
3. later experiences are exercises of that continuing individual's powers;
4. the transition does not replace the subject, branch its numerical identity, or merge it into G.

These conditions organize the hypothesis; they are not a proof that the transition is possible. In particular, identifying the actual subject as one embodied organism does not yet license moving it to an arbitrary substrate or making it disembodied. The account must distinguish ordinary bodily realization from what is essential to this bearer.

An independently subsistent subject is one possible answer, with individuation, embodiment, and causal-role burdens. A changed but continuing realization is another. Neither follows from the bare fact that identity is fundamental. Continuing divine knowledge, a stored description, or a relation to an earlier life is not itself an existing finite subject. Calling such a trace an identity-core does not supply the missing bearer.

Its genuine comparative benefit is that no cross-nonexistence identity extension is required if a constitutively adequate bearer really persists. Its cost is explaining the persistence itself after the current realization ends. It is not free just because its label says continuity.

## The genuinely gappy route

Let J1 and J2 be separated nonempty intervals and let x have life-set J1 union J2. There is no x in the intervening interval. No disembodied waiting, experiences, or hidden carrier is included. The claim is about one complete career, not something crossing a period in which it does not exist.

The required additional modal premise is:

> At least some bearer of this kind admits a numerically single, origin- and essence-compatible, non-branching career with disconnected existence intervals.

This is the content already preferred abductively in C448. It is stronger than primitive identity alone and must not be advertised as a theorem of E+. C446's gap profile includes the identity fact; it constrains a proposed witness but cannot prove that witness admissible merely by naming it.

The primitive is a fact about the bearer, not a further object surviving in its absence. No reductive identity-maker is owed once C436 is selected. But the modal extension across nonexistence is still a metaphysical commitment whose costs and potential defeaters must be assessed. The absence of a known connectedness requirement supports the existing preference within the wider model; it does not demonstratively establish possibility.

On the selected conditional account, G can actualize an independently admissible later phase of x. This is a task directed at an already actual individual, not a duplicate later renamed x. The explanatory order remains admissible task, qualified capacity, exercise, result. Neither the intention to restore x nor recognition of x supplies admissibility. At the current depth the positive link is primitive same-bearer career membership constrained by the profile, not a newly discovered causal mechanism.

## Adversarial tests

### One duplicate is enough to expose qualitative insufficiency

Consider three schematic histories sharing x's earlier life and a later subject's entire qualitative profile:

- Terminal-plus-copy: x ends and a distinct y later has that profile.
- Gappy-x: x exists again with that profile, under the gap-admissibility premise.
- Continuous-x: x persists through an interval without episodes and later has that profile, under the continuity-extension premise.

They differ in bearer assignment or interval existence despite matching the stipulated later memories. These are diagnostic interpretations, not independent demonstrations that all three are metaphysically possible. In the first case there is only one successor. Thus uniqueness of the successor is not sufficient for survival. Nor is causal dependence of the reconstruction on x's records sufficient: the copy case can include that dependence.

### Branching and symmetry

If y and z are distinct, x=y and x=z would imply y=z, a contradiction. Non-branching is necessary but does not select y merely because z is absent. A separately grounded identity fact may distinguish an original from a copy despite qualitative symmetry; if no such difference is supplied, left/right labels or divine declaration do not do the work. The project retains S45's distinction between qualitative resemblance and independently grounded career membership.

### The divine-ground shortcut

Under the preferred grounding account many distinct subjects depend on the same G. Therefore common dependence cannot entail that two subjects are identical. A divine representation of x can also obtain in the terminal model. A constitutive relation specifically to x's renewed career presupposes its independent admissibility; it is not a shortcut around that condition. The finite-form/origin profile of S82 remains the dependency on which deeper explanation turns.

### Bare identity and kind restrictions

A label with no bearer cannot be the survivor. A persisting subject with unmanifested powers is a stronger, substantive possibility, not an empty label; its supporting constitution must be stated. Neither route may exempt itself from the independently established essence of the embodied subject. If uninterrupted organic life proved essential, both the genuine gap and a disembodied continuation would face a defeater. No such proof or rejection is supplied here.

### Numerical versus biographical continuation

Same-bearer identity can survive loss of memories on the selected account. It does not follow that memory, acquired dispositions, relationships, and authorship are dispensable to a fitting continuation. Restoring or developing those goods is a further welfare and agency task; it must not overwrite the person's will or turn a changed individual into a different bearer by definition. Numerical continuation alone does not discharge C721's bearer-level horizon.

## Comparative assessment and positive result

| Route | What it preserves or adds | Main explanatory gain | Remaining cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Uninterrupted bearer, possibly interrupted experience | A finite subject and admissible unity/powers through every intermediate stage | Avoids existence-gap identity | Explain support and kind-compatible transition beyond the present realization |
| Genuine gap with renewed realization | Primitive de re identity over disconnected intervals plus adequate later realization | Adds no intermediate survivor | Justify disconnected careers for this bearer kind; no reductive mechanism derived |
| Mere reconstruction of a matching successor | Profile, memories, possibly causal dependence and uniqueness | Can preserve information and projects | Fails same-bearer continuation unless an independent identity premise is supplied |

The existing preference for renewed realization without an obligatory hidden survivor is retained. Economy concerns the extra carrier, not an automatic claim that a discontinuous career has lower total metaphysical cost. Continuous realization change could also avoid an extra substance; this pass does not establish a global winner over that alternative. A genuine gap remains a permitted preferred possibility rather than a required feature of afterlife.

The concrete advance is an explicit allocation of the two bridge burdens and exclusion of several false solutions: uninterrupted consciousness is unnecessary, a lone copy is insufficient, shared divine ground is insufficient, and no detachable identity-object explains persistence. Primitive identity is an acknowledged stopping point; kind-sensitive modal admissibility and actual realization are the remaining work. This is more than renaming the gap, but less than a complete survival theory.

Nothing in the comparison entails actual continuation, its universality, infinite duration, or consent to communion. The reason for the author's hope remains the previously accepted theological commitment and the defeasible bearer-directed reasons, with C721's stronger closure as faith content. No new divine intention is inferred from qualified power.
