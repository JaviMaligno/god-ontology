## Positive proposal: one concrete power-bearing unity

Take C338's subject-level dual-aspect candidate as the explanatory hypothesis for this local pass, explicitly proposed rather than treating its open psychophysical bridge as settled. The subject is the concrete unified bearer with bodily organization and intrinsic phenomenal powers. It is not a bare owner added behind its body, capacities, and experiences. Actual bodily processes and lived experience can be dimensions of that subject's own exercise; this is not a claim that any physical description logically entails phenomenality or libertarian authorship.

Relations among the items are now specified:

| Item | Relation to the person | Explanatory job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bodily constituents and processes | Constituent/process relations within the actual embodied bearer | Realize its life, interaction, and operative powers; a constituent is not automatically another whole subject |
| Form or organization | Actual ordering and integration of those constituents and powers | Explains their coordinated operation as a living unity; does not alone derive a first-person field |
| Conscious and agentive capacities | Intrinsic powers of the organized concrete subject | Their bearer dependence differs from spatial parthood; their possession need not be current exercise |
| Experience and freely originated action | Temporal states/processes or exercises of those powers | Experienced and performed by the subject, not by an additional inner observer or by G in its place |
| Memory and biographical dispositions | States of the subject and effects of its history | Shape subsequent exercises without being an inventory sufficient for numerical identity |
| G under principal ANDC | Underived constitutive ground of the distinct finite bearer | Supplies ontological dependence without spatial partition or ownership of the creature's contrastive acts |

The proposal does not equate the whole body with an organ or identify every bodily process with an episode of consciousness. Nor does it infer one subject wherever bodily causal integration is described. The local bridge posits a concrete unity whose powers have both an embodied realization and an intrinsic experiential character; the macro-subject boundary remains to be justified.

This is stronger than a mere list of roles because it places the powers and episodes intrinsically in one concrete being and specifies how their dependence differs. A power is not another agent sending instructions into a body. An experience need not be transmitted to an additional owner: it is already an episode of the bearer. Form explains coordinated organization; primitive phenomenal ownership supplies what structural coordination alone has not derived. This is an explicit theoretical stopping point, not the assertion that a new term has solved phenomenality.

## Individuation through real origination, not epistemic labeling

Within the preferred creation account, the positive ground of individuality is actual origination/constitution of a finite concrete unity. The explanatory contribution of G is not exhausted by observing an independently existing person. The creature's real organization and source-bearing unity are actualized in a concrete origin history, ordinarily with secondary causes. Creation need not be a separate direct intervention for each person or an event at a privileged first instant.

There are three orders to keep clear:

1. **Actualization:** a concrete finite bearer and its powers are made actual within an admissible order. This individuates by producing reality, not by naming a template.
2. **Creaturely life:** that bearer undergoes experiences and contributes its own acts; an actual history is not an antecedent divine script.
3. **Acquaintance:** G knows the actual subject and its own perspective. Factive knowledge entails its actual referent, but its semantic factivity is not the productive explanation of the referent.

These are explanatory roles, not temporal learning in G. No pre-actual human individual is selected from a warehouse of identities. One creative act can ground many distinct subjects; numerical identity is not reduced to “same divine act,” a date, or a naming relation.

The proposed answer to “why this subject rather than a matching other?” is that there is this actual power-bearing unity and its concrete origination, rather than merely the repeatable description both could satisfy. Two instantiations can reproduce the description while supplying two different centers of experience and sourcehood. Their ultimate token difference is a fact of concrete plurality and intrinsic ownership; it is not yet derived from a wholly qualitative base. This respects C436 rather than silently pretending to overcome it.

The view therefore offers a constitutive explanation of what is individuated and where its reality comes from, with primitive token unity explicitly exposed. It does not offer a complete reductive definition of identity across arbitrary gaps. Re-realizing the same individual needs the existing independent admissibility premise, not just a new act of creation.

## Divine participation: stronger than resemblance, less than identity collapse

The live commitments support differentiated claims:

- **Divine origin:** under the creator hypothesis, finite conscious capacities derive ultimately from G, even where secondary bodily processes realize them.
- **Finite resemblance:** conscious reason-guided agency is a genuine but limited analogue of divine sourcehood, the already preferred C105 rationale. This is not proof that it was G's sole actual motive (C113).
- **Constitutive participation:** C660 prefers that the undivided G really constitutes distinct finite beings without partition, depletion, or reversed dependence. On this hypothesis “divine in us” may name a real constitutive source, not merely a metaphorical resemblance. The exact relation remains C686's burden.
- **Identity of bearers or powers:** neither origin nor constitution licenses saying the human subject is G, human powers are numerically divine powers, or human acts are G's acts.

The underlying argument is conditional: if x is genuinely distinct from G and x owns its conscious powers and free acts, sharing a constitutive ground cannot erase their bearer indices without contradicting the preferred premises. Constitution also does not transmit every property of the constituent to the constituted being; such transmission would need a further bridge. Hence no inference from G's necessity or eternity to x's necessity or immortality.

Constitutive dependence concerns the whole person, not a special divine organ inside an otherwise independent body. Conscious agency is a distinctive finite resemblance, not the only part of the creature allowed to depend on G. A merely disjoint creator model could retain divine origin and resemblance while rejecting constitutive participation; this fallback shows why the strict claim must not be smuggled into the word “reflects.” C660's preference remains asymmetric, while C686 is not declared solved.

## Local tests and material limits

1. **Remove the supposed spectator:** episodes are already of the concrete bearer, so no additional inner subject is needed to receive them. This blocks an explanatory regress under the selected intrinsic-power model; it does not derive consciousness from organization.
2. **Copy organization and memory:** two distinct concrete unities can match a description. Neither common design nor common G makes them one. A copied capacity is a capacity of the copy, not a transferred owner.
3. **Couple two subjects causally:** interaction and coordination alone do not entail one shared first-person bearer. The intrinsic-unity clause is substantive; no causal-integration metric is claimed sufficient.
4. **Identify creaturely choice with divine choice:** source separation fails. To create a finite agent rather than merely an instrument, its relevant contrastive settlements must remain its own.
5. **Try to derive survival from constitutive intimacy:** terminal-life and gappy-life profiles are still distinguishable. G's eternity and acquaintance do not decide which life-set belongs to the finite bearer.
6. **Replace “constitution” with “causation”:** much of the subject model still works in the disjoint fallback. The precise extra content of whole constitution remains open; this pass does not count its explanatory synthesis as discharge of C686.

The advance is a positive local hypothesis of intrinsic powers and episodes of one concrete unity, tied to real origination rather than recognition, plus a correct account of the current divine-participation preference. The unresolved point is now explicit: why a particular realized organization has one intrinsic experiential unity, and what preserves that unity across radical change. The next test should target that psychophysical/constitutive relation rather than produce another general vocabulary list.
