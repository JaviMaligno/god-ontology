## Constructive proposal: finite reflective authorship

The shared creative explanation concerns actual individuals with finite powers, not an empty bearer plus a detachable description. “Substance” can mean a concrete individual or an ultimate constitutive base; commonality in the second sense would not make the first identical. “Form” is real power-conferring organization, not another parcel of stuff or a mathematical description that produces a subject by itself.

Apply that common account to a demanding kind of bearer. The proposed profile joins:
- lived presentation: something is experienced by this individual;
- understanding: considerations can be apprehended as reasons;
- reflective evaluation: the subject can examine beliefs, desires, standards and ends;
- practical uptake: what is understood can contribute to action;
- formation over time and with others: learning, commitments and reciprocal reason-giving can shape a life.

These are linked explanatory roles, not five substances, a mandatory sequence in every act, or an empirical claim that they always covary. A basic contrast is choosing an effective means to an end versus also being able to ask whether the end deserves pursuit. This need not require choosing one's original nature or all inherited standards. Reflection can be mistaken, constrained or absent from a particular episode; rational influence and care can help it develop.

The candidate difference is thus in finite organization and capacities. It is real if instantiated even when the constituents or origin are common. It need not require a second immaterial substance or a new ontological ingredient, but the positive realization of phenomenal and rational efficacy is still owed. This specifies what must be explained and relates it to the creation model; it is not the missing mechanism itself.

P71's human example is a first-person conceptual starting point: desiring something while questioning whether to pursue it, and revising a belief in light of recognized error. It is not a comparative survey. Other creatures realizing the profile would count; differences of extent, organization and kind are not settled here. Neither current exercise nor intellectual achievement is a definition of personal worth. Existing P73 and P77 remain unchanged at their scoped statuses.

## Bounded inference checks

1. **Common base cannot be the sufficient discriminator.** If all concrete creatures satisfy B(x)=G, then this formula alone selects neither humans nor conscious bearers. Finite differentiation requires an additional warranted profile/realization premise. No equal-divinity or human-only constitution is inferred.
2. **Description is not actuality.** Stating the same finite profile twice does not establish two realized subjects, and describing a subject does not produce it. The instantiated bearer and its powers remain the creative explanandum in either candidate.
3. **Reflective profile is not libertarian proof.** A local vocabulary can describe appraisal and behavior while leaving open whether appraisal is causally efficacious and whether antecedents independently settle the outcome. The full sourcehood premise cannot be obtained by relabeling sophistication.
4. **Capacity is not mission.** Retain C914's distinctions among capacity, valued good, directed vocation and individual obligation. A candidate purpose fitting the capacities still needs evidence of actual divine intention. Purpose may be plural and relational rather than a uniform assignment.
5. **Generic constitution is not incarnation.** B(x)=G leaves x distinct from G in the shared-base proposal. The inspected incarnation comparator asserts that the human life has a divine personal subject. Equal base-values alone cannot establish that relation. This is an inference limit, not a disproof of either model.
6. **A fitting human profile does not uniquely select humanity.** If another possible kind realized the same relevant profile, a reason citing only that profile would apply to it too. A specifically human history or addressee relation could distinguish the choice, but must be supported independently rather than invented as God's actual motive.
