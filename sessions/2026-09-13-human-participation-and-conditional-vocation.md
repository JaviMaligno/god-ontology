## Positive proposal

Within the preferred creation model, all creatures share dependence on God. Finite analogies concern specified instantiated capacities; common origin alone entails neither uniform properties nor a divine material constituent. Reflexive participation adds a role: understanding reality, examining reasons, communicating understanding, and orienting action through it.

This is a capacity profile, not an empirical species essence or a single scalar measuring creaturely worth. Reflective cognition and the project's libertarian sourcehood have different criteria. A capacity can depend on embodiment, learning, language and social support without being an independent collective subject. The developmental and communal dimensions specify conditions of exercise, not a new species mind or a reduction of personhood to group membership.

The proposed distinctive human role is therefore a shared practice of understanding and responsible response. The targeted studies below constrain the comparison but establish neither universal human exclusivity nor a mission. Independent traits may be shared while their organization differs; neither a sharp metaphysical break nor a wholly gradual account is derived.

## Internal constructive argument

1. C105–C106/C523 prefer real finite others with their own sourcehood; C121–C124 allow personal relation with actual creaturely participation.
2. Knowing that another could understand, and knowing a truth oneself, do not instantiate that other's understanding. C112/C125–C126 preserve atemporal divine knowledge while differentiating finite realizations.
3. Understanding can enable the truth-directed relation and reason-sensitive response already valued under C187–C188.
4. Thus finite understanding and participation supply an intelligible candidate creative good within this architecture.
5. If a mission is assigned to such participants, this good is a fitting proposed organizing content, with different ways of contributing.

Steps 1 and 3 import the project's preferred, revisable sourcehood/relation and value premises. Step 4 is a coherence-based integration. Step 5 is a proposed conditional interpretation, not a deduction of mission existence or uniqueness. Understanding can be misused; neither all information gain nor maximal knowledge is thereby required.

The reason is stronger than mere resemblance: the finite act realizes an actual good for or among distinct participants. It remains weaker than evidence that God assigns those participants a mission. A rival may value the existence, freedom and relations of subjects without assigning this particular vocation.

## Bounded F2: capacity, purpose, mission, and obligation

Use the following local predicates:

- `Cap(x,k)`: x has the relevant capacity k, whether currently exercised or not.
- `Values_G(g)`: g occurs among the creative reasons as a valued good.
- `V_G(C,g)`: God intends g as an end for community C and its participation, rather than merely as an available or incidental benefit. This is the stipulated strong vocation claim.
- `O(x,a,c)`: in circumstances c, x has a binding obligation to perform action a.

These are different relations. Neither `Cap` nor `Values` contains the directed intention in `V`; `V` contains no complete individual-duty allocation.

Consider two finite interpretations of the same local base. Each contains G, a community C with agents x and y, a capacity k, and a possible good g. In both, G grounds x/y, both have k, and G values actual otherness, sourcehood and truthful relation. In M0, `V_G(C,g)` is false; in M1 it is true. The inherited base has no axiom selecting one. This witnesses non-entailment in the stated signature, not a proof that both complete world histories satisfy every unspecified metaphysical law.

Within M1, let a be performing an original scientific discovery at a given moment. The community's end does not imply that every member has `O(x,a,c)`. Different contributions, inability, competing reasons and receiving care remain possible. A further normative/allocation premise is required to generate particular obligations. Mere creation by an agent also does not, without such a bridge, establish that agent's unrestricted moral authority.

The weak use of “vocation” for a fitting activity is easier to support conditionally on the preferred reasons. It must not silently replace the stronger claim that an actual mission is assigned. S125's existence question remains substantive under the stronger reading.

## Bearer value and shared participation

C144 and C187–C188 already give conscious welfare and truthful relation standing alongside sourcehood. Consequently, failure to produce a discovery does not remove every ground of concern for a bearer. C438–C442 also separate an individual's persistence from current cognitive performance.

A community-level vocation can be realized through varied contributions. But contribution is not an admission condition for concern: a person can be a recipient of care without becoming an instrument for someone else's achievements. This does not assert that every individual has unrealized scientific potential.

The outcome is an inference limit plus a proposed inclusive interpretation of the shared role. It is not a full proof of equal moral status in all possible cases or a settlement of C320's personhood boundary. Avoid deriving a complete moral hierarchy from cognitive profiles.

## Bounded F2: divine knowledge and finite understanding

Let `K_G(p)` denote divine knowledge of p and `K_x(p)` finite understanding/knowledge of p. Factivity gives `K_G(K_x(p)) -> K_x(p)`, but `K_G(p)` alone does not entail `K_x(p)`. Confusing the two would erase the bearer-indexed fact the proposal values.

ACO knows the actual finite discovery in its place in the complete history; this introduces no temporal learning by God and no truth about a merely possible libertarian outcome. The additional actuality is a creature's understanding and possible response. Its value depends on the preferred otherness/sourcehood/relation interface, rather than on informational novelty for God. This supplies P78's answer without establishing `V_G`.

### Templer and Hampton

**Exact source:** Victoria L. Templer and Robert R. Hampton, “Rhesus monkeys (Macaca mulatta) show robust evidence for memory awareness across multiple generalization tests,” *Animal Cognition* 15 (2012), 409–419; DOI 10.1007/s10071-011-0468-4. Inspected general methods, Experiments 1–4, and general discussion, including alternative stimulus-control and response-competition accounts, in the [author's copy](https://hamptonlab.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2011animcog-memoryawarenesstemplerhampton.pdf).

**Argument and project assessment:** Six trained male macaques could choose a smaller certain reward instead of a memory test. If novelty alone caused rejection, unusual memory-strengthening trials should also increase rejection. Double presentation improved accuracy and reduced rejection, whereas omitted samples and longer delays increased it. The authors favor memory monitoring. The project retains bounded functional evidence relevant to P73; it does not infer libertarian agency or human-like phenomenology. The study leaves behavioral-cue and response-competition explanations incompletely excluded. Experimental memory manipulation and reward interpretation are imported at the task level; species universality, moral rank and divine purpose are not.

### Dean and colleagues

**Exact source:** L. G. Dean, R. L. Kendal, S. J. Schapiro, B. Thierry and K. N. Laland, “Identification of the Social and Cognitive Processes Underlying Human Cumulative Culture,” *Science* 335 (2012), 1114–1118; DOI 10.1126/science.1213969. Inspected design, main results and discussion, plus relevant supplementary methods, in the [article and supplement](https://faculty.washington.edu/ccab/Dean%20etal%20-%20cognitive%20basis%20of%20culture%20-%20Science%202012.pdf).

**Argument and project assessment:** A sequential three-stage puzzlebox made solution advancement and social behavior comparable within a specified design. Children's advanced success covaried with teaching, imitation and social support, unlike the tested nonhuman groups. The authors infer a role for that sociocognitive package. The project retains a scoped difference supporting attention to organized shared capacities (P72–P73), not a universal necessary condition or an inherent ceiling on other species. Samples, enculturation, task suitability and unequal exposure/conditions limit transfer; covariation is not by itself causal proof. Their broad 2012 exclusivity claims are not adopted as a current finding or as evidence of divine assignment.

### Catholic doctrinal comparator

**Exact source:** [*Catechism of the Catholic Church*, §§456–475](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_one/section_two/chapter_two/artcile_3.html), especially §§457–460 and 464–475. ## Incarnation: exact compatibility audit

The source's claim of one divine personal subject with full humanity is stronger than communication between a created human subject and G. It also does not entail that human cognitive superiority explains the selection or that no other creature could have a comparable vocation.

Three questions must remain separate:

1. **Purpose fit:** a human life can realize interpersonal understanding and response; the inspected theological comparator adds reconciliation/communion. This is an intelligible conditional fit.
2. **Distinct natures or constituent bases:** no mixture or transfer of divine material is required by the source's two-nature description.
3. **One personal bearer:** bodily and mental human activities must be attributable to the divine personal subject. Merely naming a special relation cannot discharge this requirement.

C248's formal CCD condition concerns shared constituents and mutual mode relations. It contains no independently analyzed personal-assumption predicate. In a minimal relational signature one can stipulate disjoint divine/human constituent sets, false mutual `ModeOf` relations, and a common-bearer relation `SubjectOf(p,h)`. This witnesses only that the bare CCD formula does not decide that new predicate. It is not a metaphysical realization model: the new relation and its causal work have merely been stipulated.

Conversely, add the unrestricted bridge `HumanLife(h) -> its personal subject is a created individual distinct from every divine subject`. A divine personal subject for that same life then contradicts the bridge. Whether the ordinary organismic account C438–C442 admits such a universal extension is exactly what must be assessed; it is not silently restricted or overturned here.

The next positive model would have to justify the bearer relation, retain complete human powers, avoid two unrelated personal agents, and integrate temporal human knowledge/acts with divine knowledge and the agency account. The source distinguishes divine and human intellect/will; typed predicates can locate apparent conflicts, but labels alone do not resolve them. The project's G is not silently identified with a fully analyzed Trinitarian person, and no Trinity model is derived in this pass.

Thus C825's preferred creation model is retained. Full incarnation compatibility is neither established nor declared impossible. A material revision, if needed, must be proposed with its exact scope and downstream cost.

## What could support a mission's existence?

The required target is the directed intention in `V_G(C,g)`. Candidate support could be an independently defended creative-purpose argument or evidence of communication whose divine attribution and intended content survive alternative explanations and defeaters. These are research routes, not conclusions or guarantees. Personal resonance, beneficial effects or a capacity's mere presence do not suffice to establish source or assignment.

The comparative burden is to improve on a creator who values finite others and their capacities without assigning this specific mission. A mere explanation of how cognition develops neither settles nor automatically defeats that further purpose question. Do not import an evolutionary unreliability argument, an absence-of-scientific-explanation argument, perfect divine goodness, or a maximizing knowledge duty.
