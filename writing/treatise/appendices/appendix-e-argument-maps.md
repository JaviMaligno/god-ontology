## E.1 From contingency to a necessary agent (Part III)

```
Datum: the physical order is contingent (constants, laws, existence not derived)      [C01–C03]
Preference: weak explanatory preference, defeasible, not a PSR                         [C457]
  └─ Necessitation lemma: no necessary being necessitates a contingent fact             [C458]  (deductive, normal modal logic)
       └─ Four stopping points partition the options                                    [C459]  (classification)
            ├─ brute contingency: coherent, lowest ranked under WEP
            ├─ necessitarianism: coherent, excluded as working option                    [C461]
            ├─ impersonal necessity + chance / selection law / plenitude: contrast unexplained or ontology inflated
            └─ necessary agent with non-necessitating settling: contrastive, reason-guided [C460]  (abductive; preferred)
Withdrawal conditions: incoherence of necessary agency; emptiness of reason-guided settling;
  an impersonal model grounding contingency and created agency at lower cost              [S09]
```
